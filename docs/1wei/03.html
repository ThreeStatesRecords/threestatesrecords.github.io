3  Annals of Emperor Míng

Tàihé (227 - 232)

Míng Huángdì forbidden name Ruì, appellation Yuánzhòng, was Wén-dì’s [Cáo Pī] Heir-Apparent. When he was born Tàizǔ [Cáo Cāo] favored him, always having him among his Left and Right. [1] Aged fifteen years, enfeoffed as Marquess of Wǔdé. Huángchū Second Year, became Duke of Qí, Third Year, became King of Píngyuán. As his mother was executed, therefore he was not yet established as the successor. [2] Seventh Year, Summer, Fifth Moon [226 Jun 13 – Jul 11], the Emperor was critically ill, and so enthroned him as Imperial Heir-Apparent. Dīngsì [226 Jun 29], succeeded the Imperial Emperor’s throne, Great Amnesty. Honored the Imperial Dowager Empress as Grand Imperial Dowager Empress, Imperial Empress as Imperial Dowager Empress. The various ministers were enfeoffed with noble ranks each on distinct basis. [3] Guǐwèi [226 Jul 25], posthumously titled his mother the consort Zhēn as Wén-Zhāo Huánghòu. Rénchén [226 Aug 3], enthroned the Imperial younger brother [Cáo] Ruí as King of Yángpíng.

明皇帝諱叡，字元仲，文帝太子也。生而太祖愛之，常令在左右。〔一〕年十五，封武德侯，黃初二年為齊公，三年為平原王。以其母誅，故未建為嗣。〔二〕七年夏五月，帝病篤，乃立為皇太子。丁巳，即皇帝位，大赦。尊皇太后曰太皇太后，皇后曰皇太后。諸臣封爵各有差。〔三〕癸未，追諡母甄夫人曰文昭皇后。壬辰，立皇弟蕤為陽平王。

[1] Wèi shū states: The Emperor was several years old and had appearance of precocious intelligence, Wǔ Huángdì [Cáo Cāo] was impressed with him, and said: “Our foundation due to you is three generations.” At every Court and feast gathering, he was with the Attendant Centrals and close ministers together lined within the curtains. He enjoyed study and remembered much, and especially devoted attention on legal principles.

〔一〕　魏書曰：帝生數歲而有岐嶷之姿，武皇帝異之，曰：「我基於爾三世矣。」每朝宴會同，與侍中近臣並列帷幄。好學多識，特留意於法理。

[2] Wèi lüè states: Emperor Wén, because the empress Guō had no children, had Edict for her adopt and raise the Emperor. The Emperor because his mother had not ended well was extremely indignant in thought. Later he could not obtain his wishes, and so respectfully served the empress Guō, morning and evening using the Chief Managers to ask after her rising and resting. The empress Guō also because she had no children therefore applied parental affection on him. Emperor Wén originally, because the Emperor had been displeased, had intention and wish for another concubine’s son the King of Jīngzhào [Cáo Lǐ] to become successor, and therefore for a long time did not appoint an Heir-Apparent.

Wèi Mò zhuàn states: The Emperor once accompanied Emperor Wén in a hunt, seeing a child and mother deer. Emperor Wén shot and killed the mother deer, and ordered the Emperor shoot the child deer. The Emperor did not obey, saying: “Your Majesty has already killed its mother, Your Servant cannot bear to further kill its child.” Therefore he shed tears. Emperor Wén at once dropped his bow and arrows, because of this was deeply surprised of him, and so his intentions on the establishment were settled.

〔二〕　魏略曰：文帝以郭后無子，詔使子養帝。帝以母不以道終，意甚不平。後不獲已，乃敬事郭后，旦夕因長御問起居，郭后亦自以無子，遂加慈愛。文帝始以帝不悅，有意欲以他姬子京兆王為嗣，故久不拜太子。魏末傳曰：帝常從文帝獵，見子母鹿。文帝射殺鹿母，使帝射鹿子，帝不從，曰：「陛下已殺其母，臣不忍復殺其子。」因涕泣。文帝即放弓箭，以此深奇之，而樹立之意定。

[3] Shìyǔ states: The Emperor and the Court servicemen had previously not interacted. After he ascended the throne, the various subordinates wanted to know of his mannerisms. After a few days, he met alone with Attendant Central Liú Yè, conversing all day. Everyone leaned in to listen. When [Liú] Yè exited, they asked: “What is he like?” [Liú] Yè said: “A peer of Qín Shǐhuáng and Hàn Xiào-Wǔ, the gap in talent is miniscule.

〔三〕　世語曰：帝與朝士素不接，即位之後，群下想聞風采。居數日，獨見侍中劉曄，語盡日。眾人側聽，曄既出，問「何如」？曄曰：「秦始皇、漢孝武之儔，才具微不及耳。」

Eighth Moon [226 Sep 10 – Oct 8], Sūn Quán attacked Jiāngxià prefecture, the Administrator Wén Pìn firmly defended. The Court discussed wishing to mobilize soldiers to rescue it. The Emperor said: “[Sūn] Quán is accustomed to river battle. That he dares dismount his boats to attack on land is only to surprise attack the unprepared. Now he is already locked together with [Wén] Pìn, attackers must be several times stronger than defenders, so in the end he will not dare remain long.” Before that time he had sent Managing Documents Attendant Censor Xún Yǔ to show appreciations for labors on the border regions. [Xún] Yǔ arrived at Jiāngxià and mobilized the soldiers of the counties he passed through and his accompanying infantry and cavalry of a thousand men to climb up the mountains and light fires. [Sūn] Quán retreated and fled.

八月，孫權攻江夏郡，太守文聘堅守。朝議欲發兵救之，帝曰：「權習水戰，所以敢下船陸攻者，幾掩不備也。今已與聘相持，夫攻守勢倍，終不敢久也。」先時遣治書侍御史荀禹慰勞邊方，禹到，於江夏發所經縣兵及所從步騎千人乘山舉火，權退走。

Xīnsì [226 Sep 21], established the Imperial son [Cáo] Jiǒng as King of Qīnghé. Wú officers Zhūgě Jǐn, Zhāng Bà, and others plundered Xiāngyáng. Succoring Army General-in-Chief Sīmǎ [Yì] Xuān-wáng suppressed and defeated them, beheaded [Zhāng] Bà. Campaigning East General-in-Chief Cáo Xiū also defeated their separate officers at Xúnyáng. Discussed the achievements and enacted rewards each on distinct basis. Winter, Tenth Moon [226 Nov 8 – Dec 6], the King of Qīnghé [Cáo] Jiǒng died. Twelfth Moon [227 Jan 6 – Feb 3], appointed Grand Commandant Zhōng Yáo as Grand Tutor, Campaigning East General-in-Chief Cáo Xiū as Marshal-in-Chief, Central Army General-in-Chief Cáo Zhēn as General-in-Chief, Excellency over the Masses Huà Xīn as Grand Commandant, Excellency of Works Wáng Lǎng as Excellency over the Masses, Defending Army General-in-Chief Chén Qún as Excellency of Works, Succoring Army General-in-Chief Sīmǎ [Yì] Xuān-wáng as Agile Cavalry General-in-Chief.

辛巳，立皇子冏為清河王。吳將諸葛瑾、張霸等寇襄陽，撫軍大將軍司馬宣王討破之，斬霸，征東大將軍曹休又破其別將於尋陽。論功行賞各有差。冬十月，清河王冏薨。十二月，以太尉鍾繇為太傅，征東大將軍曹休為大司馬，中軍大將軍曹真為大將軍，司徒華歆為太尉，司空王朗為司徒，鎮軍大將軍陳群為司空，撫軍大將軍司馬宣王為驃騎大將軍。

Tàihé Inaugural Year, Spring, Standard Moon [227 Feb 4 – Mar 5], Suburb Sacrifices to Wǔ Huángdì to match with Heaven, Ancestral Sacrifices to Wén Huángdì in Míngtáng to match with Highest God. Divided Jiāngxià’s southern part, installed a Jiāngxià Southern Division Commandant. Xīpíng’s Qū Yīng rebelled, killed the Línqiāng Magistrate and Xīdū Chief. Sent Generals Hǎo Zhāo and Lù Pán to suppress and beheaded him. Second Moon [227 Mar 6 – Apr 3], Xīnwèi [227 Mar 10], the Emperor plowed the Register Field. Xīnsì [227 Mar 20], established Wén-Zhāo Huánghòu’s tomb and temple at Yè. Dīnghài [227 Mar 26], Morning Sun in the East Suburbs. Summer, Fourth Moon [227 May 4 – Jun 1], enacted the wǔzhū coinage. Jiǎshēn [227 May 22], began construction of the Ancestral Temple. Autumn, Eighth Moon [227 Aug 30 – Sep 27], Evening Moon in the West Suburbs. Winter, Tenth Moon [227 Oct 28 – Nov 26], Bǐngyín [227 Oct 31], Drilled Troops in the East Suburbs. The King of Yānqí sent a son to enter as an attendant. Eleventh Moon [227 Nov 27 – Dec 25], enthroned as Imperial Empress Máo-shì. Bestowed on Heaven’s Under’s adult males noble rank at level two, the widowers and widows and orphans and childless and those unable to support themselves were bestowed grain. Twelfth Moon [227 Dec 26 – 228 Jan 24], enfeoffed the Empress’s father Máo Jiā as a Ranked Marquess. Xīnchéng Administrator Mèng Dá rebelled, Edict on Agile Cavalry General Sīmǎ [Yì] Xuān-wáng to suppress him. [4]

太和元年春正月，郊祀武皇帝以配天，宗祀文皇帝於明堂以配上帝。分江夏南部，置江夏南部都尉。西平麴英反，殺臨羌令、西都長，遣將軍郝昭、鹿磐討斬之。二月辛未，帝耕於籍田。辛巳，立文昭皇后寢廟於鄴。丁亥，朝日于東郊。夏四月乙亥，行五銖錢。甲申，初營宗廟。秋八月，夕月于西郊。冬十月丙寅，治兵于東郊。焉耆王遣子入侍。十一月，立皇后毛氏。賜天下男子爵人二級，鰥寡孤獨不能自存者賜穀。十二月，封后父毛嘉為列侯。新城太守孟達反，詔驃騎將軍司馬宣王討之。〔一〕

[4] Sānfǔ Juélù states: Bóláng, Liáng province man, his reputation not good.

Its annotation states: Bóláng was surnamed Mèng, personal name Tā, a Fúfēng man. In Emperor Líng’s time, Central Regular Attendant Zhāng Ràng controlled the Court and government, [Zhāng] Ràng’s overseer slave managed and protected household affairs. [Mèng] Tā’s political career was not successful, and so he completely used his family fortune to bribe the overseer slave, with him forming marriage alliance, and over the years his family finances because of this were completely ruined. The multitude of slaves were all ashamed and asked [Mèng] Tā what he wished for. [Mèng] Tā said: “I only wish for you sirs to bow [to me].” The slaves had received his generosity for a long time, and all agreed. At the time guest retainers sought to meet [Zhāng] Ràng so that below his gates there were always several hundred carriages, some for accumulated days being unable to pass through. [Mèng] Tā at the very end arrived, the multitude of slaves watched for his arrival, all welcomed his carriage and bowed, and directly led [Mèng] Tā’s carriage alone to enter. Everyone was completely amazed, said that [Mèng] Tā was friendly with [Zhāng] Ràng, and competed to gift precious treasures to [Mèng] Tā. [Mèng] Tā obtained these and completely used them to bribe [Zhāng] Ràng. [Zhāng] Ràng was greatly pleased. [Mèng] Tā also had grape alcohol of one hú gifted to [Zhāng] Ràng, and at once was appointed Liáng Province Inspector.

[Mèng] Tā begat [Mèng] Dá, who as a youth entered Shǔ. His residence in Shǔ’s affairs and legacy are in Liú Fēng zhuàn.

Wèi lüè states: [Mèng] Dá in Yánkāng Inaugural Year led his division retainers of over four thousand families to submit to Wèi. Emperor Wén [Cáo Pī] at the time had only just succeeded the King’s throne, had already been aware of [Mèng] Dá, heard of his coming, was extremely pleased, ordered important ministers with skill in appraisal to go observe him, returning they said: “He is a talent to be an officer,” or else said: “He is a vessel to be a minister.” The King increased his admiration of [Mèng] Dá. The greeting letter to [Mèng] Dá said: “In recent days there was a command that was not enough to convey my intentions. Why? In the past Yī Zhì turned from Shāng and submitted to Zhōu, Băilǐ left Yú and entered Qín, Yuè Yì was emotionally moved by liquor purse to molt like a cicada, Wáng Zūn recognized rebellion and obedience in order to leave and arrive. All investigated the correspondences and verifications of rise and fall, understood the inevitability of success or failure, and therefore vermillion and viridian painted their appearances, good scribes recorded their achievements. Hearing that your disposition and demeanor are genuine and fine, capacity outstanding, you should employ your abilities in an enlightened time, leaving a reputation to be transmitted and recorded. Presently you completely reform yourself, cleansing scales in pure currents, extremely commendable and joyous, my open heart looking west, yearning as for an old friend, using brush to write, my joyous heart follows it. In the past Yú Qīng entered Zhào and in a second meeting became Chancellor, Chén Píng arrived at Hàn and in one audience became an advisor in the carriage. I at present have emotions for you surpassing the past, and therefore deliver my own driving horses and equipment to demonstrate loyalty and love.” It also said: “Presently Within the Seas is purified and settled, ten thousand lǐ united as one, three sides without the alarm of frontier dust, the central Xià without the apprehensions of barking dogs, and so slackens restrictions and loosens prohibitions, have no doubts with the world, the prison offices are empty and vacant, for the first time there are no hostage appointments. You are coming to join me and should understand my intentions. Carefully do not allow your family followers to cause commotions on the roads, it would be having the close disturb the distant. If you wish to come and meet me, for the time being you should first calm your division retainers, have them guaranteed secure, and only afterward slowly and gently with light riders come east.” When [Mèng] Dá arrived at Qiáo, in advancing to meet he was graceful, his talent in debate surpassed others, of the masses none did not direct their eyes. Also the King nearby set out, rode a small carriage, grasped [Mèng] Dá’s hand, patted his back, and teased him saying: “You could never be Liú Bèi’s assassin, right?” Therefore they rode together. Also applied appointment as Cavalier Regular Attendant, designate Xīnchéng Administrator, entrusted with the responsibilities of the southwest. At the time of the multitudes of ministers some believed the treatment was too much, and also inappropriate to entrust a regional responsibility. The King heard this and said: “My guarantee that there will be no other [problems] is that it is like the example of a mugwort arrow shooting into mugwort.” [Mèng] Dá had been favored by Emperor Wén and also close and friendly with Huán Jiē and Xiàhóu Shàng. When Emperor Wén passed away, at the time Huán [Jiē] and [Xiàhóu] Shàng both had died, [Mèng] Dá himself believed he was a distant traveler who had for a long time on the borders, his heart was not at ease. Zhūgě Liàng heard of this, secretly wished to entice [Mèng] Dá, repeatedly sent letters to recruit him, and [Mèng] Dá sent replies. Wèixīng Administrator Shēn Yí with [Mèng] Dá had conflict, and secretly memorialized that [Mèng] Dá was covertly communicating with Shǔ. The Emperor did not yet believe it. Sīmǎ [Yì] Xuān-wáng sent Advisor to the Army Liáng Jī to investigate him, and also encourage him to enter Court. [Mèng] Dá was alarmed, and therefore rebelled.

Gān Bǎo’s Jìn jì states: When [Mèng] Dá first entered Xīnchéng, he climbed up Báimǎ fort, sighed and said: “Liú Fēng and Shēn Dān occupied metal fortresses of a thousand lǐ and yet lost this!”

You support me in having this Edict conferred on the Grand Commandant.” The warden galloped to leave. Before this, the King of Yān on behalf of the Emperor drew up a plan, that as Guānzhōng’s affairs were important, it was appropriate to use the shortcut side roads to send [Sīmǎ Yì] Xuān-wáng from Hénèi to return west, and the matter was put into effect. [Sīmǎ Yì] Xuān-wáng obtained the previous Edict, and shortly after this again obtained the later hand-written one, suspected the capital had a rebellion, and thus galloped to arrive, entering to meet the Emperor. The inquiries of his labors finished, then summoned the two Kings of Qí and Qín to show to [Sīmǎ Yì] Xuān-wáng, separately pointing at the King of Qí to say to [Sīmǎ Yì] Xuān-wáng stating: “This is the one. You carefully look at him, and make no mistake.” Also instructed the King of Qí to go forward and hug [Sīmǎ Yì] Xuān-wáng’s neck.

〔一〕　魏略曰：帝既從劉放計，召司馬宣王，自力為詔，既封，顧呼宮中常所給使者曰：「辟邪來！汝持我此詔授太尉也。」辟邪馳去。先是，燕王為帝畫計，以為關中事重，宜便道遣宣王從河內西還，事以施行。宣王得前詔，斯須復得後手筆，疑京師有變，乃馳到，入見帝。勞問訖，乃召齊、秦二王以示宣王，別指齊王謂宣王曰：「此是也，君諦視之，勿誤也！」又教齊王令前抱宣王頸。魏氏春秋曰：時太子芳年八歲，秦王九歲，在于御側。帝執宣王手，目太子曰：「死乃復可忍，朕忍死待君，君其與爽輔此。」宣王曰：「陛下不見先帝屬臣以陛下乎？」

Calculating to reach this year’s Standard Moon, altogether it is only thirty-four years. 〔三〕　臣松之按：魏武以建安九年八月定鄴，文帝始納甄后，明帝應以十年生，計至此年正月，整三十四年耳。時改正朔，以故年十二月為今年正月，可彊名三十五年，不得三十六也。

[36] Wèi shū states: The Emperor’s appearance and demeanor were impressive, displaying solemn dignity. From when he was in the East Palace, he did not engage with the Court ministers and did not ask after government affairs, only immersing his thoughts in books and nothing more. After he ascended the throne, he commended and respected the chief ministers, surmised and selected by achievement and ability, the true and the false unable to be passed off for one another, attended to cutting off the origins of the pretentious and flowery and of slander and defamation. In deploying the brigades and mobilizing the multitudes, discussing and deciding great affairs, the strategy ministers and officers and officials all submitted to the Emperor’s grand strategy. By nature he had especially strong memory, for even the Left and Right minor ministers, the career histories, personalities and conduct, names and reputations that he encountered, reaching to their fathers and elder brothers and sons and younger brothers, once they entered his ears or eyes, to the end he did not forget them. He swallowed insults and concealed displeasures, tolerantly accepted direct speech, listened to and accepted the sent-up letters from the officials and civilians and servicemen and commoners, within one moon several tens to hundreds of letters arriving, and even if the language was shallow and coarse, yet he read and reviewed to the end, his thoughts never tiring.

Sūn Shèng states: Hearing from elder generations, Wèi Emperor Míng had natural beauty refined and exceptional, on standing his hair hung to the ground, he stuttered and seldom spoke, but was steady and firm and very decisive. Previously, the various Excellencies received legacy orders to support and guide him, but the Emperor placed them all in regional appointments and governance came from himself. But he was exceptionally courteous to the chief ministers, open and tolerant and enjoying directness, and even when offended to the face with extreme remonstration, none were repressed or killed, his capacity as a lord of men was as great as this. However he did not consider establishing virtue to bequeath influence, did not solidify the foundation of maintaining walls, so that it caused great power to be unbalanced, the State Altars without protection, a sorrow!

〔四〕　魏書曰：帝容止可觀，望之儼然。自在東宮，不交朝臣，不問政事，唯潛思書籍而已。即位之後，褒禮大臣，料簡功能，真偽不得相貿，務絕浮華譖毀之端，行師動眾，論決大事，謀臣將相，咸服帝之大略。性特彊識，雖左右小臣官簿性行，名跡所履，及其父兄子弟，一經耳目，終不遺忘。含垢藏疾，容受直言，聽受吏民士庶上書，一月之中至數十百封，雖文辭鄙陋，猶覽省究竟，意無厭倦。孫監曰：聞之長老，魏明帝天姿秀出，立髮垂地，口吃少言，而沉毅好斷。初，諸公受遺輔導，帝皆以方任處之，政自己出。而優禮大臣，開容善直，雖犯顏極諫，無所摧戮，其君人之量如此之偉也。然不思建德垂風，不固維城之基，至使大權偏據，社稷無衛，悲夫！

Appraisal States: Emperor Míng was steady and firm, decisive and knowledgeable, followed his will and enacted it, overall having the utmost bearing of a lord of men. At the time when the Hundred Surnames were exhausted and worn, the Four Seas divided and collapsed, he did not first cultivate the legacy of his ancestors to open and expand vast foundations, but instead hurriedly imitated Qín Huáng and Hàn Wǔ, building Palaces and buildings, shelving the long-term plans, was it dangerous haste!

評曰：明帝沉毅斷識，任心而行，蓋有君人之至概焉。于時百姓彫弊，四海分崩，不先聿脩顯祖，闡拓洪基，而遽追秦皇、漢武，宮館是營，格之遠猷，其殆疾乎！
