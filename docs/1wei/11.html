11  Biographies of Yuán, Zhāng, Liáng, Guó, Tián, Wáng, Bǐng, Guǎn

Yuán Huàn

Yuán Huàn appellation Yàoqīng was a Chén prefecture Fúlè man. His father [Yuán] Pāng was a Hàn Excellency over the Masses. [1] At the time the sons of the various Excellencies had many transgress laws and limits, but [Yuán] Huàn was pure and tranquil, in actions certain to follow propriety. The prefecture mandated him as a Merit Officer,1 inside the prefectures the treacherous officials all themselves withdrew and left. Later he was recruited to the Offices of the Excellencies, nominated High Grade, promoted Attendant Censor.2 Sent out as Qiáo Magistrate, he did not go. When Liú Bèi was Yù Province (Governor), he nominated [Yuán] Huàn as Abundant Talent.3 Later he fled to between the Jiāng and Huái, and was by Yuán Shù mandated. Every time [Yuán] Shù had a consultation or inquiry, [Yuán] Huàn always had upright comments, [Yuán] Shù could not resist, however he respected him and did not dare not be courteous. Shortly after this, Lǚ Bù struck [Yuán] Shù at Fùlíng, [Yuán] Huàn went to follow him, and therefore again was by [Lǚ] Bù detained. [Lǚ] Bù originally was harmonious and close with Liú Bèi, later they separated into discord. [Lǚ] Bù wished to have [Yuán] Huàn wrote a letter to insult and disgrace [Liú] Bèi, [Yuán] Huàn would not, two and three times forcing him, he would not agree. [Lǚ] Bù was greatly furious, with weapons threatened [Yuán] Huàn stating: “Do it and you live, do not do it and you die.” [Yuán] Huàn’s expression did not change, he laughed and answered him stating: “[I, Yuán] Huàn have heard that only virtue can disgrace someone, and not heard of using insults. Suppose if the other is indeed a noble man, then he would not be humiliated by your words General, and if the other is truly a petty man, he will return your intentions General, then the disgrace would be here and not with the other. Moreover [I, Yuán] Huàn in another day served General Liú, yet today I serve you General. If in another morning I leave here, and return to insult you General, would it be permissible?” [Lǚ] Bù was ashamed and stopped.

袁渙字曜卿，陳郡扶樂人也。父滂，為漢司徒。〔一〕當時諸公子多越法度，而渙清靜，舉動必以禮。郡命為功曹，郡中姦吏皆自引去。後辟公府，舉高第，遷侍御史。除譙令，不就。劉備之為豫州，舉渙茂才。後避地江、淮間，為袁術所命。術每有所咨訪，渙常正議，術不能抗，然敬之不敢不禮也。頃之，呂布擊術於阜陵，渙往從之，遂復為布所拘留。布初與劉備和親，後離隙。布欲使渙作書詈辱備，渙不可，再三彊之，不許。布大怒，以兵脅渙曰：「為之則生，不為則死。」渙顏色不變，笑而應之曰：「渙聞唯德可以辱人，不聞以罵。使彼固君子邪，且不恥將軍之言，彼誠小人邪，將復將軍之意，則辱在此不在於彼。且渙他日之事劉將軍，猶今日之事將軍也，如一旦去此，復罵將軍，可乎？」布慚而止。

[1] Yuán Hóng’s Hàn jì states: [Yuán] Pāng appellation Gōngxī, was pure and plain with few desires, to the end did not speak of the shortcomings of others. When power and favor were flourishing, some by their alliances or opposition attracted disaster, [Yuán] Pāng alone stood neutral at Court, and therefore the loves and hates did not reach him.

〔一〕　袁宏漢紀曰：滂字公熙，純素寡欲，終不言人之短。當權寵之盛，或以同異致禍，滂獨中立於朝，故愛憎不及焉。

When [Lǚ] Bù was executed, [Yuán] Huàn was able to join Tàizǔ. [2] [Yuán] Huàn advised stating: “Warfare is a vicious instrument, used when one cannot but use it. Drum them with principle and virtue, campaign against them with benevolence and righteousness, simultaneously succor their people and eliminate their harms. If so, then they can die for you and can live for you. From since the great chaos onward it has been ten and several years, the people’s desire for security is extreme as hanging upside down, however yet the violent chaos has not yet stopped. Why? An opinion is that government has lost its way. [I, Yuán] Huàn have heard that the enlightened lord excels in rescuing the world, therefore when the world is in chaos then they order it with righteousness, in times of deceit then they subdue it with simplicity. As eras differ and situations change, the governance of states is not the same, it cannot be not investigated. Modification of regulation systems, this is what not need be the same from antiquity to the present. As for universal love for Heaven’s Under and returning it to the correct, even though one use warfare to pacify chaos and relieve it with virtue, truly it is the Way that the Hundred Kings did not change. Your Excellency have enlightenment and wisdom surpassing the era, the ways that the ancients obtained the people, Your Excellency has already diligently strived for, the ways that the present lost the people, Your Excellency has already guarded against. Within the Seas depends on Your Excellency to escape from the disaster of danger and destruction, however the people do not yet know righteousness, thus may Your Excellency instruct them, then Heaven’s Under would be extremely fortunate.” Tàizǔ deeply accepted this. Appointed as Pèi South Division Commandant.

布誅，渙得歸太祖。〔一〕渙言曰：「夫兵者，凶器也，不得已而用之。鼓之以道德，征之以仁義，兼撫其民而除其害。夫然，故可與之死而可與之生。自大亂以來十數年矣，民之欲安，甚於倒懸，然而暴亂未息者，何也？意者政失其道歟！渙聞明君善于救世，故世亂則齊之以義，時偽則鎮之以樸；世異事變，治國不同，不可不察也。夫制度損益，此古今之不必同者也。若夫兼愛天下而反之於正，雖以武平亂而濟之以德，誠百王不易之道也。公明哲超世，古之所以得其民者，公既勤之矣，今之所以失其民者，公既戒之矣，海內賴公，得免於危亡之禍，然而民未知義，其惟公所以訓之，則天下幸甚！」太祖深納焉。拜為沛南部都尉。

[2] Yuán-shì Shìjì4 states: When [Lǚ] Bù was defeated, Chén Qún father and sons at the time also were in [Lǚ] Bù’s army, on meeting Tàizǔ all bowed. [Yuán] Huàn alone highly saluted and was not (excessively) courteous.5 Tàizǔ was extremely cautious and respectful of him. At the time Tàizǔ also gave the multitude of officials each several carts, having them take things from [Lǚ] Bù’s army, whatever they wanted. Everyone all loaded heavily, only [Yuán] Huàn took books of several hundred fascicles and provisions and nothing more. Everyone heard of this and was greatly ashamed. [Yuán] Huàn spoke to those close to him stating: “If I were travelling with the formation, on the army setting out there would be enough for travel provisions, and I would not have taken this as my own. Because of this it sharpened my reputation, I greatly regret it.” Tàizǔ because of this increasingly respected him.

〔一〕　袁氏世紀曰：布之破也，陳群父子時亦在布之軍，見太祖皆拜。渙獨高揖不為禮，太祖甚嚴憚之。時太祖又給眾官車各數乘，使取布軍中物，唯其所欲。眾人皆重載，唯渙取書數百卷。資糧而已，眾人聞之，大慚。渙謂所親曰：「脫我以行陳，令軍發足以為行糧而已，不以此為我有。由是厲名也，大悔恨之。」太祖益以此重焉。

At the time newly recruited civilians opened Garrison Farms, the people were not pleased, many fled and deserted.6 [Yuán] Huàn informed Tàizǔ stating: “People once settled on land are difficult to move, and cannot be quickly changed, easy to move in compliance, difficult to move in opposition. It is appropriate to comply with their wishes, please them and then take them, and those that do not wish it not forced.” Tàizǔ followed this, the Hundred Surnames were greatly pleased. Promoted to Liáng Chancellor. [Yuán] Huàn every time ordered the various counties: “Attend to caring for widowers and widows and highly aged, memorializing exceptional filial sons and chaste women. Always discussions state: ‘When the world is governed then ritual is detailed, then world is in chaos then ritual is simplified.’ Everything is merely in balancing. Thought right now is disturbed and in turmoil, difficult to transform with ritual. however it is in how we do it.” In enacting government he esteemed teaching and instruction, deeply thought and afterward acted, outside warm and gentle but inside capable of decisiveness. [3] Due ot illness left office, the Hundred Surnames missed him. Later summoned as Remonstration Consultant Grandee, Assistant Chancellor’s Army Libationer. Beginning to end his obtained bestowments were extremely many, he gave it all out completely, his household had no stores, to the end he did not ask after property and business, when lacking then he borrowed from others, did not act with demanding scrutiny, however at the time people submitted to his purity.

是時新募民開屯田，民不樂，多逃亡。渙白太祖曰：「夫民安土重遷，不可卒變，易以順行，難以逆動，宜順其意，樂之者乃取，不欲者勿彊。」太祖從之，百姓大悅。遷為梁相。渙每敕諸縣：「務存鰥寡高年，表異孝子貞婦。常談曰『世治則禮詳，世亂則禮簡』，全在斟酌之閒耳。方今雖擾攘，難以禮化，然在吾所以為之。」為政崇教訓，恕思而後行，外溫柔而內能斷。〔一〕以病去官，百姓思之。後徵為諫議大夫、丞相軍祭酒。前後得賜甚多，皆散盡之，家無所儲，終不問產業，乏則取之於人，不為皦察之行，然時人服其清。

[3] Wèi shū states: Gǔshū Chief7 Lǚ Qí was friendly with Zhū Yuān and Yuán Jīn, sent them as envoys to study abroad, returned, summoned and employed them, met with them, sent them out to install [Zhū] Yuān as Teacher Friend Libationer, [Yuán] Jīn as Resolving Doubts Libationer. [Zhū] Yuān and the rest took advantage to each return home, not accepting the installations. [Lǚ] Qí was greatly furious, led clerks and civilians to arrest [Zhū] Yuān and the rest, and all beat them to death. Of commentators many criticized this. [Yuán] Huàn instructed to not impeach. Registrar Sūn Huī and others believed: “The crime of [Zhū] Yuān and the rest was not enough for death, Chief Clerks do not have authority to arbitrarily kill. Kǒng-zǐ appraised:‘Only instruments and names cannot be lent to others.’8 In calling them Teacher Friend and yet apply great execution, the punishment and name contradict one another, it cannot be a teaching example.” [Yuán] Huàn’s instructions said: “The Registrar believes not requesting to be a crime, and this then is correct. Saying that the crime of [Zhū] Yuān and the rest was not enough for death, then that is incorrect. The name of teacher friend, antiquity to the present has had it, however there are teacher friends to lords and there are teacher friends to serviceman officials. A lord installs the office of Teacher Friend in order to respect their servants. Those with crimes being applied with punishments is the state’s law. Now to not discuss the crime and yet call it killing a Teacher Friend, this is to be mistaken of it. The Registrar uses the name of a disciple killing teacher, but applies it on the fact of a lord executing servant, it is not of its category. The governance of the sages observed timing and then moved, and therefore did not necessarily follow regularity, using expediences. In the middle of the world in chaos, people undermine their superiors, and though we attend to honoring lords and diminishing servants, yet sometimes it is not enough, but yet you instead increase the transgressions of the world, is it not also absurd?” Therefore did not impeach.

〔一〕　魏書曰：穀熟長呂岐善朱淵、爰津，遣使行學還，召用之，與相見，出署淵師友祭酒，津決疑祭酒。淵等因各歸家，不受署。岐大怒，將吏民收淵等，皆杖殺之，議者多非焉。渙教勿劾，主簿孫徽等以為「淵等罪不足死，長吏無專殺之義，孔子稱『唯器與名，不可以假人』。謂之師友而加大戮，刑名相伐，不可以訓。」渙教曰：「主簿以不請為罪，此則然矣。謂淵等罪不足死，則非也。夫師友之名，古今有之。然有君之師友，有士大夫之師友。夫君置師友之官者，所以敬其臣也；有罪加於刑焉，國之法也。今不論其罪而謂之戮師友，斯失之矣。主簿取弟子戮師之名，而加君誅臣之實，非其類也。夫聖哲之治，觀時而動，故不必循常，將有權也。閒者世亂，民陵其上，雖務尊君卑臣，猶或未也，而反長世之過，不亦謬乎！」遂不劾。

When Wèi state was first established, became Minister of Cadets,9 Enacting Censorate Grandee Affairs. [Yuán] Huàn advised Tàizǔ stating: “Now Heaven’s Under’s greatest troubles are already eliminated. Employing both the civil and the martial is the way for the long term. I believe we can greatly collect fasciles and registers, make clear the instructions of the former sages, to change what the people see and hear, to have Within the Seas gracefully turn to influence, then the distant people that do not submit can be by civil virtue made to come.” Tàizǔ approved his advice. At the time there was rumor that Liú Bèi had died, the various ministers all gave congratulations, [Yuán] Huàn because he had once been a clerk nominated by [Liú] Bèi alone did not give congratulations. Holding office for several years he died. Tàizǔ shed tears for him, bestowed grain of two thousand hú, one instruction: “From the Grand Granary’s grain of a thousand hú bestowed on the family of the Minister of Cadets,” one instruction: “Have the grain from below my walls of a thousand hú given to Yàoqīng’s family.” The outside did not understand its meaning. Instruction said: “Using grain from the Grand Granary is for government law. Using grain from below my walls is for personal old friendship.” Also the Emperor heard of the matter in the past of [Yuán] Huàn refusing Lǚ Bù, and asked [Yuán] Huàn’s younger cousin [Yuán] Mǐn: “Was [Yuán] Huàn brave or timid?” [Yuán] Mǐn replied stating: “[Yuán] Huàn in appearance seemed harmonious and gentle, however when he faced great matters or was in danger, even Bēn and Yù did not surpass him.”10 [Yuán] Huàn’s son [Yuán] Kǎn also was pure and refined, having his father’s mannerisms, successively ranked as Prefecture Administrator and Secretariat Writer. [4]

魏國初建，為郎中令，行御史大夫事。渙言於太祖曰：「今天下大難已除，文武並用，長久之道也。以為可大收篇籍，明先聖之教，以易民視聽，使海內斐然向風，則遠人不服可以文德來之。」太祖善其言。時有傳劉備死者，群臣皆賀；渙以嘗為備舉吏，獨不賀。居官數年卒，太祖為之流涕，賜穀二千斛，一教「以太倉穀千斛賜郎中令之家」，一教「以垣下穀千斛與曜卿家」，外不解其意。教曰：「以太倉穀者，官法也；以垣下穀者，親舊也。」又帝聞渙昔拒呂布之事，問渙從弟敏：「渙勇怯何如？」敏對曰：「渙貌似和柔，然其臨大節，處危難，雖賁育不過也。」渙子侃，亦清粹閒素，有父風，歷位郡守尚書。〔一〕

[4] Yuán-shì Shìjì states: [Yuán] Huàn had four sons: Kǎn, Yǔ, Ào, Zhǔn. [Yuán] Kǎn appellation Gōngrán in discussion was pure and proper, gentle but unyielding, excelled in interacting with others. In the middle of the fall and rise, people attended to their pursuits, he always modestly withdrew and did not act. At the time people because of this praised him. Successively ranked as Yellow Gate Selections Department Cadet, called as “Pure and Fair.”11 Gradually promoted ot reach Secretariat Writer, early died. [Yuán] Yǔ appellation Xuānhòu was elite in debate and had reasoning, enjoyed the teachings of the Dào school, in youth was ill, not yet held office and died. [Yuán] Ào appellation Gōngróng in conduct was sufficient to sharpen customs, speech balanced and yet logical and proper, ended as Minister of Staff. [Yuán] Zhǔn appellation Xiàoní was loyal and faithful, fair and upright, not ashamed to ask subordinates, ony fearing others would not surpass him. As the matters of the world had many dangers, therefore he always was quiet and withdrawn and did not dare seek advancement. He wrote books of over a hundred thousand words, discussed the affairs of governing the world, made commentaries for the Yì, Zhōu Guān, and Shī,12 with a Discourse on the obscure meanings of the Five Classics, the subtle words of sages, to transmit to the world. This is [Yuán] Zhǔn’s self introduction.

Xún Chuò’s Jiǔzhōu jì appraises [Yuán] Zhǔn as having outstanding talent, during Tàishǐ [265 - 274] became a Dispensing Affairs Central. The Yuán clan’s descendants for generations had fame and position, nobility reaching to the present.

〔一〕　袁氏世紀曰：渙有四子，侃、宇、奧、準。侃字公然，論議清當，柔而不犯，善與人交。在廢興之間，人之所趣務者，常謙退不為也。時人以是稱之。歷位黃門選部郎，號為清平。稍遷至尚書，早卒。宇字宣厚，精辯有機理，好道家之言，少被病，未官而卒，奧字公榮，行足以厲俗，言約而理當，終於光祿勳。準字孝尼，忠信公正，不恥下問，唯恐人之不勝已。以世事多險，故常恬退而不敢求進。著書十餘萬言，論治世之務，為易、周官、詩傳，及論五經滯義，聖人之微言，以傳於世。此準之自序也。荀綽九州記稱準有雋才，泰始中為給事中。袁氏子孫世有名位，貴達至今。

Previously, [Yuán] Huàn’s younger cousin [Yuán] Bà was public-minded and scrupulous, having achievement and competence, at Wèi’s beginning became Minister of Agriculture, with his same prefecture’s Hé Kuí both famed at the time. And [Yuán] Bà’s son [Yuán] Liàng, [Hé] Kuì’s son [Hé] Zēng with [Yuán] Kǎn further were also equal in reputation and friendship. He wished to distantly travel to study, visited Ānqiū’s Sūn Sōng.56 [Sūn] Sōng declined stating: “Your hometown’s Zhèng-jūn, do you know of him?” [Bǐng] Yuán answered stating: “Yes.” [Sūn] Sōng said: “Zhèng-jūn studies and reads antiquity to the present, widely learned and strong memory, hooks deep and reaches far, truly a model teacher for scholars. You yet abandon him and tread a thousand lǐ, what is called treating Zhèng as an east family rustic. You seem to not know and yet say yes, why?” [Bǐng] Yuán said: “Your statement master truly can be called bitter medicine and good needle, however yet you do not understand my meager intent. People each have their ambitions, what they plan is not the same, therefore then there are those that climb mountains to pluck jade, those that enter the seas to pluck pearls, how can it be said that the one climbing mountains does not know the depth of the sea and those that enter the sea do not know the height of mountains? You say I treat Zhèng as an east family rustic, are you are treating me as a west family fool?” [Sūn] Sōng apologized. Also said: “The servicemen of Yǎn and Yù, I know of many, there are none that compare to you. I will share my books with you.” [Bǐng] Yuán valued his intention, found it difficult to decline them, accepted the books and departed. [Bǐng] Yuán in his heart believed: “In seeking teachers and beginning study, ambitions being high leads to understanding, it is not like friendly relationships relying on sharing to succeed. Books are of what use?” Thus he stored the books in the house and traveled. [Bǐng] Yuán formerly was able to drink alcohol, from after he travelled, within eight to nine years, alcohol did not reach his mouth. He walked alone carying bookbag, laboring body and exerting strength, in reaching Chénliú then took as teacher Hán Zǐzhù, Yǐngchuān then took as exemplar Chén Zhònggōng, Rǔnán then associated Fàn Mèngbó, Zhuō Prefecture then close with Lú Zǐgàn. When about to part, the teachers and friends believed [Bǐng] Yuán did not drink alcohol, and gathered rice and meat to send off [Bǐng] Yuán. [Bǐng] Yuán said: “Originally I was able to drink alcohol, only because it disturbs thinking and damages work, therefore I abstained from it. Now when about to distantly depart, and because I meet with a farewell dinner, I can have one drink.” Thereupon they together sat and drank alcohol, to the end of day not intoxicated. He returned home and had the books returned to Sūn Sōng, explaining the intention of not bringing the books. Later he was summoned by the prefecture, appointed Merit Officer Registrar. At the time Lǔ state’s Kǒng Róng was at the prefecture, instructed that the selections for Accounting should appoint talents for the Excellencies and Ministers, thus appointed Zhèng Xuán as Accounting Assistant, Péng Qiú as Accounting Clerk, [Bǐng] Yuán as Accounting Aide. [Kǒng] Róng had one person he favored, always extensively sighing in admiration of him. Later he was enraged and disappointed and wished to kill him, the Court clerks all pleaded. At the time the person also was seated, knocked head until blood flowed, but [Kǒng] Róng’s emotions were not resolved. [Bǐng] Yuán alone did not plead for him. [Kǒng] Róng spoke to [Bǐng] Yuán stating: “Everyone is all pleading but why do you sir alone not?” [Bǐng] Yuán replied stating: “Your Enlightened Office’s57 relationship to him originally was not meager, always saying at the end of the year you would nominate him, this is what is called ‘my one child.’ Like this, the Court clerks in receiving favor have never been ahead of him, but now you wish to kill him. When Your Enlightened Office loves him then you drew him in and compared him to your child, when you detest him then you push him away wishing to endanger his life. [I, Bǐng] Yuán am foolish, and do not understand Your Enlightened Office for what loves him and for what hates him.” [Kǒng] Róng said: “He was born to a humble family, I brought up he and his brothers, selected out and employed him, he now turns back on me and my kindness. When good then advance them, when evil then execute them, this is assuredly the lord’s way. Formerly Yīng Zhòngyuǎn was Tàishān Administrator, nominated one Filial-Incorruptible, and between ten days and a moon yet killed him. For a lord of men, in generosity or meagerness what constancy is to be had?” [Bǐng] Yuán replied stating: “[Yīng] Zhòngyuǎn nominating a Filial-Incorruptible and killing him, where is the righteousness in that? The Filial-Incorruptible is the state’s eminent selection. If nominating him was correct, then killing him was wrong. If killing him was correct, then nominating him was wrong. The Shī says:”That creature, not following its engagement,“58 probably to ridicule this. The Yǔ says:”Loving them wishing for their life, hating them wishing for their death. Already wishing for their life, also wishing for their death, this is confusion.”59 Zhòngyuǎn’s confusion was extreme. Why does Your Enlightened Office seek this?” [Kǒng] Róng then greatly laughed and said: “I was only joking!” [Bǐng] Yuán also said: “For a noble man their words are produced from the body and applied on the people. Words and actions are the turning point of the noble man. How can there be a wish to kill a man that can be made into a joke?” [Kǒng] Róng was without an answer. At the time the Hàn Court was in decline, government achieved through bribery. [Bǐng] Yuán thus led his family to enter the Yù island mountains. The prefecture nominated him as Having the Way, [Kǒng] Róng’s letter explained to [Bǐng] Yuán stating: “You cultivate your nature and protect steadfastness, purify humility and maintain loftiness, endangered countries you do not enter, long hidden in happy lands. The Royal House has many troubles, westward moved to Hàojīng.60 The Sagely Court labors humbly,61 widely seeks62 eminent talents. I go to search for settlement, certificate commands earnest and sorrowful. When the state was about to fall, the widow did not concern with weaving.63 When the family is about to persih, Tíyíng walked and waded.64 These were commoner women, yet they maintained this righteousness. I truly look to you Gēnjǔ, to take benevolence as your own responsibility, to give a hand to save the drowning, to raise the people from troubles. Yet instead you quietly reside at rest, none willing to consider me. Calling this a noble man, surely can it be like this? Gēnjǔ, Gēnjǔ, you can come now.” [Bǐng] Yuán therefore arrived at Liáodōng. Liáodōng had many tigers, [Bǐng] Yuán’s village alone had no tiger problems. [Bǐng] Yuán once walked and found lost money, picked it up and tied it to a tree branch. This money was not claimed, but the tied money increased. Asking the reason, those answering said it was a spirit tree. [Bǐng] Yuán hated that an illicit cult had formed because of him, and so explained it, thereupon the neighborhood then collected the money to provide for altar sacrifices. Later [Bǐng] Yuán wished to return to his hometown, stopped at Sānshān.65 Kǒng Róng’s letter said: “Suí Huì at Qín, Jiǎ Jì at Dí,66 nowhere to turn for guidance, the sighs increase in longing. Does the Shī not say: ‘Coming to return from Hào, our journey ever long.’67 Now I send my Five Office Assistant to respectfully ask after the labors of men of the boat and oars, to comfort and console disasters or fortunes of movements or stillness. The steps to chaos are not yet ended, the powerful relying on soldiers are like board game competing pieces.” [Bǐng] Yuán thereupon then again turned back. Accumulated over ten years, afterward then he escaped and returned. He had journeyed south already for several days when [Gōngsūn] Dù just became aware. [Gōngsūn] Dù knew [Bǐng] Yuán could not be again pursued, and therefore said: “That Lord Bǐng was called a white crane in the clouds was that he is not what can be caught with nets for quails. Also I myself drove him away, do not again seek him.” Therefore he escaped danger and trouble. From since he returned to his homeland, [Bǐng] Yuán thereupon lectured on ritual and music, recited the Shī and Shū, his disciples were several hundred, the submitting to the way several tens. At the time Zhèng Xuán was widely learned with extensive knowedge, annotated and explained canon registers, therefore servicemen of classicist refinement gathered to him. [Bǐng] Yuán also was himself lofty and pure, nourished will in quite simplicity, mouth without fussy words, body without fussy conduct, therefore servicemen of outstanding greatness turned to him. At the time Within the Seas Pure Criticism said that Qīng Province had the scholarship of Bǐng and Zhèng. When Wèi Tàizǔ was Excellency of Works, he recruited [Bǐng] Yuán and appointed him East Pavilion Libationer. Tàizǔ campaigned north against the Three Prefectures Chányú, returned and stopped at Chángguó,68 feasted the servicemen officials. While intoxicated from alcohol, Tàizǔ said: “When I return, the various sirs guarding Yè certainly will come welcome, by today and tomorrow at dawn, I judge they will all have arrived. The ones that will not come, will only be Libationer Bǐng.” Not long after he finished speaking, [Bǐng] Yuán first arrived. The gate keeper announced the visit, Tàizǔ was greatly surprised and pleased, got up holding his shoes, distantly went out to welcome [Bǐng] Yuán and said: “The worthy truly are difficult to fathom and judge! Tàizǔ was bewildered and asked about it, at the time Xún Wénruò was seated, and replied stating: “It is only to be able to see and ask after Bǐng Yuán.” Tàizǔ said: “This sir’s reputation is heavy then to also overturn the hearts of the servicemen officials?” Wénruò said: “This is an exceptional man of the entire era, the refined elite of servicemen, Your Excellency should be thoroughly courteous in treating him.” Tàizǔ said: “Assuredly it is my long held intention.” From this afterward, he met with increasingly heavy respect. Although [Bǐng] Yuán was in the army in successive appointments, he always claimed illness, resting high in his home alleys, to the end not handling affairs, also rarely meeting. Hénèi’s Zhāng Fàn was the son of a famed Excellency, his will and conduct having correspondance with [Bǐng] Yuán, the were extremely close with and respected one another. Hearing that Zhāng-zǐ tends to wish to learn from him, I fear those that encounter him will be wealthy, those who follow him be impoverished.” When the Wèi Heir-Apparent was Five Office Central Cadet General, Heaven’s Under turned in admiration, guest retainers like clouds, but [Bǐng] Yuán alone upheld the way and maintained constancy, and if it was not a public affair he did not rashly act or move. Tàizǔ secretly sent someone to casually ask him, [Bǐng] Yuán said: “I have heard that when the state is endangered do not serve the prime minister, when the lord is away do not revere the heir presumptive. This is a canonical regulation.” Thereupon he was then transferred to Five Office Chief Scribe, the Order said: “My son is weak and not talented, I fear he is difficult to correct. I greedily wish to impose on you, to rectify and encourage him. Although it is said to benefit from the worthy, can I not be ashamed?” The Heir-Apparent held a feast gathering, the mass of guests were a hundred and several tens of people, the Heir-Apparent established a discussion stating: “If lord and father each had a critical illness, and you had a medicine of one pill, that could save one person, would you rescue lord or father?” Everyone was varied and muddled, some choosing father and some choosing lord. At the time [Bǐng] Yuán was seated, and did not participate in this discussion. The Heir-Apparent consulted [Bǐng] Yuán about it, [Bǐng] Yuán indignantly replied stating: “Father.” The Heir-Apparent also did not further challenge this.

〔二〕　原別傳曰：原十一而喪父，家貧，早孤。鄰有書舍，原過其旁而泣。師問曰：「童子何悲？」原曰：「孤者易傷，貧者易感。夫書者，必皆具有父兄者，一則羨其不孤，二則羨其得學，心中惻然而為涕零也。」師亦哀原之言而為之泣曰：「欲書可耳！」答曰：「無錢資。」師曰：「童子苟有志，我徒相教，不求資也。」於是遂就書。一冬之間，誦孝經、論語。自在童齔之中，嶷然有異。及長，金玉其行。欲遠游學，詣安丘孫崧。崧辭曰：「君鄉里鄭君，君知之乎？」原答曰：「然。」崧曰：「鄭君學覽古今，博聞彊識，鉤深致遠，誠學者之師模也。君乃舍之，躡屣千里，所謂以鄭為東家丘者也。君似不知而曰然者，何？」原曰：「先生之說，誠可謂苦藥良鍼矣；然猶未達僕之微趣也。人各有志，所規不同，故乃有登山而採玉者，有入海而採珠者，豈可謂登山者不知海之深，入海者不知山之高哉！君謂僕以鄭為東家丘，君以僕為西家愚夫邪？」崧辭謝焉。又曰：「兗、豫之士，吾多所識，未有若君者；當以書相分。」原重其意，難辭之，持書而別。原心以為求師啟學，志高者通，非若交游待分而成也。書何為哉？乃藏書於家而行。原舊能飲酒，自行之後，八九年間，酒不向口。單步負笈，苦身持力，至陳留則師韓子助，潁川則宗陳仲弓，汝南則交范孟博，涿郡則親盧子幹。臨別，師友以原不飲酒，會米肉送原。原曰：「本能飲酒，但以荒思廢業，故斷之耳。今當遠別，因見貺餞，可一飲讌。」於是共坐飲酒，終日不醉。歸以書還孫崧，解不致書之意。後為郡所召，署功曹主簿。時魯國孔融在郡，教選計當任公卿之才，乃以鄭玄為計掾，彭璆為計吏，原為計佐。融有所愛一人，常盛嗟歎之。後恚望，欲殺之，朝吏皆請。時其人亦在坐，叩頭流血，而融意不解。原獨不為請。融謂原曰：「眾皆請而君何獨不？」原對曰：「明府於某，本不薄也，常言歲終當舉之，此所謂『吾一子』也。如是，朝吏受恩未有在某前者矣，而今乃欲殺之。明府愛之，則引而方之於子，憎之，則推之欲危其身。原愚，不知明府以何愛之？以何惡之？」融曰：「某生于微門，吾成就其兄弟，拔擢而用之；某今孤負恩施。夫善則進之，惡則誅之，固君道也。往者應仲遠為泰山太守，舉一孝廉，旬月之閒而殺之。夫君人者，厚薄何常之有！」原對曰：「仲遠舉孝廉，殺之，其義焉在？夫孝廉，國之俊選也。舉之若是，則殺之非也；若殺之是，則舉之非也。詩云：『彼己之子，不遂其媾。』蓋譏之也。語云：『愛之欲其生，惡之欲其死。既欲其生，又欲其死，是惑也。』仲遠之惑甚矣。明府奚取焉？」融乃大笑曰：「吾直戲耳！」原又曰：「君子於其言，出乎身，加乎民；言行，君子之樞機也。安有欲殺人而可以為戲者哉？」融無以答。是時漢朝陵遲，政以賄成，原乃將家人入鬱洲山中。郡舉有道，融書喻原曰：「脩性保貞，清虛守高，危邦不入，久潛樂土。王室多難，西遷鎬京。聖朝勞謙，疇咨雋乂。我徂求定，策命懇惻。國之將隕，嫠不恤緯，家之將亡，緹縈跋涉，彼匹婦也，猶執此義。實望根矩，仁為己任，授手援溺，振民於難。乃或晏晏居息，莫我肯顧，謂之君子，固如此乎！根矩，根矩，可以來矣！」原遂到遼東。遼東多虎，原之邑落獨無虎患。原嘗行而得遺錢，拾以繫樹枝，此錢既不見取，而繫錢者愈多。問其故，答者謂之神樹。原惡其由己而成淫祀，乃辨之，於是里中遂斂其錢以為社供。後原欲歸鄉里，止於三山。孔融書曰：「隨會在秦，賈季在翟，諮仰靡所，歎息增懷。頃知來至，近在三山。詩不云乎，『來歸自鎬，我行永久』。今遣五官掾，奉問榜人舟楫之勞，禍福動靜告慰。亂階未已，阻兵之雄，若棋弈爭梟。」原於是遂復反還。積十餘年，後乃遁還。南行已數日，而度甫覺。度知原之不可復追也，因曰：「邴君所謂雲中白鶴，非鶉鷃之網所能羅矣。又吾自遣之，勿復求也。」遂免危難。自反國土，原於是講述禮樂，吟咏詩書，門徒數百，服道數十。時鄭玄博學洽聞，註解典籍，故儒雅之士集焉。原亦自以高遠清白，頤志澹泊，口無擇言，身無擇行，故英偉之士向焉。是時海內清議，云青州有邴、鄭之學。魏太祖為司空，辟原署東閤祭酒。太祖北伐三郡單于，還住昌國，燕士大夫。酒酣，太祖曰：「孤反，鄴守諸君必將來迎，今日明旦，度皆至矣。其不來者，獨有邴祭酒耳！」言訖未久，而原先至。門下通謁，太祖大驚喜，攬履而起，遠出迎原曰：「賢者誠難測度！孤謂君將不能來，而遠自屈，誠副饑虛之心。」謁訖而出，軍中士大夫詣原者數百人。太祖怪而問之，時荀文若在坐，對曰：「獨可省問邴原耳！」太祖曰：「此君名重，乃亦傾士大夫心？」文若曰：「此一世異人，士之精藻，公宜盡禮以待之。」太祖曰：「固孤之宿心也。」自是之後，見敬益重。原雖在軍歷署，常以病疾，高枕里巷，終不當事，又希會見。河內張範，名公之子也，其志行有與原符，甚相親敬。令曰：「邴原名高德大，清規邈世，魁然而峙，不為孤用。聞張子頗欲學之，吾恐造之者富，隨之者貧也。」魏太子為五官中郎將，天下向慕，賓客如雲，而原獨守道持常，自非公事不妄舉動。太祖微使人從容問之，原曰：「吾聞國危不事冢宰，君去不奉世子，此典制也。」於是乃轉五官長史，令曰：「子弱不才，懼其難正，貪欲相屈，以匡勵之。雖云利賢，能不恧恧！」太子燕會，眾賓百數十人，太子建議曰：「君父各有篤疾，有藥一丸，可救一人，當救君邪，父邪？」眾人紛紜，或父或君。時原在坐，不與此論。太子諮之于原，原悖然對曰：「父也。」太子亦不復難之。

Guǎn Níng

Guǎn Níng appellation Yòu’ān was a Běihǎi Zhūxū man. [29] Aged sixteen he lost his father, his distaff cousins69 pitied that he was orphaned and poor, all together offered funeral gifts, all he declined and would not accept, using his own money for the funeral. He grew to eight chǐ, beautiful beard and eyebrows. He with Píngyuán’s Huà Xīn and his same county’s Bǐng Yuán were friends with one another, together traveling and studying in other states, and they together respected and befriended Chén Zhònggōng.70 Heaven’s Under was in great chaos, he heard that Gōngsūn Dù spread order beyond the sea, and therefore with [Bǐng] Yuán and Píngyuán’s Wáng Liè and others arrived at Liáodōng. [Gōngsūn] Dù cleared a lodge to wait for them. After going to meet [Gōngsūn] Dù, he then lived in a hut in a mountain valley.
