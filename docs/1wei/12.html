12  Biographies of Cuī, Máo, Xú, Hé, Xíng, Bào, Sīmǎ

Cuī Yǎn

Cuī Yǎn appellation Jìguī was a Qīnghé Dōngwǔchéng man. As a youth he was plain and inarticulate, enjoyed fencing swords, esteemed martial affairs. Aged twenty-three, the village transferred him to be a Rectifier, he was first emotionally moved and roused, read the Lúnyǔ and Hán Shī. Reaching age twenty-nine, then he joined Gōngsūn Fāng and others in going to Zhèng Xuán to receive study. Their studies were not yet finished, Xú Province Huángjīn banidts attacked and defeated Běihǎi, [Zhèng] Xuán with his disciples arrived at Bùqí mountain to flee the troubles. At the time grain in the county was scarce, [Zhèng] Xuán dismissed and apologized to his various students. [Cuī] Yǎn had already been sent away, but bandits and thieves were everywhere, the roads west were impassable. Thereupon he traveled around the outskirts of Qīng, Xú, Yǎn, Yù, east downstream to Shòuchūn, south looking out the Jiāng and Hú. From when he left home was four years, then he returned home, with zither and books amusing himself.

崔琰字季珪，清河東武城人也。少樸訥，好擊劍，尚武事。年二十三，鄉移為正，始感激，讀論語、韓詩。至年二十九，乃結公孫方等就鄭玄受學。學未期，徐州黃巾賊攻破北海，玄與門人到不其山避難。時穀糴縣乏，玄罷謝諸生。琰既受遣，而寇盜充斥，西道不通。于是周旋青、徐、兗、豫之郊，東下壽春，南望江、湖。自去家四年乃歸，以琴書自娛。

General-in-Chief Yuán Shào heard of and recruited him. At the time soldiers were unrstrained and violent, digging and excavating grave mounds, [Cuī] Yǎn remonstrated stating: “In the past Sūn Qīng had a saying: ‘If servicemen are not previously trained, armor and weapons are not sharp, even Tāng and Wǔ would be unable to be victorious in battle.’ Now the roads have exposed bones, the people do not yet see virtue, it is appropriate to order the prefectures and counties to cover bones and bury rotting flesh, to show compassion for sorrow and grief, and recall the benevolence of King Wén.” [Yuán] Shào appointed him a Cavalry Commandant. Later [Yuán] Shào drilled troops at Líyáng, stationed at Yánjīn, [Cuī] Yǎn again remonstrated stating: “Heaven’s Son is at Xǔ, the people hope to assist obedience, it is not as good as defending borders and reporting duties, to bring peace to the realm.” [Yuán] Shào did not listen, and then was defeated at Guāndù. Reaching to [Yuán] Shào’s death, his two sons contested with one another, contested wishing to obtain [Cuī] Yǎn. [Cuī] Yǎn claimed illness and firmly declined, because of this was deemed guilty, secluded in prison, relying on Yīn Kuí and Chén Lín for rescue he was able to avoid punishment.

大將軍袁紹聞而辟之。時士卒橫暴，掘發丘隴，琰諫曰：「昔孫卿有言：『士不素教，甲兵不利，雖湯武不能以戰勝。』今道路暴骨，民未見德，宜敕郡縣掩骼埋胔，示憯怛之愛，追文王之仁。」紹以為騎都尉。後紹治兵黎陽，次于延津，琰復諫曰：「天子在許，民望助順，不如守境述職，以寧區宇。」紹不聽，遂敗于官渡。及紹卒，二子交爭，爭欲得琰。琰稱疾固辭，由是獲罪，幽于囹圄，賴陰夔、陳琳營救得免。

Tàizǔ defeated the Yuán clan, was designated Jì Province Governor, recruited [Cuī] Yǎn as his Separate Carriage Following Official,1 spoke to [Cuī] Yǎn stating: “Yesterday I checked the household registers, it is possible to obtain multitudes of three hundred thousand, indeed this is a large province.” [Cuī] Yǎn replied stating: “Now Heaven’s Under is divided and collapsed, the Nine Provinces torn apart, the two Yuán brothers personally sought shields and spears, Jì region’s masses left as exposed bones in the wilderness. We have not yet heard the Royal Brigades benevolent sound ahead on the road, inquiring after social customs, rescuing from mud and ash, but instead you calculate the armored troops, only taking this as foremost, how is this what our humble province’s servicemen and women hope for from Your Enlightened Excellency?” Tàizǔ changed expression and apologized to him. At the time the guest retainers all were subdued and lost color.

太祖破袁氏，領冀州牧，辟琰為別駕從事，謂琰曰：「昨案戶籍，可得三十萬眾，故為大州也。」琰對曰：「今天下分崩，九州幅裂，二袁兄弟親尋干戈，冀方蒸庶暴骨原野。未聞王師仁聲先路，存問風俗，救其塗炭，而校計甲兵，唯此為先，斯豈鄙州士女所望於明公哉！」太祖改容謝之。于時賓客皆伏失色。

Tàizǔ campaigned against Bìng Province, leaving [Cuī] Yǎn to tutor Emperor Wén at Yè. The Heir Presumptive frequently went out hunting, changing clothes and carriage, his will on charging and chasing. [Cuī] Yǎn’s letter remonstrating stated: “Overall I have heard that indulging in roaming the fields is what the Shū warned against, Lǔ Yǐn watched fish and the Chūnqiū ridiculed him, these are the regulatory words of Zhōu and Kǒng, the clear meanings of the two classics. Yīn reflected on the Xià Lord, the Shī praised them as not far off. On Zǐ and Mǎo no music,2 ritual believed it scrupulous, these also are the successes and failures of the recent, it cannot be not deeply investigated. The Yuán clan was wealthy and strong, sons of nobility lenient and unrestrained, indulged in roaming and nourished extravagance, righteous sound was not heard, wise men and noble men soon had intention to change expression, bear-like strong warriors degenerated to tools for devouring, assuredly this is how, despite embracing followers of a million and striding and possessing the Hé’s north, they had nowhere to place their feet. Now the country is desolate and distressed, kindness and prosperity not yet spread, servicemen and women stand toe to heel, what they long for is virtue. Moreover his Excellency personally drives war horses, above and below labor and toil, the Heir Presumptive should observe the great way, cautious in enacting the upright, thinking of high strategies for managing the state, inside reflect on nearby admonishments, outside raise up far-reaching integrity, deeply ponder being heir and aide, treating your body as a treasure. Yet you rustically wear the lowly clothes of a border guard, recklessly gallop across dangerous terrain, will on the small amusements of pheasants and hares, forgetting the heaviness of the State Altars, this truly is why those with knowledge are sorrowful in heart. May the Heir Presumptive burn screens and discard tunic, to block disappointment by the masses, and not cause Your Old Servant to be deemed guilty by Heaven.” The Heir Presumptive’s reply stated: “Yesterday accepting your commendable mandate, kindly revealing elegant charges, wishing to have me burn screens and discard tunic, the screens are already destroyed, tunic also removed. Later if there are any comparable to this, may I be again immersed in your instructions.”

太祖征并州，留琰傅文帝於鄴。世子仍出田獵，變易服乘，志在驅逐。琰書諫曰：「蓋聞盤于游田，書之所戒，魯隱觀魚，春秋譏之，此周、孔之格言，二經之明義。殷鑒夏后，詩稱不遠，子卯不樂，禮以為忌，此又近者之得失，不可不深察也。袁族富彊，公子寬放，盤游滋侈，義聲不聞，哲人君子，俄有色斯之志，熊羆壯士，墮於吞噬之用，固所以擁徒百萬，跨有河朔，無所容足也。今邦國殄瘁，惠康未洽，士女企踵，所思者德。況公親御戎馬，上下勞慘，世子宜遵大路，慎以行正，思經國之高略，內鑒近戒，外揚遠節，深惟儲副，以身為寶。而猥襲虞旅之賤服，忽馳騖而陵險，志雉兔之小娛，忘社稷之為重，斯誠有識所以惻心也。唯世子燔翳捐褶，以塞眾望，不令老臣獲罪於天。」世子報曰：「昨奉嘉命，惠示雅數，欲使燔翳捐褶，翳已壞矣，褶亦去焉。後有此比，蒙復誨諸。」

When Tàizǔ became Assistant Chancellor, [Cuī] Yǎn again was East and West Department Assistant and Associate in Recruitment Affairs. Previously at the time of being appointed to the East Department, the instructions said: “You sir have the air of Bó Yí, the directness of Shǐ Yú, greedy men admire your reputation and become pure, brave servicemen esteem your praise and become sharpened, this is ability to lead the times already. Therefore I appoint you to the East Department, go and fulfill its duties.” When Wèi state was first established, appointed a Secretariat Writer. At the time an Heir-Apparent was not yet established, the Marquess of Línzī [Cáo] Zhí had talent and favor. Tàizǔ was hesitant, and used a sealed order to secretly consult the exterior. Only [Cuī] Yǎn on open placard answered stating: “Overall I have heard that in the principles of the Chūnqiū, establish the elder son, moreover the Five Office General is benevolent and filial, acute and intelligent, it is appropriate to follow the standard succession. [I, Cuī] Yǎn to the death maintain this.” [Cáo] Zhí was [Cuī] Yǎn’s elder brother’s daughter’s husband. Tàizǔ valued his public-mindedness and brightness, sighed deeply, [1] promoted to Central Commandant.

太祖為丞相，琰復為東西曹掾屬徵事。初授東曹時，教曰：「君有伯夷之風，史魚之直，貪夫慕名而清，壯士尚稱而厲，斯可以率時者已。故授東曹，往踐厥職。」魏國初建，拜尚書。時未立太子，臨菑侯植有才而愛。太祖狐疑，以函令密訪於外。唯琰露板答曰：「蓋聞春秋之義，立子以長，加五官將仁孝聰明，宜承正統。琰以死守之。」植，琰之兄女婿也。太祖貴其公亮，喟然歎息，〔一〕遷中尉。

[1] Shìyǔ states: [Cáo] Zhí’s wife wore embroidery, Tàizǔ ascended the terrace and saw her, believed it violation of regulation commands, sent her home and bestowed death.

〔一〕　世語曰：植妻衣繡，太祖登臺見之，以違制命，還家賜死。

[Cūi] Yǎn in voice and appearance was lofty and fluent, eyebrows and eyes sparse and clear, beard length four chǐ, extremely having majesty and weightiness, the Court servicemen looked to him, and Tàizǔ also respected and feared him. [2] [Cuī] Yǎn once recommended Jùlù’s Yáng Xùn, although his talents and goodness were insufficient, yet he was pure and steadfast and maintained the way, Tàizǔ at once with ritual recruited him. Later when Tàizǔ became King of Wèi, [Yáng] Xùn, published a memorial praising his achievements and merits, commending and relating his splendid virtues, at the time of people some laughed at [Yáng] Xùn as pandering to the world and superficial and false, saying that [Cuī] Yǎn had been mistaken in his recommendation. [Cuī] Yǎn from [Yáng] Xùn requested a draft of the memorial and read it, his letter to [Yáng] Xùn said: “Examining the memorial, the matter is merely good. In time, in time, there will be changing times.” [Cuī] Yǎn’s original meaning was to ridicule the commentators that enjoyed censuring and scolding but did not seek circumstance or reason. Someone reported that [Cuī] Yǎn in this letter disparaged the world and was resentful and slanderous, Tàizǔ angrily said: “Prover says ‘Merely begat a daughter.’ ‘Merely’ is not a statement of ‘good.’ ‘There will be changing times,’ the intention is to be insubordinate.” Thereupon punished [Cuī] Yǎn to be a labor slave, sent someone to observe him, his statements and expression were unyielding. Tàizǔ’s Order said: “Although [Cuī] Yǎn met with punishment, yet he communicates with guest retainers, his gates like a marketplace, in replying to guest retainers he curls his whiskers and looks directly, as if he has resentment.” Therefore bestowed [Cuī] Yǎn death.3 [3]

琰聲姿高暢，眉目疏朗，鬚長四尺，甚有威重，朝士瞻望，而太祖亦敬憚焉。〔一〕琰嘗薦鉅鹿楊訓，雖才好不足，而清貞守道，太祖即禮辟之。後太祖為魏王，訓發表稱贊功伐，褒述盛德。時人或笑訓希世浮偽，謂琰為失所舉。琰從訓取表草視之，與訓書曰：「省表，事佳耳！時乎時乎，會當有變時。」琰本意譏論者好譴呵而不尋情理也。有白琰此書傲世怨謗者，太祖怒曰：「諺言『生女耳』，『耳』非佳語。『會當有變時』，意指不遜。」於是罰琰為徒隸，使人視之，辭色不撓。太祖令曰：「琰雖見刑，而通賓客，門若市人，對賓客虯鬚直視，若有所瞋。」遂賜琰死。〔二〕

[2] Xiānxián Xíngzhuàng states: [Cuī] Yǎn was pure and loyal, lofty and bright, refined in insight, far-reaching in management, pushed forward the fair and direct in the way, stern expression at Court. In the Wèi dynasty’s beginning years, he was entrusted with responsibilities of measuring talent, unified and standardized pure discussion, for ten and more years. Civil and military numerous talents, may were identified and selected by him. The Court submitted to his loftiness, Heaven’s Under praised him as fair.

〔一〕　先賢行狀曰：琰清忠高亮，雅識經遠，推方直道，正色於朝。魏氏初載，委授銓衡，總齊清議，十有餘年。文武群才，多所明拔。朝廷歸高，天下稱平。

[3] Wèi lüè states: Someone obtained [Cuī] Yǎn’s letter, used it to wrap their headscarf covering, walked in the roads of the capital. At the time there was someone who was formerly not at peace with [Cuī] Yǎn, from afar saw [Cuī] Yǎn’s name written on the headscarf covering, followed and read it, and therefore reported it. Tàizǔ believed [Cuī] Yǎn had hidden criticism and heartfelt slander, and so arrested and sent him to prison, shaving punishment and sent to labor. The one who had previously reported [Cuī] Yǎn also again reported him saying: “[Cuī] Yǎn as a laborer curled whiskers and looked directly, his heart appearing to not be at peace.” At the time Tàizǔ also believed it was so, and therefore wished to kill him. Thus he sent a pure and public-minded great clerk to go manage [Cuī] Yǎn, the orders on the clerk stating: “Report due in three days.” [Cuī] Yǎn did not understand. After several days, the clerk therefore reported [Cuī] Yǎn was at peace and calm. His Excellency was furious and said: “Does Cuī Yǎn certainly wish to force me to use blade and saw?” The clerk had these instructions reported to [Cuī] Yǎn, [Cuī] Yǎn apologized to the clerk stating: “I was extremely inappropriate, not realizing that his Excellency’s intentions had reached to this.” Therefore he killed himself.

〔二〕　魏略曰：人得琰書，以裹幘籠，行都道中。時有與琰宿不平者，遙見琰名著幘籠，從而視之，遂白之。太祖以為琰腹誹心謗，乃收付獄，髡刑輸徒。前所白琰者又復白之云：「琰為徒，虯鬚直視，心似不平。」時太祖亦以為然，遂欲殺之。乃使清公大吏往經營琰，敕吏曰：「三日期消息。」琰不悟，後數日，吏故白琰平安。公忿然曰：「崔琰必欲使孤行刀鋸乎！」吏以是教告琰，琰謝吏曰：「我殊不宜，不知公意至此也！」遂自殺。

Previpously [Cuī] Yǎn and Sīmǎ Lǎng were friends, Jìn Emperor Xuān [Sīmǎ Yì] was just in his prime, [Cuī] Yǎn spoke to [Sīmǎ] Lǎng stating: “Your younger brother is acute and wise, enlightened and thorough, firm and decisive, outstanding and towering, probably not what you can reach.” [4] [Sīmǎ] Lǎng believed it was not so, but [Cuī] Yǎn always maintained this opinion. [Cuī] Yǎn’s younger cousin [Cuī] Lín in youth was without fame, even of his in-laws and clan many yet thought little of him, but [Cuī] Yǎn always said: “This is what is called ‘a large vessel is late to be completed.’ In the end he will certainly reach far.” When Zhuō prefecture’s Sūn Lǐ and Lú Yù first entered the army offices, [Cuī] Yǎn also named them stating: “Sūn is clear and bright, fervent and fierce, resolute and concise, capable of decisevness. Lú is pure and alert, clear in reason, smelted a hundred times without dissolving. Both are talents to be Excellencies.” Later [Cuī] Lín, [Sūn] Lǐ, [Lú] Yù all reached the Three Excellencies.4 When [Cuī] Yǎn’s friends Gōngsūn Fāng and Sòng Jiē early died, [Cuī] Yǎn supported their orphans, kindness like to his own children. His reflection on insight and sincerity in righteousness was all like this. [5]

始琰與司馬朗善，晉宣王方壯，琰謂朗曰：「子之弟，聰哲明允，剛斷英跱，殆非子之所及也。」〔一〕朗以為不然，而琰每秉此論。琰從弟林，少無名望，雖姻族猶多輕之，而琰常曰：「此所謂大器晚成者也，終必遠至。」涿郡孫禮、盧毓始入軍府，琰又名之曰：「孫疏亮亢烈，剛簡能斷，盧清警明理，百鍊不消，皆公才也。」後林、禮、毓咸至鼎輔。及琰友人公孫方、宋階早卒，琰撫其遺孤，恩若己子。其鑒識篤義，類皆如此。〔二〕

[4] Your Servant Sōngzhī notes: “Towering” is sometimes written “special.” I humbly say “outstanding and special” is correct.

〔一〕　臣松之案：「跱」或作「特」，竊謂「英特」為是也。

Máo Jiè

Máo Jiè appellation Xiàoxiān was a Chénliú Píngqiū man. In youth he was a county clerk, for purity and public-mindedness praised. About to flee the chaos to Jīng Province, he had not yet arrived, heard Liú Biǎo’s government and orders were not enlightened, and therefore went to Lǔyáng. Tàizǔ oversaw Yǎn Province, recruited him as Manager Central Following Official. [Máo] Jiè spoke to Tàizǔ stating: “Now Heaven’s Under is divided and collapsed, the state’s ruler moved and shifted, the living people abandon their work, hungry and in famine wandering in exile, the public purse without reserves to last the year, the Hundred Surnames without the will for calm and stability, difficult to last for long. Now Yuán Shào and Liú Biǎo though have servicemen and civilians numerous and strong, both are without thorough far-reaching considerations, and have not planted foundations and established roots.
