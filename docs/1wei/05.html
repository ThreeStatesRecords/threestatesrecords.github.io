5  Biographies of Empresses and Consorts

Preface

The Yì1 states: “The Male correct position is the exterior, the Female correct position is the interior. Male and Female correct, is the Great Principle of Heaven and Earth.” Of the ancient former wise kings, none did not understand the regulation of Empresses and Consorts, follow the virtues of Heaven and Earth, therefore when the Two Consorts married to Guī, the Way of Yú completed prosperity, when Rén and Sì matched to Jī, the Zhōu House then flourished. Fall and Rise, Survival and Destruction, constantly are by this. The Chūnqiū explanations say Heaven’s Son has twelve women, vassal lords nine women, examining this by sense and reason, it is an unchanging standard. But the last generations [of a dynasty] are extravagant and indulgant, unrestrained in their excessive desires, reaching to causing male and female to be resentful and unmarried, emotionally disturbing harmonious aura, only attractiveness is esteemed, not rooted in beautiful virtue, therefore moral teachings decline and the great [social] network is ruined and destroyed. Is it not lamentable? Alas, for those with state and with family, this can be an eternal reflection!

易稱「男正位乎外，女正位乎內；男女正，天地之大義也」。古先哲王，莫不明后妃之制，順天地之德，故二妃嬪媯，虞道克隆，任、姒配姬，周室用熙，廢興存亡，恆此之由。春秋說云天子十二女，諸侯九女，考之情理，不易之典也。而末世奢縱，肆其侈欲，至使男女怨曠，感動和氣，惟色是崇，不本淑懿，故風教陵遲而大綱毀泯，豈不惜哉！嗚呼，有國有家者，其可以永鑒矣！

In the Hàn system, the Emperor’s paternal grandmother is called Grand Imperial Dowager Empress, the Emperor’s mother called Imperial Dowager Empress, the Emperor’s consort called Imperial Empress, the other inner offices [the harem] ten and four ranks. Wèi followed the Hàn laws, the titles of the mothers and Empresses, all were as the old system. From the Lady (fūrén) on down, the generations had additions and reductions. Tàizǔ established the state, first mandating the Royal Queen, those below were five ranks: having Lady (fūrén), having Zhāoyí, having Jiéyú, having Rónghuá, having Měirén. Emperor Wén added Guìpín, Shūyuán, Xiūróng, Shùnchéng, Liángrén. Emperor Míng added Shūfēi, Zhāohuá, Xiūyí; abolished the Shùnchéng office. During Tàihé [227 - 232] first restored the Mìng Fūrén, raised its position to above Shūfēi. From Fūrén on down the nobility was altogether twelve ranks: Guìpín and Fūrén positions were after the Imperial Empress, nobility without a match. Shūfēi position matched the Chancellor of State, nobility comparable to the vassal lords and kings. Shūyuán position matched the Censorate Grandee, nobility comparable to county Duke. Zhāoyí comparable to county Marquess. Zhāohuá comparable to village Marquess. Xiūróng comparable to precinct Marquess. Xiūyí comparable to Within the Passes Marquess. Jiéyú matched to Central Two Thousand Dàn. Rónghuá matched to True Two Thousand Dàn. Měirén matched to Comparable Two Thousand Dàn. Liángrén matched to Thousand Dàn.

漢制，帝祖母曰太皇太后，帝母曰皇太后，帝妃曰皇后，其餘內官十有四等。魏因漢法，母后之號，皆如舊制，自夫人以下，世有增損。太祖建國，始命王后，其下五等：有夫人，有昭儀，有婕妤，有容華，有美人。文帝增貴嬪、淑媛、脩容、順成、良人。明帝增淑妃、昭華、脩儀；除順成官。太和中始復命夫人，登其位於淑妃之上。自夫人以下爵凡十二等：貴嬪、夫人，位次皇后，爵無所視；淑妃位視相國，爵比諸侯王；淑媛位視御史大夫，爵比縣公；昭儀比縣侯；昭華比鄉侯；脩容比亭侯；脩儀比關內侯；婕妤視中二千石；容華視真二千石；美人視比二千石；良人視千石。

Wǔ-Xuān Imperial Empress Biàn

Wǔ-Xuān2 Imperial Empress Biàn was a Lángyé Kāiyáng woman, Emperor Wén’s mother. Originally a performer,3 [1] aged twenty, Tàizǔ at Qiáo accepted the Empress4 as a concubine. The Empress accompanied Tàizǔ to Luò. Reaching to Dǒng Zhuó making chaos, Tàizǔ [disguised] in humble clothing went out east to escape trouble. Yuán Shù spread rumors of Tàizǔ’s misfortune, at the time Tàizǔ’s Left and Right that had gone to Luò all wished to return home, the Empress stopped them saying: “Lord Cáo’s fortune or misfortune cannot yet be known, if today you return home, tomorrow if he survives, how would you have to face him again? Even if disaster arrives, dying together is what bitterness?” Therefore they followed the Empress’s words. Tàizǔ heard and praised this. At the beginning of Jiàn’ān (196 - 220), lady Dīng was deposed, and therefore had the Empress be the successor wife. The various sons that were without mothers, Tàizǔ all had the Empress raise them. [2] When Emperor Wén became Heir-Apparent, the Left and Right and Chief Maid5 congratulated the Empress saying: “The General6 is appointed Heir-Apparent, Under Heaven none are not joyous, [You, The] Queen should pour out the treasury to bestow rewards.” The Empress said: “The King naturally because [Cáo] Pī’s age is largest therefore emmployed him as successor. I should only consider avoiding the fault of not instructing and guiding him to be good fortune, also why should there be heavy bestowments for this?” The Chief Maid returned and provided her words to Tàizǔ. Tàizǔ was pleasued and said: “In anger to not change expression, in joy to not lose moral restraint, indeed this is the most difficut.”

武宣卞皇后，瑯邪開陽人，文帝母也。本倡家，〔一〕年二十，太祖於譙納后為妾。後隨太祖至洛。及董卓為亂，太祖微服東出避難。袁術傳太祖凶問，時太祖左右至洛者皆欲歸，后止之曰：「曹君吉凶未可知，今日還家，明日若在，何面目復相見也？正使禍至，共死何苦！」遂從后言。太祖聞而善之。建安初，丁夫人廢，遂以后為繼室。諸子無母者，太祖皆令后養之。〔二〕文帝為太子，左右長御賀后曰：「將軍拜太子，天下莫不歡喜，后當傾府藏賞賜。」后曰：「王自以丕年大，故用為嗣，我但當以免無教導之過為幸耳，亦何為當重賜遺乎！」長御還，具以語太祖。太祖悅曰：「怒不變容，喜不失節，故是最為難。」

[1] Wèi shū states: The Empress in Hàn’s Yánxī Third Year Twelfth Moon Jǐsì [159 Dec 30] was born in Qí prefecture’s Báitíng, having a yellow aura fill the room all day. Her father Marquess Jìng7 was bewildered by it, and asked a diviner Wáng Dàn. Dàn said: “This is an auspicious omen.”

〔一〕　魏書曰：后以漢延熹三年十二月己巳生齊郡白亭，有黃氣滿室移日。父敬侯怪之，以問卜者王旦，旦曰：「此吉祥也。」

[2] Wèi lüè states: Tàizǔ first had lady Dīng, and also lady Liú bore [Cáo Áng] Zǐxiū and the eldest Princess of Qīnghé. Liú early died, Dīng adopted Zǐxiū. Zǐxiū died at Ráng, Dīng always said: “You killed my son, and completely no longer think of him!” Therefore she wept without restraint. Tàizǔ was angered by this, sent her back to her family, desiring to break her will. Later Tàizǔ arrived to meet her, the lady was just weaving, someone outside passed word: “His Excellency arrives.” The lady sat at her loom as before. Tàizǔ arrived, caressed her back saying: “Will you look back at me and ride the carriage together to return home?” The lady did not look back, and also did not answer. Tàizǔ backed away, stood outside the door, and further said: “Is it impossible then?” Still she did not answer. Tàizǔ said: “It is really farewell.” Therefore they were divorced, and he wished for her family to remarry her off, her family did not dare. Previously, lady Dīng had been the primary wife and furthermore had Zǐxiū, Dīng treated the Empress, mother and her children, as beneath her. When the Empress became the successor wife, she did not recall old grievances, took advantage of when Tàizǔ was out on campaign, to regularly in the four seasons send people with gifts, and also privately invited her, having her [Dīng] in the main seat and herself [Biàn] below her, welcoming her coming and escorting her going, like in the old days. Dīng apologized saying: “For a deposed and exiled woman [like me], how can [you, my] lady be able to always be like this?” Later when Dīng died, the Empress requested Tàizǔ bury her, he permitted it, and so she was buried to Xǔ city wall’s south. Later when Tàizǔ was seriously ill, he himself realized he would not recover, and sighed and said: “In my beginning to end conduct and intentions, in my heart there has never been regrets. But supposing if the dead have awareness, if Zǐxiū asks”Where is my mother,” what statement would I have to answer?”

Wèi shū states: The Empress by nature was temperate and frugal, not esteeming the flowery and magnificent, had no patterned embroidery or pearls and jade, her utensils all were black lacquer. Tàizǔ once obtained famed earrings of several sets, and mandated the Empress herself choose one set. The Empress chose a middle one. Tàizǔ asked the reason, she replied saying: “Taking the highest is greed, taking the lowest is hypocrisy, therefore I chose the middle.”

〔二〕　魏略曰：太祖始有丁夫人，又劉夫人生子脩及清河長公主。劉早終，丁養子脩。子脩亡於穰，丁常言：「將我兒殺之，都不復念！」遂哭泣無節。太祖忿之，遣歸家，欲其意折。後太祖就見之，夫人方織，外人傳云「公至」，夫人踞機如故。太祖到，撫其背曰：「顧我共載歸乎！」夫人不顧，又不應。太祖卻行，立于戶外，復云：「得無尚可邪！」遂不應，太祖曰：「真訣矣。」遂與絕，欲其家嫁之，其家不敢。初，丁夫人既為嫡，加有子脩，丁視后母子不足。后為繼室，不念舊惡，因太祖出行，常四時使人饋遺，又私迎之，延以正坐而己下之，迎來送去，有如昔日。丁謝曰：「廢放之人，夫人何能常爾邪！」其後丁亡，后請太祖殯葬，許之，乃葬許城南。後太祖病困，自慮不起，歎曰：「我前後行意，於心未曾有所負也。假令死而有靈，子脩若問『我母所在』，我將何辭以答！」魏書曰：后性約儉，不尚華麗，無文繡珠玉，器皆黑漆。太祖常得名璫數具，命后自選一具，后取其中者，太祖問其故，對曰：「取其上者為貪，取其下者為偽，故取其中者。」

Twenty-Fourth Year [219], appointed as Royal Queen, the conferment said: “Fūrén Biàn-shì fostered and raised the various sons, having the virtue of maternal ceremony. Now advance in rank to Royal Queen, the Heir-Apparent and vassal lords accompany in position, the various ministers send up longevity wishes, reduce within [Wèi] state death penalties by one degree.” Twenty-Fifth Year [220], Tàizǔ passed away, Emperor Wén ascended the King’s position, honored the Empress as Royal Dowager Queen, reaching to the ascension of the [Imperial] throne, honored the Empress as Imperial Dowager Empress, calleas Yǒngshòu Palace. [3] Emperor Míng succeeded the position, honored the Dowager Empress as Grand Imperial Dowager Empress.

二十四年，拜為王后，策曰：「夫人卞氏，撫養諸子，有母儀之德。今進位王后，太子諸侯陪位，群卿上壽，減國內死罪一等。」二十五年，太祖崩，文帝即王位，尊后曰王太后，及踐阼，尊后曰皇太后，稱永壽宮。〔一〕明帝即位，尊太后曰太皇太后。

[3] Wèi shū states: The Empress because state expenditures were not enough, abolished and reduced her imperial meals, the various gold and silver utensils were all removed. The King of Dōng’ē [Cáo] Zhí was the Dowager Empress’s youngest son and the most favored. Later [Cáo] Zhí violated law, was memorialized by relevant managers, Emperor Wén ordered the Dowager Empress’s younger brother’s son Serving Chariots Commandant [Biàn] Lán to take the discussions of the Excellencies and Ministers to report to the Dowager Empress. The Dowager Empress said: “Unexpectedly this boy had behavior like this, you return and tell the Emperor, that he cannot ruin the state’s laws because of me.” Reaching to personally meeting the Emperor, she did not speak of it.

Your Servant Sōngzhī notes: Emperor Wén dreamed of rubbing a coin, wishing to have the inscription removed but it became all the more clear and asked Zhōu Xuān about it. Xuān replied saying: “This is Your Majesty’s family affairs, though your intentions wish for it, yet the Dowager Empress does not listen.” Then the Dowager Empress employed her intentions, and could not be as what this book says.

Wèi shū also states: Every time the Dowager Empress accompanied the army on campaign, when seeing the highly aged and white headed, at once she stopped her chariot to call and inquire, bestowing on them fabric and silk, facing them shedding tears and saying: “I regret my father and mother did not reach our time.” Every time the Dowager Empress met the [Cáo’s] in-law relations [her Biàn family], she was not accommodating in countenance, always saying: “Your residences should be frugal in affairs. You should not expect rewards and bestowments or think of self indulgences. The in-law families are bewildered that my treatment as too meager. It is because I have my own constant standards. I served Emperor Wǔ for forty to fifty years, acted frugally for a long time, and cannot change myself to extravagance. If there are any violations of laws and prohibitions, I would only be able to increase the punishment by one degree. Do not expect cash or rice or gracious loans.” The Emperor for the Dowager Empress’s younger brother [Biàn] Bǐng raised a mansion, the mansion was completed, the Dowgaer Empress visited the mansion and invited the various families of the [Cáo’s] in-law relations, set an inferior kitchen, with no exceptional delicacies. The Dowager Empress’s Left and Right ate vegetables and millet, with no fish or meat. Her frugality was like this.

〔一〕　魏書曰：后以國用不足，滅損御食，諸金銀器物皆去之。東阿王植，太后少子，最愛之。後植犯法，為有司所奏，文帝令太后弟子奉車都尉蘭持公卿議白太后，太后曰：「不意此兒所作如是，汝還語帝，不可以我故壞國法。」及自見帝，不以為言。臣松之案：文帝夢磨錢，欲使文滅而更愈明，以問周宣。宣答曰：「此陛下家事，雖意欲爾，而太后不聽。」則太后用意，不得如此書所言也。魏書又曰：太后每隨軍征行，見高年白首，輒住車呼問，賜與絹帛，對之涕泣曰：「恨父母不及我時也。」太后每見外親，不假以顏色，常言「居處當務節儉，不當望賞賜，念自佚也。外舍當怪吾遇之太薄，吾自有常度故也。吾事武帝四五十年，行儉日久，不能自變為奢，有犯科禁者，吾且能加罪一等耳，莫望錢米恩貸也。」帝為太后弟秉起第，第成，太后幸第請諸家外親，設下廚，無異膳。太后左右，菜食粟飯，無魚肉。其儉如此。

During Huángchū, Emperor Wén wished to posthumously enfeoff the Dowager Empress’s father and mother. Secretariat Writer Chén Qún memorialized stating: “Your Majesty by sagely virtue answered fate to receive the Mandate, beginning the enterprise and reforming systems, and should forever be a model for posterity. Citing the writings of the canon registers, there is no system of women sharing in territory and mandated nobility. In ritual canon, the wife follows the husband’s noble title. Qín violated the ancient laws, the Hàn clan followed them. It is not the excellent canon of the Former Kings.” The Emperor said: “This comment is corect. Thus do not put into effect. Have the composed and written Edict sent down for storage in the Terrace Pavillions, to forever be a model for posterity.” Reaching Tàihé Fourth Year [230] Spring, Emperor Míng then posthumously titled the Dowager Empress’s paternal grandfather [Biàn] Guǎng as Marquess Gōng of Kāiyáng, father [Biàn] Yuǎn as Marquess Jìng, paternal grandmother Zhōu enfeoffed as Lady of Yángdū along with Marquess Jìng’s consort, all bestowed Seals and Ribbons. That Year Fifth Moon, the Empressed passed away. Seventh Moon, joint burial [with Emperor Wǔ] at Gāolíng.

黃初中，文帝欲追封太后父母，尚書陳群奏曰：「陛下以聖德應運受命，創業革制，當永為後式。案典籍之文，無婦人分土命爵之制。在禮典，婦因夫爵。秦違古法，漢氏因之，非先王之令典也。」帝曰：「此議是也，其勿施行。以作著詔下藏之臺閣，永為後式。」至太和四年春，明帝乃追諡太后祖父廣曰開陽恭侯，父遠曰敬侯，祖母周封陽都君及（恭）〔敬〕侯夫人，皆贈印綬。其年五月，后崩。七月，合葬高陵。

Previously, the Dowager Empress’s younger brother [Biàn] Bǐng for achievements was enfeoffed as a city village Marquess, Huángchū Seventh Year [226] advanced enfeoffment to Marquess of Kāiyáng, fief of one thousand two hundred households, became Shining Illustrious General. [4] When [Biàn] Bǐng died, his son [Biàn] Lán succeeded. As a youth had talent and scholarship, [5] became Serving Chariots Commandant, Roaming Strike General, additionally Cavalier Regular Attendant. when [Biàn] Lán died, his son [Biàn] Huī succeeded. 〔二〕　魏書曰：后上表謝曰：「妾無皇、英釐降之節，又非姜、任思齊之倫，誠不足以假充女君之盛位，處中饋之重任。」后自在東宮，及即尊位，雖有異寵，心愈恭肅，供養永壽宮，以孝聞。是時柴貴人亦有寵，后教訓獎導之。後宮諸貴人時有過失，常彌覆之，有譴讓，輒為帝言其本末，帝或大有所怒，至為之頓首請罪，是以六宮無怨。性儉約，不好音樂，常慕漢明德馬后之為人。

The Empress early lost her elder and younger brothers, had her elder cousin [Guō] Biǎo succeed as [Guō] Yǒng’s posterity, appointed Serving Chariots Commandant. The Empress’s maternal relative Liú Fěi with another state was bethrothed, the Empress heard of this, her orders said: “The various relatives in marriages should be with equal matches with families of their hometowns, and should not take advantage of their power to force marriages with people of other regions.” The Empress’s elder sister’s son Mèng Wǔ to his hometown to seek a lesser wife [concubine], the Empress stopped him. Therefore her orders to the various families said: “In the present age women are few, and should be matched with officers and soldiers. Do not take advantage of status to take them as concubines. You should each be self-cautious, and not be the first to incur punishments. [15]

后早喪兄弟，以從兄表繼永後，拜奉車都尉。后外親劉斐與他國為婚，后聞之，敕曰：「諸親戚嫁娶，自當與鄉里門戶匹敵者，不得因勢，彊與他方人婚也。」后姊子孟武還鄉里，求小妻，后止之。遂敕諸家曰：「今世婦女少，當配將士，不得因緣取以為妾也。宜各自慎，無為罰首。」〔一〕

[15] Wèi shū states: The Empress always admonished and warned [Guō] Biǎo and [Mèng] Wǔ and others stating: “The Hàn clan’s [in-law] families of the Pepper Rooms [Jiāofáng ”Pepper Rooms” was the name of a Hall in the Palace of the Empress during Western Hàn, reportedly from a practice of using a fragrant pepper paste to paint the walls. It became a term to refer to the Empresses and Consorts in general.] few were able to preserve themselves, all because of arrogance and extravagance.38 Can you not be cautious?”

〔一〕　魏書曰：后常敕戒表、武等曰：「漢氏椒房之家，少能自全者，皆由驕奢，可不慎乎！」

Fifth Year [224], the Emperor went on eastern campaign, the Empress remained at Xǔchāng’s Yǒngshǐ Terrace. At the time it continuously rained for over a hundred days, of the city walls and towers many were damaged, relevant managers memorialized to request she relocate her residence. The Empress said: “In the past Chǔ King Zhāo went out on tour, Zhēn Jiāng remained at Jiān Terrace, the Jiāng’s [flood] waters arrived, the envoy invited her [to relocate] but was without [the authentication] talismen, she did not leave, and in the end drowned. Now the Emperor is far away, I fortunately do not yet have such a crisis, but to yet relocate residence, how can it be?” Of the various ministers none dared again speak. Sixth Year [225], the Emperor went on eastern campaign against Wú, reached Guǎnglíng the Empress remained at Qiáo Palace. At the time [Guō] Biǎo remained in the Residential Guard, and wished to restrain the waters to catch fish. The Empress said: “The water should be connected for transport canals, and also there is few timber and wood, if the slaves and retainers are not kept before your eyes, they will further privately steal government bamboo and wood to build bridges and dams. Now what the Serving Chariots [Commandant] lacks, how is it fish?”

五年，帝東征，后留許昌永始臺。時霖雨百餘日，城樓多壞，有司奏請移止。后曰：「昔楚昭王出游，貞姜留漸臺，江水至，使者迎而無符，不去，卒沒。今帝在遠，吾幸未有是患，而便移止，奈何？」群臣莫敢復言。六年，帝東征吳，至廣陵，后留譙宮。時表留宿衛，欲遏水取魚。后曰：「水當通運漕，又少材木，奴客不在目前，當復私取官竹木作梁遏。今奉車所不足者，豈魚乎？」

When Emperor Míng succeeded the throne, honored the Empress as Imperial Dowager Empress, called as Yǒng’ān Palace. Tàihé Fourth Year [230], Edict enfeoffed [Guō] Biǎo as Marquess of Ānyáng precinct, also advanced the noble title to Marquess of a village, increased estate adding to the previous [to total] five hundred households, promoted to Central Ramparts General. Appointed [Guō] Biǎo’s son [Guō] Xiáng as Cavalry Commandant. That Year, the Emperor posthumously titled the Dowager Empress’s father [Guō] Yǒng as Marquess Jìng of Ānyáng village, mother Dǒng as Lady of a city village. Promoted [Guō] Biǎo as Shining Virtue General, applied gold [seal] and purple [ribbon], position as Specially Advanced. [Guō] Biǎo’s second son [Guō] Xùn became a Cavalry Commandant. When Mèng Wǔ’s mother died, he wished to lavishly bury and raise a sacrifice hall, the Dowager Empress stopped him stating: “From the decline and chaos onward, of graves and tombs none were not excavated, all because of lavish burials. Shǒuyánglíng can be the standard.”39 Qīnglóng Third Year [235] Spring, the Empress passed away at Xǔchāng, following [Cáo Pī’s] Final Regulations built the tomb, Third Moon Gēngyín [235 Apr 16], buried at Shǒuyánglíng’s west. [16] The Emperor advanced [Guō] Biǎo’s noble title to Marquess of Guànjīn,40 increased estate by five hundred, adding to the previous [to total] one thousand households. Promoted [Guō] Xiáng as Attached Horse Commandant. Fourth Year [236], posthumously changed the enfeoffment of [Guō] Yǒng to Marquess Jìng of Guànjīn, his wife Dǒng to be Lady of Tángyáng. Posthumously enfeoffed and titled the Empress’s elder brother [Guō] Fú as Marquess Dài of Liánglǐ precinct,41 [Guō] Dū as Marquess Xiào of Wǔchéng preinct,42 [Guō] Chéng as Marquess Dìng of Xīnlè precinct,43 all had sent envoys to present the conferments, sacrifices with Grand Offerings. When [Guō] Biǎo died, his son [Guō] Xiáng succeeded, also divided [Guō] Biǎo’s noble title to enfeoff [Guō] Xiáng’s younger brother [Guō] Shù as a Ranked Marquess. When [Guō] Xiáng died, his son [Guō] Zhāo succeeded.

明帝即位，尊后為皇太后，稱永安宮。太和四年，詔封表安陽亭侯，又進爵鄉侯，增邑并前五百戶，遷中壘將軍。以表子詳為騎都尉。其年，帝追諡太后父永為安陽鄉敬侯，母董為都鄉君。遷表昭德將軍，加金紫，位特進，表第二子訓為騎都尉。及孟武母卒，欲厚葬，起祠堂，太后止之曰：「自喪亂以來，墳墓無不發掘，皆由厚葬也；首陽陵可以為法。」青龍三年春，后崩于許昌，以終制營陵，三月庚寅，葬首陽陵西。〔一〕帝進表爵為觀津侯，增邑五百，并前千戶。遷詳為駙馬都尉。四年，追改封永為觀津敬侯，世婦董為堂陽君。追封諡后兄浮為梁里亭戴侯，都為武城亭孝侯，成為新樂亭定侯，皆使使者奉策，祠以太牢。表薨，子詳嗣，又分表爵封詳弟述為列侯。詳薨，子釗嗣。

[16] Wèi lüè states: When Emperor Míng succeeded the throne, he recalled the pain of Empress Zhēn’s death, and therefore the Dowager Empress in anxiety suddenly passed away. When Empress Zhēn faced death, she had the Emperor entrusted to Madame Lǐ. When the Dowager Empress passed away, the madame then explained the disaster of Empress Zhēn meeting with slander, not obtaining the main shroud, disheveled hair covering the face. The Emperor in grief and resentment shed tears, and mandated that the encoffining and burial of the Dowager Empress would all be by the precedent of Empress Zhēn.

Hàn Jìn Chūnqiū states: Previously, the execution of Empress Zhēn was because of the favor for Empress Guō. At the encoffining, it was ordered to have the disheveled hair cover the face, with chaff stuffing the mouth, and therefore established Empress Guō, having her raise Emperor Míng. The Emperor knew of it, his heart always harbored anger, repeatedly he sobbed and asked of the circumstances of Empress Zhēn’s death. Empress Guō said: “The Former Emperor himself killed her, why blame and question me? Moreover you as a son of others, can you have posthumous enmity for your deceased father, or for your previous mother unjustly kill your later mother?” Emperor Míng was angry, and therefore oppressed her to death, ordering the encoffining to be by the precedent of Empress Zhēn.

Wèi shū records the Mourning Conferment stated: “On Qīnglóng Third Year Third Moon Rénshēn,44 the Imperial Dowager Empress’s catalpa palace45 was opened for the encoffining, about to be buried at Shǒuyáng’s West Tomb. The Grieving Son Imperial Emperor [I, Cáo] Ruì personally presented the texts for the ancestral conveyance,46 and then personally sent the libations, beating chest and jumping [in sorrow], wailing and looking up to complain [to Heaven], pained by the departure of the soul, sorrowful at the apperanace chariot47 heading on the road turning back on the three lights to be hidden away, into the yellow clay and quietly placed. Oh alas! In the past the two daughters were consrts to Yú, the Emperor’s Way by this manifested; the three mothers were wives of Zhōu, sagely excellence all the more glorious, having received many blessings, they enjoyed the state for long. Grieving grieving for loving mother, who raised and cultivated the women’s rooms, dragon flying to violet utmost, joined with the Sagely Imperial, unexpectedly in middle age, suddenly suffering disaster. Pitiable me the small son, desolate desloate broken and wounded, although the soul is forever gone, where can I look for my inquries?48 Oh alas!”

〔一〕　魏略曰：明帝既嗣立，追痛甄后之薨，故太后以憂暴崩。甄后臨沒，以帝屬李夫人。及太后崩，夫人乃說甄后見譖之禍，不獲大斂，被髮覆面，帝哀恨流涕，命殯葬太后，皆如甄后故事。漢晉春秋曰：初，甄后之誅，由郭后之寵，及殯，令被髮覆面，以糠塞口，遂立郭后，使養明帝。帝知之，心常懷忿，數泣問甄后死狀。郭后曰：「先帝自殺，何以責問我？且汝為人子，可追讎死父，為前母枉殺後母邪？」明帝怒，遂逼殺之，敕殯者使如甄后故事。魏書載哀策曰：「維青龍三年三月壬申，皇太后梓宮啟殯，將葬于首陽之西陵。哀子皇帝叡親奉冊祖載，遂親遣奠，叩心擗踊，號咷仰訴，痛靈魂之遷幸，悲容車之向路，背三光以潛翳，就黃壚而安厝。嗚呼哀哉！昔二女妃虞，帝道以彰，三母嬪周，聖善彌光，既多受祉，享國延長。哀哀慈妣，興化閏房，龍飛紫極，作合聖皇，不虞中年，暴罹災殃。愍予小子，煢煢摧傷，魂雖永逝，定省曷望？嗚呼哀哉！」

Míng-Dào Imperial Empress Máo

Míng-Dào Imperial Empress Máo was a Hénèi woman. During Huángchū, she was selected to enter the East Palace, Emperor Míng at the time was King of Píngyuán, she was advanced to service him and had favor, going out and coming in sharing the same carriage. When he succeeded the Emperor’s position, she became a Guìpín. Tàihé Inaugural Year [227], established as Imperial Empress. The Empress’s father [Máo] Jiā was appointed a Cavalry Commandant, the Empress’s younger brother [Máo] Zēng a Cadet Central.

明悼毛皇后，河內人也。黃初中，以選入東宮，明帝時為平原王，進御有寵，出入與同輿輦。及即帝立，以為貴嬪。太和元年，立為皇后。后父嘉，拜騎都尉，后弟曾，郎中。

Previously, when Emperor Míng was King, he first accepted Hénèi’s Yú-shì as a consort. When the Emperor succeeded the throne, Yú-shì was not established as Empress, Grand Imperial Dowager Empress Biàn comforted and consoled her. Yú-shì said: “The Cáo clan naturally enjoys establishing the lowly, never once able to raise by righteousness. However the Empress’s duty is in inner affairs, the Lord deals with outer governance, these principles complement one another for completion. If one is not able to begin with the good, never is there the ability to have a good end. Almost certainly from this will be the destruction of the state and the loss of sacrifices.” Yú-shì therefore was demoted and sent back to Yè Palace. Advanced [Máo] Jiā to be Serving Chariots Commandant, [Máo] Zēng Cavalry Commandant, the favor and bestowments icnreasingly applied. Shortly after this, enfeoffed [Máo] Jiā as Marquess of Bópíng village, promoted to Radiant Grandee, [Máo] Zēng to Attached Horse Commandant. [Máo] Jiā originally managed chariot labor, very suddenly obtained wealth and nobility, Emperor Míng had the Court ministers gather at his house for drinking and feasting, his demeanor and movements were extremely foolish and ignorant, when speaking then he called himself “My Marquess Self,” at the time people laughed at him. [17] Later also applied on [Máo] Jiā position as Specially Advanced, [Máo] Zēng promoted to Cavalier Attendant Cadet. Qīnglóng Third Year [235], [Máo] Jiā died, posthumously titled Radiant Grandee, changed enfeoffment to Marquess of Ānguó, increased estate by five hundred, adding to the previous [to total] one thousand households, posthumous name called Marquess Jié. Fourth Year [236], posthumously enfeoffed the Empress’s mother Xià as Lady of Yěwáng.

初，明帝為王，始納河內虞氏為妃，帝即位，虞氏不得立為后，太皇后卞太后慰勉焉。虞氏曰：「曹氏自好立賤，未有能以義舉者也。然后職內事，君聽外政，其道相由而成，苟不能以善始，未有能令終者也。殆必由此亡國喪祀矣！」虞氏遂絀還鄴宮。進嘉為奉車都尉，曾騎都尉，寵賜隆渥。頃之，封嘉博平鄉侯，遷光祿大夫，曾駙馬都尉。嘉本典虞車工，卒暴富貴，明帝令朝臣會其家飲宴，其容止舉動甚蚩騃，語輒自謂「侯身」，時人以為笑。〔一〕後又加嘉位特進，曾遷散騎侍郎。青龍三年，嘉薨，追贈光祿大夫，改封安國侯，增邑五百，并前千戶，諡曰節侯。四年，追封后母夏為野王君。

[17] Sūn Shèng states: The ancient Kings were certain to seek the good and virtuous to match the raising of utmost virtue, expanding the royal influence to Guānjū,49 conveying pure manner to Línzhǐ.50 Reaching to the end of the Three Dynasties,51 chaos consumed these threads, righteousness was drowned in passions, positions muddled by favoritism, noble and lowly were not distinct, subordinates usurped and replaced superiors, rise and decline and expansion and fall, all are the results. Wèi from Wǔ-wáng [Cáo Cāo] reaching to Lièzǔ [Cáo Ruì], Three Empresses in their ascension all rose from the obscure and lowly. When the roots are from the inferior, how can there be a long reign? The Shī says: ‘Fine cloth or coarse cloth, frigid they are from the wind.’ Is this what it speaks of?

〔一〕　孫盛曰：古之王者，必求令淑以對揚至德，恢王化於關雎，致淳風于麟趾。及臻三季，並亂茲緒，義以情溺，位由寵昏，貴賤無章，下陵上替，興衰隆廢，皆是物也。魏自武王，暨于烈祖，三后之升，起自幽賤，本既卑矣，何以長世？詩云：「絺兮綌兮，淒其以風。」其此之謂乎！

When the Emperor favored the Empress Guō Yuán,52 the Empress’s favor daily weakened. Jǐngchū Inaugural Year [237], the Emperor was touring the Rear Garden, and summoned Cáirén and above to a private feast in utmost revelry. Empress Yuán said: “It is appropriate to invite the Imperial Empress.” The Emperor did not permit it, and so forbade the Left and Right, to have it not announced. The Empress learned of this, the next day, the Emperor met the Empress, the Empress said: “Yesterday’s tour and feast in the North Garden, was it enjoyable?” The Emperor believed the Left and Right had leaked it, those killed were over ten people. Bestowed the Empress with death,53 however yet applied posthumous name, buried at Mǐnlíng. Promoted [Máo] Zēng to Cavalier Regular Attendant, later transferred to Feather Forest Tiger Elite Central Cadet General, Yuánwǔ Manager of Agriculture.

帝之幸郭元后也，后愛寵日弛。景初元年，帝游後園，召才人以上曲宴極樂。元后曰「宜延皇后」，帝弗許。乃禁左右，使不得宣。后知之，明日，帝見后，后曰：「昨日游宴北園，樂乎？」帝以左右泄之，所殺十餘人。賜后死，然猶加諡，葬愍陵。遷曾散騎常侍，後徙為羽林虎賁中郎將、原武典農。

Míng-Yuán Imperial Empress Guō

Míng-Yuán Imperial Empress Guō was a Xīpíng woman, for generations a powerful clan of Héyòu. During Huángchū, her home prefecture rebelled, and therefore she was taken to enter the Palace. When Emperor Míng succeeded the throne, she extremely met with affection and favor, appointed as a Madame. Her father’s younger brother [Guō] Lì became a Cavalry Commandant, cousin’s father [Guō] Zhī became Tiger Elite Central Cadet General. The Emperor’s illness was critical, and therefore she was established as Imperial Empress. When the King of Qí succeeded the throne, honored the Empress as Imperial Dowager Empress, called as Yǒngníng Palace, posthumously enfeoffed and posthumous title on the Dowager Empress’s father [Guō] Mǎn as Marquess Dìng of Xīdū, using [Guō] Lì’s son [Guō] Jiàn to continue the noble rank. Enfeoffed the Dowager Empress’s mother Dù as Lady of Héyáng. [Guō] Zhī was promoted to Cavalier Regular Attendant, Cháng River Colonel, [18] [Guō] Lì to Announcing Virtue General, all enfeoffed as Ranked Marquesses. [Guō] Jiàn’s elder brother [Guō] Dé was sent out to be adopted by the Zhēn clan. [Guō] Dé with [Guō] Jiàn both became Defending and Protecting Generals, all enfeoffed as Ranked Marquesses, together handling the Residential Guard. It happened that the Three Rulers were young and weak, the governing assistant controlled the government, participating and seizing great affairs, all first consulted and informed the Doager Empress and only afterward was put into effect. When Guànqiū Jiǎn and Zhōng Huì and others made chaos, all falsely claimed her mandates as their justification. Jǐngyuán Fourth Year Twelfth Moon she passed away, Fifth Year Second Moon buried at Gāopínglíng’s west. [19]

明元郭皇后，西平人也，世河右大族。黃初中，本郡反叛，遂沒入宮。明帝即位，甚見愛幸，拜為夫人。叔父立為騎都尉，從父芝為虎賁中郎將。帝疾困，遂立為皇后。齊王即位，尊后為皇太后，稱永寧宮，追封諡太后父滿為西都定侯，以立子建紹其爵。封太后母杜為郃陽君。芝遷散騎常侍、長水校尉，〔一〕立，宣德將軍，皆封列侯。建兄德，出養甄氏。德及建俱為鎮護將軍，皆封列侯，並掌宿衛。值三主幼弱，宰輔統政，與奪大事，皆先咨啟於太后而後施行。毌丘儉、鍾會等作亂，咸假其命而以為辭焉。景元四年十二日崩，五年二月，葬高平陵西。

[18] Wèi lüè states: Among the various Guō, [Guō] Zhī was the most stalwart and upright. Before this time he already for another achievement was enfeoffed as a Marquess.

〔一〕　魏略曰：諸郭之中，芝最壯直。先時自以他功封侯。

[19] Jìn Zhūgōng Zàn states: Jiàn appellation Shūshǐ had capacity and decorum and strong learning, during Tàishǐ died of illness. His son [Guō] Gǔ succeeded, became a Dispensing Affairs Central.

〔二〕　晉諸公贊曰：建安叔始，有器局而彊問，泰始中疾薨。子嘏嗣，為給事中。

Appraisal

Appraisal states: The families of Wèi’s Empresses and Consorts, although they enjoyed wealth and nobility, never once was there like in the decline of Hàn any taking of improper position or seizing control of Court governance. Reflecting on the past to change course, in this was beauty. Looking back on Chén Qún’s discussion, Zhàn Qián’s discourse, they are suitably sufficient to be a standard canon for a Hundred Kings, to be bequeathed as a standard model for later generations.

評曰：魏后妃之家，雖云富貴，未有若衰漢乘非其據，宰割朝政者也。鑒往易軌，於斯為美。追觀陳群之議，棧潛之論，適足以為百王之規典，垂憲範乎後葉矣。

The former was done to protect state dignity and political legitimacy, and the latter to keep the text concise as a high level overview. Usually the various explanatory commentaries such as the Zuǒ Zhuàn then provide more explicit explanations of taboo events and various additional minor details. To some extent this general practice continued into the Sān Guó zhì, with Chén Shòu using euphemisms for certain large scandals and generally omitting small events, and Péi Sōngzhī’s annotations explaining the euphemisms and adding additional minor stories.↩︎
