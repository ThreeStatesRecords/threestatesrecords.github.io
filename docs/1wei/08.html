8  Biographies of Two Gōngsūn, Táo Qiān, Four Zhāng

Gōngsūn Zàn

Gōngsūn Zàn appellation Bóguī was a Liáoxī Lìngzhī man. [1] He became a prefecture’s Below the Gates writing aide,1 had impressive appearance and loud voice, Administrator Hóu valued him, had a daughter be wife to him, [2] and sent him to visit Zhuō prefecture’s Lú Zhí to study the classics. Later he returned and became a prefecture clerk. Administrator Liú in connection to a matter was summoned to visit the Ministry of Justice, [Gōngsūn] Zàn was the chariot driver, personally working as an attendant. When Liú was exiled to Rìnán, [Gōngsūn] Zàn prepared rice and meat, at Běimáng’s top sacrificed to his ancestors, raised the goblet and praid stating: “In the past I was a man’s son, now I am a man’s servant, about to visit Rìnán. Rìnán has bad air, I fear perhaps I will not return, and will take leave of my ancestors from here.” He bowed twice and passionately rose, at the time of those that saw none did not sob. Liú on the road obtained pardon and returned. [Gōngsūn] Zàn as a Filial-Incorruptible became a Cadet,2 appointed Liáodōng Dependent State3 Chief Clerk. Once he with following several tens of cavalry went out the frontier, encountered Xiānbēi of several hundred cavalry, [Gōngsūn] Zàn then withdrew and entered inside an empty station, made a pact with his following cavalry stating: “Now to not charge them, then is to completely die.” [Gōngsūn] Zàn then personally held lance, on both ends attached blades, galloped out to stab the Hú, killing and wounding several tens of men, also lost half of his following cavalry, and therefore was able to escape. The Xiānbēi were deterred, and later did not dare again enter the frontiers. Promoted to Zhuō Magistrate.4 During Guānghé, Liáng Province rebels rose up, mobilized Yōu Province charger cavalry of three thousand men, temporarily granted [Gōngsūn] Zàn Overall Supervisor Enacting Affairs credentials, sent to command them. The army arrived at Jìzhōng,5 Yúyáng’s Zhāng Chún enticed Liáoxī’s Wūhuán6 Qiūlìjū and others to rebel, rob and plunder Jìzhōng, declared himself a General, [3] captured clerks and civilians and attacked Yòuběipíng’s and Liáoxī Dependent State’s various cities, wherever he arrived as ruined and broken. [Gōngsūn] Zàn commanded those he managed, pursued and suppressed [Zhāng] Chún and the rest and had achievements, promoted to Cavalry Commandant. Dependent State Wūhuán Tānzhìwáng led his tribesmen to visit [Gōngsūn] Zàn to surrender. Promoted to Central Cadet General, enfeoffed a city precinct Marquess, advanced to garrison the Dependent State, with the Hú attacking and striking one another for five to six years. Qiūlìjū and others plundered and raided Qīng, Xú, Yōu, Jì, four provinces were harmed, [Gōngsūn] Zàn could not resist.

公孫瓚字伯珪，遼西令支人也。〔一〕為郡門下書佐。有姿儀，大音聲，侯太守器之，以女妻焉，〔二〕遣詣涿郡盧植讀經。後復為郡吏。劉太守坐事徵詣廷尉，瓚為御車，身執徒養。及劉徙日南，瓚具米肉，於北芒上祭先人，舉觴祝曰：「昔為人子，今為人臣，當詣日南。日南瘴氣，或恐不還，與先人辭於此。」再拜慷慨而起，時見者莫不歔欷。劉道得赦還。瓚以孝廉為郎，除遼東屬國長史。嘗從數十騎出行塞，見鮮卑數百騎，瓚乃退入空亭中，約其從騎曰：「今不衝之，則死盡矣。」瓚乃自持矛，兩頭施刃，馳出刺胡，殺傷數十人，亦亡其從騎半，遂得免。鮮卑懲艾，後不敢復入塞。遷為涿令。光和中，涼州賊起，發幽州突騎三千人，假瓚都督行事傳，使將之。軍到薊中，漁陽張純誘遼西烏丸丘力居等叛，劫略薊中，自號將軍，〔三〕略吏民攻右北平、遼西屬國諸城，所至殘破。瓚將所領，追討純等有功，遷騎都尉。屬國烏丸貪至王率種人詣瓚降。遷中郎將，封都亭侯，進屯屬國，與胡相攻擊五六年。丘力居等鈔略青、徐、幽、冀，四州被其害，瓚不能禦。

[1] Lìng pronounced láng and dìng together. Zhī pronounced qí and ér together.

〔一〕　令音郎定反。支音其兒反。

[2] Diǎnlüè states: [Gōngsūn] Zàn by nature was eloquent and intelligent, every time in reporting a matter he was not willing to be tentative, always collectively explaining multiple department affairs, without any forgetting or errors, the Administrator was amazed with his talent.

〔二〕　典略曰：瓚性辯慧，每白事不肯梢入，常總說數曹事，無有忘誤，太守奇其才。

[3] Jiǔzhōu Chūnqiū states: [Zhāng] Chún declared himself Filling Heaven General, King of Āndìng.

〔三〕　九州春秋曰：純自號彌天將軍、安定王。

Liú Yú

The Court discussed believing: “Minister of the Imperial Clan7 Dōnghǎi’s Liú Bó’ān already possesses virtue and righteousness, in the past was Yōu Province Inspector, his grace and faith flowed widely, the Róng and Dí attached to him, if he is sent to defend and succor, without laboring the multitudes it can be settled.” Thus appointed Liú Yú as Yōu Province Governor. [4] [Liú] Yú arrived, sent envoy to reach among the Hú, announcing the benefits and harms, demanding they send [Zhāng] Chún’s head. Qiūlìjū and the rest heard that [Liú] Yú had arrived, were pleased, and each sent translators to on their own submit. [Gōngsūn] Zàn resented that [Liú] Yú had the achievement, and so secretly sent someone to intercept and kill the Hú envoys. The Hú knew the situation, and indirectly traveled to visit [Liú] Yú. [Liú] Yú sent up to dismiss the various garrison soldiers, only keeping [Gōngsūn] Zàn’s commanded infantry and cavalry of ten thousand men to garrison Yòuběipíng. [Zhāng] Chún then abandoned his wife and children, fled to enter among the Xiānbēi, and was by his retainer Wáng Zhèng killed, sent the head to [Liú] Yú. Enfeoffed [Wáng] Zhèng as a Ranked Marquess. [Liú] Yú for the achievement was immediately appointed Grand Commandant, enfeoffed Marquess of Xiāngbēn.8 [5] It happened that Dǒng Zhuó reached Luòyáng, promoted [Liú] Yú to Marshal-in-Chief, [Gōngsūn] Zàn to Exerting Warfare General and enfeoffed Marquess of Jì.

朝議以宗正東海劉伯安既有德義，昔為幽州刺史，恩信流著，戎狄附之，若使鎮撫，可不勞眾而定，乃以劉虞為幽州牧。〔一〕虞到，遣使至胡中，告以利害，責使送純首。丘力居等聞虞至，喜，各遣譯自歸。瓚害虞有功，乃陰使人徼殺胡使。胡知其情，閒行詣虞。虞上罷諸屯兵，但留瓚將步騎萬人屯右北平。純乃棄妻子，逃入鮮卑，為其客王政所殺，送首詣虞。封政為列侯。虞以功即拜太尉，封襄賁侯。〔二〕會董卓至洛陽，遷虞大司馬，瓚奮武將軍，封薊侯。

[4] Wú shū states: [Liú] Yú was a descendant of King Gōng of Dōnghǎi, encountered an era of decline and chaos, and also with the time’s ruler was very distantly related, served in the county as Households Department clerk. For his ability in managing himself and serving duties, summoned to be a prefecture clerk, as a Filial-Incorruptible became a Cadet, eventually promoted to reach Yōu Province Inspector, transferred to Gānlíng Chancellor,9 extremely obtained the hearts of the eastern lands’ Róng and Dí. Later due to illness he returned home, always humbling himself and living unobtrusively, with those of his town and community sharing in joys and sorrows, treating equally those with and without, and did not for his fame and position distinguish himself, the village community all together revered him. At the time when the village community had a law dispute, they did not visit the clerks, but personally went to [Liú] Yú to judge it. [Liú] Yú by the circumstances and reason for them discussed and judged, all large or small respectfully followed, and were not resentful. Once there was someone who lost an ox, in bone and body and fur color resembled [Liú] Yú’s ox and therefore believed it was his, [Liú] Yú immediately yielded and gave it to him. Later the owner himself obtained the original ox, and then returned and apologized for the offense. It happened that Gānlíng was again in chaos, the clerks and civilians remembered [Liú] Yú’s governance and conduct, again he became Gānlíng Chancellor, Gānlíng was greatly governed. Summoned and appointed Secretariat Writers Director, Minister of Staff,10 and as a member of the Ruling Clan with courtesy, he further became Minister of the Imperial Clan.

Yīngxióng jì states: [Liú] Yú became Bópíng Magistrate,11 governance upright and extended fairness, lofty and esteemed, pure and unadorned, within the borders without robbers and bandits, calamities did not appear. At the time in neighboring counties that shared a border, locusts did harm, on reaching Bópíng’s border, flew past and did not enter.

Wèi shū states: [Liú] Yú in Yōu Province was pure and quiet, frugal and restrained, used ritual and righteousness to influence the people. In Emperor Líng’s time, the South Palace burned down, clerks sent to fill vacancies in the provinces and prefectures were all required to contribute cash for repairs of the Palace, some ten million, some twenty million, the rich used private wealth to settle it, some extracted the people’s cash to provide it, the poor that were pure and conscientious had no way to meet the requirements, some going as far as to kill themselves. Emperor Líng because [Liú] Yú was pure and poor, specially did not have him send cash.

〔一〕　吳書曰：虞，東海恭王之後也。遭世衰亂，又與時主疏遠，仕縣為戶曹吏。以能治身奉職，召為郡吏，以孝廉為郎，累遷至幽州刺史，轉甘陵相，甚得東土戎狄之心。後以疾歸家，常降身隱約，與邑黨州閭同樂共卹，等齊有無，不以名位自殊，鄉曲咸共宗之。時鄉曲有所訴訟，不以詣吏，自投虞平之；虞以情理為之論判，皆大小敬從，不以為恨。嘗有失牛者，骨體毛色，與虞牛相似，因以為是，虞便推與之；後主自得本牛，乃還謝罪。會甘陵復亂，吏民思虞治行，復以為甘陵相，甘陵大治。徵拜尚書令、光祿勳，以公族有禮，更為宗正。英雄記曰：虞為博平令，治正推平，高尚純樸，境內無盜賊，災害不生。時鄰縣接壤，蝗蟲為害，至博平界，飛過不入。魏書曰：虞在幽州，清靜儉約，以禮義化民。靈帝時，南宮災，吏遷補州郡者，皆責助治宮錢，或一千萬，或二千萬，富者以私財辨，或發民錢以備之，貧而清慎者，無以充調，或至自殺。靈帝以虞清貧，特不使出錢。

[5] Yīngxióng jì states: [Liú] Yú declined the Grand Commandant office, took advantage to recommend Minister of the Guard12 Zhào Mó, Yì Province Governor Liú Yān, Yù Province Governor Huáng Wǎn, Nányáng Administrator Yáng Xù, all were appointed as Excellencies.

〔二〕　英雄記曰：虞讓太尉，因薦衛尉趙謨、益州牧劉焉、豫州牧黃琬、南陽太守羊續，並任為公。

East of the passes Righteous Troops rose up, [Dǒng] Zhuó therefore seized the Emperor to relocate west, summoned [Liú] Yú as Grand Tutor, the roads were blocked, the letter of appointment was unable to reach. Yuán Shào and Hán Fù discussed, believing: “The Young Emperor is controlled by a Traitor Minister, Heaven’s Under has nowhere to devote heart. [Liú] Yú is of the Imperial Clan and famed, who the people look toward.” Therefore nominated [Liú] Yú to be Emperor. Sent envoy to visit [Liú] Yú, [Liú] Yú to the end would not agree to accept. [Yuán] Shào and the rest further urged [Liú] Yú to manage Secretariat Writer affairs, to undertake authority to enfeoff and appoint,13 [Liú] Yú also did not listen, however he yet with [Yuán] Shào and the rest joined in alliance. [6] [Liú] Yú’s son [Liú] Hé was Attendant Central, at Cháng’ān. Heaven’s Son thought of returning east, sent [Liú] Hé to pretend to escape from [Dǒng] Zhuó, secretly sent out Wǔ Pass to visit [Liú] Yú, to have him lead troops to come welcome. [Liú] Hé on the road passed Yuán Shù, and explained Heaven’s Son’s intentions. [Yuán] Shù thought it advantageous to have [Liú] Yú be an assistance, detained [Liú] Hé and did not send him, promising when troops arrive they would together go west, having [Liú] Hé make a letter to [Liú] Yú. [Liú] Yú obtained [Liú] Hé’s letter, and so sent several thousand cavalry to visit [Liú] Hé. [Gōngsūn] Zàn knew [Yuán] Shù had ulterior ambitions, did not wish to send troops, stopped [Liú] Yú, [Liú] Yú did not agree. [Gōngsūn] Zàn feared [Yuán] Shù would hear and resent him, and also sent his younger cousin [Gōngsūn] Yuè to command a thousand cavalry to visit [Yuán] Shù to himself join alliance, and secretly instruct [Yuán] Shù to seize [Liú] Hé and take away his troops. Because of this [Liú] Yú and [Gōngsūn] Zàn increasingly had discord. [Liú] Hé escaped [Yuán] Shù and came north, and again was by [Yuán] Shào detained.

關東義兵起，卓遂劫帝西遷，徵虞為太傅，道路隔塞，信命不得至。袁紹、韓馥議，以為少帝制於姦臣，天下無所歸心。虞，宗室知名，民之望也，遂推虞為帝。遣使詣虞，虞終不肯受。紹等復勸虞領尚書事，承制封拜，虞又不聽，然猶與紹等連和。〔一〕虞子和為侍中，在長安。天子思東歸，使和偽逃卓，潛出武關詣虞，令將兵來迎。和道經袁術，為說天子意。術利虞為援，留和不遣，許兵至俱西，令和為書與虞。虞得和書，乃遣數千騎詣和。瓚知術有異志，不欲遣兵，止虞，虞不可。瓚懼術聞而怨之，亦遣其從弟越將千騎詣術以自結，而陰教術執和，奪其兵。由是虞、瓚益有隙。和逃術來北，復為紹所留。

[6] Jiǔzhōu Chūnqiū states: [Yuán] Shào and [Hán] Fù sent former Lèláng Administrator Gānlíng’s Zhāng Qí to present the discussions to [Liú] Yú, to have him accept the Honored Title. [Liú] Yú in stern voice rebuked [Zhāng] Qí stating: “How dare you produce these words? The way of loyalty and filial piety I already have been unable to reach. I received the state’s favor, Heaven’s Under is disturbed in chaos, I have been unable to devote my life to eliminate the state’s humilation, look to the various provinces and prefectures’ servicemen of ardent righteousness to join forces to face west, to aid and welcome the young Ruler, but yet you then recklessly devise a rebellious plot, do you wish to smear and defile a loyal minister?”

Wú shū states: [Hán] Fù sent a letter to Yuán Shù that said: “The Emperor is not Xiào-Líng’s son.14 We wish to comply with the precedent of Jiàng and Guàn executing and deposing the Young Ruler and welcoming and establishing the King of Dài.15 In praise of [Liú] Yú’s achievements and virtue, governance and conduct, in the Huáxià few compare, right now of the Ruling House branch associates, of all none are able to reach him.” It also said: “In the past Guāng-Wǔ from King Dìng was five generations, as Marshal-in-Chief he managed the Hé’s north, Gěng Yǎn and Féng Yì urged him to accept the Honored Title, and in the end he replaced Gēngshǐ.16 Now Liú-gōng from King Gōng’s branch, its count is also five, as Marshal-in-Chief he manages as Yōu Province Governor, this is to be the same as Guāng-Wǔ.” At the time there were four stars converging at Jīwěi, [Hán] Fù claimed a prophecy said a divine man would appear in Yān’s allocation. Also reported that Jǐyīn man Wáng Dìng found a jade seal, the inscription stating: “Expectation of Heaven’s Son.”17 Also sighting of two sons appearing in Dài prefecture, saying Yú should replace and be established.18 [Yuán] Shào also separately wrote letter to inform [Yuán] Shù. At the time [Yuán] Shù secretly had a heart to not be a servant, believed it not advantageous for the country to have a mature ruler, and outside claimed public righteousness to answer and refuse them. [Yuán] Shào also sent someone to secretly inform [Liú] Yú. [Liú] Yú believed the state had a legitimate lineage, it was not what servants of men should discuss, firmly declined and would not agree, and so wished to plan to flee to the Xiōngnú to sever himself, [Yuán] Shào and the rest thus stopped. Someone asked the reason, answered stating: “Now if I select the robed and capped families’ junior relatives and excellent servicemen and give them riches and honor, all will themselves believe that for their offices they should obtain them, and not thank others for the kindness.” Those he favored and treated well were arrogant and unrestrained, many were the sorts of mediocre fellows, like former fortune-teller and numerologist Liú Wěitái, fabric peddler Lǐ Yízǐ, merchant Yuè Hédāng and others three men, with them established oath of brotherhood, calling himself Bó, calling the three men as Zhòng, Shū, jì,27 their wealth all in the hundreds of millions. Sometimes he took their daughters to be matched to his own sons, always citing the ancient sorts as Qǔ Zhōu and Guàn Yīng as an example.

〔二〕　英雄記曰：瓚統內外，衣冠子弟有材秀者，必抑使困在窮苦之地。或問其故，答曰：「今取衣冠家子弟及善士富貴之，皆自以為職當得之，不謝人善也。」所寵遇驕恣者，類多庸兒，若故卜數師劉緯臺、販繒李移子、賈人樂何當等三人，與之定兄弟之誓，自號為伯，謂三人者為仲叔季，富皆巨億，或取其女以配己子，常稱古者曲周、灌嬰之屬以譬也。

[10] Yīngxióng jì states: Before this there was a children’s rhyme stating: “Yān’s south boundary, Zhào’s north border, the center where they do not meet as big as a whetstone, only here at this center can one escape the world.” [Gōngsūn] Zàn believed Yì fit this, and so built the citadel to firmly defend. [Gōngsūn] Zàn’s separate officers had those besieged by the enemy, on principle he did not rescue, his explanation stating: “To rescue one man is to cause later officers to rely on rescue and not fiercely battle. Now to not rescue this one, later officers will remember to exhert themselves.” Therefore at the time when Yuán Shào first to the north struck, the separate camps atop [Gōngsūn] Zàn’s south border themselves assessed that if they defended then they could not secure themselves, and also knew they certainly would not meet with rescue, therefore some themselves killed their commanders, some were by [Yuán] Shào’s troops defeated, therefore allowing [Yuán] Shào’s army to directly reach the gates.

Your Servant Sōngzhī believes: Of the words of children’s rhyme, none were not all fulfilled. As for this record, it seems to be without verification. The composition of the rhyme’s words, probably was to cause [Gōngsūn] Zàn to from the beginning to end protect Yì, with no concern for long term strategy. But [Gōngsūn] Zàn taking advantage of the prestige of defeating the Huángjīn, his ambitions spreading far, therefore installing three provinces of Inspectors, plotting to exterminate Yuán-shì, was what brought about the defeat.

〔三〕　英雄記曰：先是有童謠曰：「燕南垂，趙北際，中央不合大如礪，惟有此中可避世。」瓚以易當之，乃築京固守。瓚別將有為敵所圍，義不救也。其言曰：「救一人，使後將恃救不力戰；今不救此，後將當念在自勉。」是以袁紹始北擊之時，瓚南界上別營自度守則不能自固，又知必不見救，是以或自殺其將帥，或為紹兵所破，遂令紹軍徑至其門。臣松之以為童謠之言，無不皆驗；至如此記，似若無徵。謠言之作，蓋令瓚終始保易，無事遠略。而瓚因破黃巾之威，意志張遠，遂置三州刺史，圖滅袁氏，所以致敗也。

How could I have expected you to abandon the high righteousness of ardent martyrs and chase the dangerous path to disaster and destruction, stopping and changing plans, having friendship exchanged for hatred, secretly sending servicemen and horses, to invade and revage Yù Province. On first hearing armored soldiers were in the south, I personally oversaw the battle lines, fearing that the flying arrows bursting forth and violent blades crossing and gathering would increase the disaster on you, and only add to my blame. Therefore I made an earnest and compassionate letter, hoping you could reform and repent. But you became all the more self indulgant, boasted of your power and strategems, saying that Heaven’s network could be devoured, towering heroes could be exterminated, and indeed caused your noble younger brother to perish at the end of blades. This report was still in the ears, but you sir did not even investigate the source of the disaster, restrain heart and blame yourself, and only wished to indulge your limitless anger, with no regard to the crossing between rebellion and obedience, concealed resentment and harmed the people, charging at my person. Therefore galloping horses and drawn bowstrings occupied my territory, afflictions upon all the living people, the violations reaching to the white bones. I could not decline to respond, and so raised the Jiè Bridge campaign. At that time your troops thundered and shook in spirit, your fine horses flashed like lightning, my brigades and followers had just gathered, the equipment not organized, the strong and the weak clearly distinct, the many and the few obviously differentiated, but borrowing the assistance of Heaven, a small battle was a great victory, therefore we overran your fleeing backs, took the ramparts and stores of grain, was this not the manifested signs of Heaven’s power assisting the faithful and fortunes abundant upon those with courtesy? Your ambitions still were not satiated, so you again gathered the remnant embers, leading the verminous bandits preying on us, to burn down Bóhǎi. I again could not be at peace, employing the brigades to Lóng river.31 Weak troops at the front to entice, the main army had not yet crossed, but your guts were broken and multitudes scattered, without beating the drums you were defeated, soldiers and multitudes disturbed in chaos, lord and servants all fleeing. This also was your doing, not my fault. From this afterward, our disastrous hostility was increasingly deep, my brigades and regiments could not contain their anger, and therefore it reached to piling up corpses into towers, skulls filled the wilds. In pity of those without crimes, never once have I not sighed and shed tears. Later on I repeatedly received your letters, the statements and intentions graceful and subdued, having words of reforming from the past and improving the coming, I rejoiced at our old friendship being restored and moreover pitied that the numerous people were not at peace, and every time I at once withdrew the brigades to drive south, to comply with dispatch letters, but in not even a short time, writings of feathered dispatches from the northern border never once did not arrive. From this I was pained in heart and distressed in head, with nowhere to express my feelings. One who occupies position as commander of the Three Armies and who bears the responsibility of ranked officers should have anger like dense frost, joy like timely rain, his praises and blames and enjoyments and dislikes should be clearly observable. But you sir are duplicitous in your virtue, changed plans based on strong or weak, in distress then humble yourself, at ease then are reckless and indulgant, conduct without consistency, speech without substance, can one who would be a strong serviceman really be like this? Then you slaughtered and killed the old and weak, the lands of Yōu were angry and resentful, the multitudes rebelled and the close departed, you were isolated and without allies. Also the Wūhuán and Huìmò were all of the same province as you sir, while they and I have different customs, but each were energized and enraged, and competed to be my spearpoint advance. Also the Eastern and Western Xiānbēi lifted heels to come attach to me. This is not what my virtue was able to attract, rather it was you sir that drove away and delivered them. In an era of desolation and peril, occupying in the dangers of shields and spears, inside violating the oath of the alliance, outside losing the hearts of the Róng and Dí, troops rising in the provincial soils, disaster appearing from within the screen walls, to like this seek to settle hegemony, is it not very difficult? Formerly as the western mountains were running rampant, I sent out troops to pacify and suppress, it happened that Qū Yì’s remnants in fear of execution fled for their lives, and therefore I then halted the main army, divided troops to sweep them away, these troops were my front advance, that at Jìe Bridge captured flags and took ramparts, the first to ascend and subdue the enemy. On first hearing that you sir were given engraved gold and woven purple, mandated to be a leading commander, I said you would because of this exert yourself, to avenge the humiliation of Mèngmíng,32 and therefore my battle men craned necks, watching for banners and pennants, and were bewildered that you then concealed light and hid in shadow, silent and unheard, in the end arriving at slaughter and extermination, on your behalf they pitied this. To have the fury to pacify Heaven’s Under, hope for achievements to endure through the ages, the authority to drive brigades and followers, maintain and nurture war horses, but the rebelling you do not suppress, the submitting you do not receive, ability to awe and to attract are both lost, how can you establish reputation? Now the old capital is restored, Heaven’s network said to be repaired, the criminals thus destroyed, the loyal and competent assist the transformation, the Huáxià orderly and solemn, looking out for the work of reverence, about to put away shield and spear, release and scatter the oxena nd horses, why do you sir alone hold to trifling servicemen to guard an expansive army interior, sweetly accepting evil reputation to hasten decay, abandoning good virtue to last long? Carefully consider it, it is not a good strategy. It is appropriate to discard regret and remove resentment, and esteem our old friendship. If these words are flawed, may August Heaven hear of it.” [Gōngsūn] Zàn did not answer, but increased his war preparations. He spoke to Guān Jìng stating: “Right now the Four Quarters like tigers compete, that none are able to sit below my city and hold for successive years is clear. What can Yuán Bvenchū do to me?”

〔五〕　漢晉春秋曰：袁紹與瓚書曰：「孤與足下，既有前盟舊要，申以討亂之誓，愛過夷、叔，分著丹青，謂為旅力同軌，足踵齊、晉，故解印釋紱，以北帶南，分割膏腴，以奉執事，此非孤赤情之明驗邪？豈寤足下棄烈士之高義，尋禍亡之險蹤，輟而改慮，以好易怨，盜遣士馬，犯暴豫州。始聞甲卒在南，親臨戰陳，懼于飛矢迸流，狂刃橫集，以重足下之禍，徒增孤（子）之咎釁也，故為薦書懇惻，冀可改悔。而足下超然自逸，矜其威詐，謂天罔可吞，豪雄可滅，果令貴弟殞于鋒刃之端。斯言猶在於耳，而足下曾不尋討禍源，克心罪己，苟欲逞其無疆之怒，不顧逆順之津，匿怨害民，聘於余躬。遂躍馬控弦，處我疆土，毒遍生民，辜延白骨。孤辭不獲已，以登界橋之役。是時足下兵氣霆震，駿馬電發；僕師徒肇合，機械不嚴，彊弱殊科，眾寡異論，假天之助，小戰大克，遂陵躡奔背，因壘館穀，此非天威棐諶，福豐有禮之符表乎？足下志猶未厭，乃復糾合餘燼，率我蛑賊，以焚爇勃海。孤又不獲寧，用及龍河之師。羸兵前誘，大軍未濟，而足下膽破眾散，不鼓而敗，兵眾擾亂，君臣並奔。此又足下之為，非孤之咎也。自此以後，禍隙彌深，孤之師旅，不勝其忿，遂至積尸為京，頭顱滿野，愍彼無辜，未嘗不慨然失涕也。後比得足下書，辭意婉約，有改往脩來之言。僕既欣於舊好克復，且愍兆民之不寧，每輒引師南駕，以順簡書。弗盈一時，而北邊羽檄之文，未嘗不至。孤是用痛心疾首，靡所錯情。夫處三軍之帥，當列將之任，宜令怒如嚴霜，喜如時雨，臧否好惡，坦然可觀。而足下二三其德，彊弱易謀，急則曲躬，緩則放逸，行無定端，言無質要，為壯士者固若此乎！既乃殘殺老弱，幽土憤怨，眾叛親離，孑然無黨。又烏丸、濊貊，皆足下同州，僕與之殊俗，各奮迅激怒，爭為鋒銳；又東西鮮卑，舉踵來附。此非孤德所能招，乃足下驅而致之也。夫當荒危之世，處干戈之險，內違同盟之誓，外失戎狄之心，兵興州壤，禍發蕭牆，將以定霸，不亦難乎！前以西山陸梁，出兵平討，會麴義餘殘，畏誅逃命，故遂住大軍，分兵撲蕩，此兵孤之前行，乃界橋搴旗拔壘，先登制敵者也。始聞足下鐫金紆紫，命以元帥，謂當因茲奮發，以報孟明之恥，是故戰夫引領，竦望旌旆，怪遂含光匿影，寂爾無聞，卒臻屠滅，相為惜之。夫有平天下之怒，希長世之功，權御師徒，帶養戎馬，叛者無討，服者不收，威懷並喪，何以立名？今舊京克復，天罔云補，罪人斯亡，忠幹翼化，華夏儼然，望於穆之作，將戢干戈，放散牛馬，足下獨何守區區之士，保軍內之廣，甘惡名以速朽，亡令德之久長？壯而籌之，非良策也。宜釋憾除嫌，敦我舊好。若斯言之玷，皇天是聞。」瓚不答，而增脩戎備。謂關靖曰：「當今四方虎爭，無有能坐吾城下相守經年者明矣。袁本初其若我何！」

Xiānyú Fǔ

Xiānyú Fǔ led his multitudes to serve Royal Command. Appointed [Xiānyú] Fǔ as Establishing Loyalty General, Supervisor of Yōu Province’s Six Prefectures. When Tàizǔ and Yuán Shào resisted one another at Guāndù, Yán Róu sent envoy to visit Tàizǔ to receive instructions, promoted to Protector of the Wūhuán Colonel. But [Xiānyú] Fǔ personally visited Tàizǔ, appointed Left Crossing the Liáo General, enfeoffed a precinct Marquess, sent to return to defend and succor his own province. [17] When Tàizǔ defeated Nánpí, [Yán] Róu led his division retainers with the Xiānbēi to proffer famed horses to provide for the army, followed the campaign against the Three Prefectures Wūhuán, for the achievements enfeoffed Marquess Within the Passes. [18] [Xiānyú] Fǔ also led his multitudes to follow. When Emperor Wén ascended the throne, appointed [Xiānyú] Fǔ as Tiger Fang General, [Yán] Róu as Crossing the Liáo General, both advanced in fief to county Marquesses, position Specially Advanced.34

鮮于輔將其眾奉王命。以輔為建忠將軍，督幽州六郡。太祖與袁紹相拒於官渡，閻柔遣使詣太祖受事，遷護烏丸校尉。而輔身詣太祖，拜左度遼將軍，封亭侯，遣還鎮撫本州。〔一〕太祖破南皮，柔將部曲及鮮卑獻名馬以奉軍，從征三郡烏丸，以功封關內侯。〔二〕輔亦率其眾從。文帝踐阼，拜輔虎牙將軍，柔度遼將軍，皆進封縣侯。位特進。

Táo Qiān

Táo Qiān appellation Gōngzǔ was a Dānyáng man. [19] In youth enjoyed study, became a scholar, served in the province and prefecture, nominated Abundant Talent,36 appointed Lú Magistrate,37 [20] promoted to Yōu Province Inspector, summoned and appointed Consultant Cadet, Advisor to Chariots and Cavalry General Zhāng Wēn’s Army Affairs, to the west suppressed Hán Suí. [21] It happened that Xú Province Huángjīn rose up, appointed [Táo] Qiān as Xú Province Inspector, struck the Huángjīn, defeated and drove them away. In the chaos of Dǒng Zhuó, the provinces and prefecture raised troops, Heaven’s Son set capital at Cháng’ān, the west was cut off, [Táo] Qiān sent envoy to secretly travel to deliver tribute, promoted to Securing East General, Xú Province Governor, enfeoffed Marquess of Lìyáng.38 At the time, Xú Province’s Hundred Surnames were flourishing, grain and rice abundantly provided, of the refugees many submitted to him. But [Táo] Qiān turned back on principles and indulged his passions. Guǎnglíng Administrator Lángyé’s Zhào Yù was a famed serviceman of Xú region, and for his loyalty and uprightness met with estrangement, [22] while Cáo Hóng39 and others were slanderous and evil petty men, and [Táo] Qiān was close with and entrusted them.
