4  Annals of Three Young Emperors

Zhèngshǐ (240 - 248)

The King of Qí forbidden name Fāng, appellation Lánqīng. Emperor Míng was without sons, and adopted the King and the King of Qín [Cáo] Xún. The Palace affairs were confidential, none had knowledge of where they came from. [1] Qīnglóng Third Year [235], established as King of Qí. Jǐngchū Third Year, Standard Moon [239 Jan 22 – Feb 20], Dīnghài [239 Jan 22], first day of the moon, the Emperor was extremely ill, and so established him as Imperial Heir-Apparent. That day, he assumed the Imperial Emperor’s throne, Great Amnesty. Honored the Imperial Empress as Imperial Dowager Empress. General-in-Chief Cáo Shuǎng and Grand Commandant Sīmǎ [Yì] Xuān-wáng assisted in governing. Edict said: “We with meager body continue and inherit a vast enterprise, utterly alone in grief, with no way to express it. The General-in-Chief and Grand Commandant respectively received the final command, to on each side assist Our person, the Excellency over the Masses, Excellency of Works, the senior ministers and foremost assistants together lead the hundred offices, to secure the State Altars. Thus with the various ministers and grandees exhort yourselves in heart, to accord with Our intentions. All the conscriptions for raising and building Palaces Residences are all by legacy Edict dismissed. Government slaves sixty and above are dismissed to be free persons.” Second Moon [239 Feb 21 – Mar 21], the Western Regions with relay translators proffered fire-laundered cloth, Edict on the General-in-Chief and Grand Commandant to oversee its testing to show to the hundred offices. [2]

齊王諱芳，字蘭卿。明帝無子，養王及秦王詢；宮省事祕，莫有知其所由來者。〔一〕青龍三年，立為齊王。景初三年正月丁亥朔，帝甚病，乃立為皇太子。是日，即皇帝位，大赦。尊皇后曰皇太后。大將軍曹爽、太尉司馬宣王輔政。詔曰：「朕以眇身，繼承鴻業，煢煢在疚，靡所控告。大將軍、太尉奉受末命，夾輔朕躬，司徒、司空、冢宰、元輔總率百寮，以寧社稷，其與群卿大夫勉勗乃心，稱朕意焉。諸所興作宮室之役，皆以遺詔罷之。官奴婢六十已上，免為良人。」二月，西域重譯獻火浣布，詔大將軍、太尉臨試以示百寮。〔二〕

[1] Wèishì Chūnqiū states: Some say they were the King of Rènchéng [Cáo] Kǎi’s sons.

〔一〕　魏氏春秋曰：或云任城王楷子。

[2] Yìwù zhì states: Sītiáo state has a fire island, in the middle of the south sea. On its top there are wild fires, in spring and summer on their own appearing, in autumn and winter on their own dying. There are trees growing in the middle that are not consumed, the branches and bark all the more thrive, in autumn and winter the fires die and then they all wither and decay. Their local custom is to collect their bark in winter to make cloth, the color a slightly green black; if dust or dirt stains it, then it is thrown into fire, and then it is all the more clean and bright.

Fù-zǐ states: In Hàn Emperor Huán’s time, General-in-Chief Liáng Jì used fire-laundered cloth to make a single robe, once at a great gathering of guest retainers, [Liáng] Jì acted as if fighting over liquor, spilled the cup and stained it, feigned anger, took off the robe and said: “Burn it.” The cloth caught fire, glowing bright, like burning ordinary cloth, the stain was consumed and fire extinguished, it was clear bright clean white, as if washed with lye water.

Sōushénjì states: In the wilderness of Kūnlún, there is a mountain of flaming fire, atop the mountain are birds and beasts and grasses and trees, all living in the middle of the flames, therefore if there is fire-laundered cloth, if it is not the bark or nettles of the grasses and trees, then it is the feathers or furs of the birds and beasts. In Hàn’s time the Western Regions used to proffer this cloth, in the interim they were for a long time cut off, so reaching to Wèi’s beginning, at the time people doubted if it existed or not. Emperor Wén believed that fire’s nature was too fierce, without an essence to tolerate life, and wrote this in the Diǎnlùn, to make clear that it was not true and end any belief by the intelligent. Reaching to Emperor Míng’s establishment, Edict on the Three Excellencies said: “The Former Emperor in the past wrote the Diǎnlùn, proper words that do not decay, thus carve on stone outside the gates of the Temple and the Grand Academy, with the Stone Classics together, to forever show to coming generations.” Reaching to when the Western Regions envoys arrived and proffered fire-laundered cloth, thereupon they were chiseled to remove this discussion, and Heaven’s Under laughed at it.

Your Servant Sōngzhī in the past accompanied the western campaign to reach Luòyáng, successively observed old artifacts, saw the Diǎnlùn stone at the Grand Academy was still preserved, but the outside of the Temple gates was without it. Asking the various elders, they said that when Jìn first received the abdication, they at once used the Wèi Temple, and moved this stone to the Grand Academy, and there were not two locations of establishment. I humbly say this report is not correct.

Also Dōngfāng Shuò’s Shényìjīng states: Beyond the southern wilderness there is a fire mountain, length thirty lǐ, width fifty lǐ, in the middle all are living trees that do not become ash, day and night burning in fire, in heavy winds it does not grow fiercer, in fierce rain it is not extinguished. In the fire there are rats, weight a hundred jīn, hair length over two chǐ, slender as silk, and can be made into cloth. Commonly they reside in fire, color a thorough red, and at times when they go out then their color becomes white. With water pursuing and dousing them they at once die, collecting their hairs, weaving into cloth.

〔二〕　異物志曰：斯調國有火州，在南海中。其上有野火，春夏自生，秋冬自死。有木生于其中而不消也，枝皮更活，秋冬火死則皆枯瘁。其俗常冬采其皮以為布，色小青黑；若塵垢汙之，便投火中，則更鮮明也。傅子曰：漢桓帝時，大將軍梁冀以火浣布為單衣，常大會賓客，冀陽爭酒，失杯而汙之，偽怒，解衣曰：「燒之。」布得火，煒曄赫然，如燒凡布，垢盡火滅，粲然絜白，若用灰水焉。搜神記曰：崑崙之墟，有炎火之山，山上有鳥獸草木，皆生於炎火之中，故有火浣布，非此山草木之皮枲，則其鳥獸之毛也。漢世西域舊獻此布，中間久絕；至魏初，時人疑其無有。文帝以為火性酷烈，無含生之氣，著之典論，明其不然之事，絕智者之聽。及明帝立，詔三公曰：「先帝昔著典論，不朽之格言，其刊石於廟門之外及太學，與石經並，以永示來世。」至是西域使至而獻火浣布焉，於是刊滅此論，而天下笑之。臣松之昔從征西至洛陽，歷觀舊物，見典論石在太學者尚存，而廟門外無之，問諸長老，云晉初受禪，即用魏廟，移此石于太學，非兩處立也。竊謂此言為不然。又東方朔神異經曰：南荒之外有火山，長三十里，廣五十里，其中皆生不燼之木，晝夜火燒，得暴風不猛，猛雨不滅。火中有鼠，重百斤，毛長二尺餘，細如絲，可以作布。常居火中，色洞赤，時時出外而色白，以水逐而沃之即死，續其毛，織以為布。

Dīngchǒu [239 Mar 13], Edict said: “The Grand Commandant embodies principles correct and upright, exhaustively loyal for three reigns, to the south captured Mèng Dá, to the west defeated the Shǔ caitiffs, to the east exterminated Gōngsūn Yuān, achievements crowning Within the Seas. In the past Zhōu Chéng established the offices of Protector and Tutor, more recently Hàn Xiǎnzōng esteemed and favored Dèng Yǔ, in order to specially glorify the outstanding and exceptional who must have honors. Thus appoint the Grand Commandant as Grand Tutor, Holding Stave to preside over troops as Overall Supervisor of various army affairs as before.” Third Moon [239 Mar 22 – Apr 20], appointed Campaigning East General Mǎn Chǒng as Grand Commandant. Summer, Sixth Moon [239 Jun 19 – Jul 17], as Liáodōng’s Tà county’s officials and people crossed the sea to reside at Qí prefecture’s border, used former Zòngchéng to form Xīntà county to house the relocated people. Autumn, Seventh Moon [239 Jul 18 – Aug 16], the Superior first personally oversaw Court, hearing the Excellencies and Ministers memorialize affairs. Eighth Moon [239 Aug 17 – Sep 14], Great Amnesty. Winter, Tenth Moon [239 Oct 15 – Nov 12], Defending South General Huáng Quán became Chariots and Cavalry General.

丁丑詔曰：「太尉體道正直，盡忠三世，南擒孟達，西破蜀虜，東滅公孫淵，功蓋海內。昔周成建保傅之官，近漢顯宗崇寵鄧禹，所以優隆雋乂，必有尊也。其以太尉為太傅，持節統兵都督諸軍事如故。」三月，以征東將軍滿寵為太尉。夏六月，以遼東東沓縣吏民渡海居齊郡界，以故縱城為新沓縣以居徙民。秋七月，上始親臨朝，聽公卿奏事。八月，大赦。冬十月，鎮南將軍黃權為車騎將軍。

Twelfth Moon [240 Dec 13 – 240 Jan 11], Edict said: “Lièzǔ Míng Huángdì in the Standard Moon relinquished and departed Heaven’s Under, the ministers and subjects will forever remember the sorrows of the tragic day, thus return to using the Xià Standard. Although it violates the Former Emperor’s principles of continuing the Three Lineages, this also is a reason for Ritual standards to be changed and reformed. Also the Xià Standard in calculations is for obtaining Heaven’s Standard, thus use the Jiàn Yín Moon to be Zhèngshǐ Inaugural Year Standard Moon, use the Jiàn Chǒu Moon as the next Twelfth Moon.

十二月，詔曰：「烈祖明皇帝以正月棄背天下，臣子永惟忌日之哀，其復用夏正；雖違先帝通三統之義，斯亦禮制所由變改也。又夏正於數為得天正，其以建寅之月為正始元年正月，以建丑月為後十二月。」

Zhèngshǐ Inaugural Year, Spring, Second Moon [240 Mar 11 – Apr 8], Yǐchǒu, added titles on Attendant Central Central Writers Overseer Liú Fàng and Attendant Central Central Writers Director Sūn Zī as Left and Right Radiant Grandees. Bǐngxū [240 Mar 16], as Liáodōng’s Wèn and Běifēng counties’ people were relocating across the sea, divided along Qí prefecture’s Xī’ān, Línzī, Chāngguó counties’ borders to form Xīnwèn and Nánfēng counties to house the refugees.

正始元年春二月乙丑，加侍中中書監劉放、侍中中書令孫資為左右光祿大夫。丙戌，以遼東汶、北豐縣民流徙渡海，規齊郡之西安、臨菑、昌國縣界為新汶、南豐縣，以居流民。

From the previous winter Twelfth Moon reaching to this moon there was no rain. Bǐngyín [240 Apr 25], Edict ordered prison offices to urgently redress injustices, investigate and release the light and trivial, and the various Excellencies and Ministers and servicemen of honest speech and commendable plans were each to thoroughly explain their hearts. Summer, Fourth Moon [240 May 9 – Jun 6], Chariots and Cavalry General Huáng Quán died. Autumn, Seventh Moon [240 Aug 5 – Sep 3], Edict said: “The Yì says ‘decrease above increase below,’ ‘limit by systems, do not injure wealth, do not harm people.’ Right now the Hundred Surnames do not have enough and yet the Attendant Offices create many gold and silver trifling objects; what is it for? Now send out yellow gold and silver objects of one hundred fifty varieties, over one thousand eight hundred jīn, to melt down to provide for military expenditures.” Eighth Moon [240 Sep 4 – Oct 2], the Chariot Carriage toured and inspected Luòyáng’s borders’ autumn harvest, bestowed on the highly aged and diligent-farmers each on distinct basis.

自去冬十二月至此月不雨。丙寅，詔令獄官亟平冤枉，理出輕微；群公卿士讜言嘉謀，各悉乃心。夏四月，車騎將軍黃權薨。秋七月，詔曰：「易稱損上益下，節以制度，不傷財，不害民。方今百姓不足而御府多作金銀雜物，將奚以為？今出黃金銀物百五十種，千八百餘斤，銷冶以供軍用」八月，車駕巡省洛陽界秋稼，賜高年力田各有差。

Second Year, Spring, Second Moon [241 Feb 28 – Mar 28], the Emperor first completed the Lúnyǔ, sent the Minister of Ceremonies with Grand Offering to sacrifice to Kǒng-zǐ at Bìyōng, with Yán Yuān accompanying.

二年春二月，帝初通論語，使太常以太牢祭孔子於辟雍，以顏淵配。

Summer, Fifth Moon [241 May 28 – Jun 25], Wú officer Zhū Rán and others besieged Xiāngyáng’s Fánchéng, Grand Tutor Sīmǎ [Yì] Xuān-wáng led the multitudes to resist them. [3] Sixth Moon [241 Jun 26 – Jul 25], Xīnchǒu [241 Jul 24], [Zhū Rán] retreated. Jǐmǎo [241 Jul 2], appointed Campaigning East General Wáng Líng as Chariots and Cavalry General. Winter, Twelfth Moon [242 Jan 19 – Feb 16], Nán’ān prefecture earthquake.

夏五月，吳將朱然等圍襄陽之樊城，太傅司馬宣王率眾拒之〔一〕。六月辛丑，退。己卯，以征東將軍王淩為車騎將軍。冬十二月，南安郡地震。

[3] Gān Bǎo’s Jìn jì states: Wú officers Quán Cóng invaded Quèbēi, Zhū Rán and Sūn Lún with fifty thousand men besieged Fánchéng, Zhūgě Jǐn and Bù Zhì invaded Zhāzhōng. [Quán] Cóng had already been defeated and fled but Fán’s siege was critical. [Sīmǎ Yì] Xuān-wáng said: “Zhāzhōng’s civilians and Yí are a hundred thousand, separated to the river’s south, wandering apart without a master, Fánchéng has been attacked for successive moons without resolution; this is a dangerous situation. I request to personally suppress them.” The commentators all said: “The rebels from afar besiege Fánchéng and cannot take it, repressed below firm city walls, having a momentum to be defeated on their own. It is appropriate to use a long-term strategy to deal with them.” [Sīmǎ Yì] Xuān-wáng said: “Military treatises have this: ‘[if] the officer is capable and yet to restrain him, this is a shackled army; [if] incapable and yet to appoint him, this is an overturned army.’ Now the borders are disturbed, the people’s hearts in doubt and confusion, this is a great concern for the State Altars.” Sixth Moon [241 Jun 26 – Jul 25], commanded the various armies on southern campaign, the Chariot Carriage escorting him to outside Jīnyáng city gates. [Sīmǎ Yì] Xuān-wáng believed the south was hot and humid, not appropriate to hold for a long time, and sent light cavalry to provoke them, [Zhū] Rán did not dare move. Thereupon then ordered the various armies to rest and wash, selected the elite, recruited a first to ascend, extended orders, showing a momentum of being certain to attack. [Zhū] Rán and the rest heard this, and so in the night escaped. Pursued reaching to Sānzhōukǒu, greatly killed and captured.

〔一〕　干寶晉紀曰：吳將全琮寇芍陂，朱然、孫倫五萬人圍樊城，諸葛瑾、步騭寇柤中；琮已破走而樊圍急。宣王曰：「柤中民夷十萬，隔在水南，流離無主，樊城被攻，歷月不解，此危事也，請自討之。」議者咸言：「賊遠圍樊城不可拔，挫于堅城之下，有自破之勢，宜長策以御之。」宣王曰：「軍志有之：將能而御之，此為縻軍；不能而任之，此為覆軍。今疆埸騷動，民心疑惑，是社稷之大憂也。」六月，督諸軍南征，車駕送津陽城門外。宣王以南方暑溼，不宜持久，使輕騎挑之，然不敢動。於是乃令諸軍休息洗沐，簡精銳，募先登，申號令，示必攻之勢。然等聞之，乃夜遁。追至三州口，大殺獲。

Third Year, Spring, Standard Moon [242 Feb 17 – Mar 18], the King of Dōngpíng [Cáo] Huī died. Third Moon [242 Apr 17 – May 16], Grand Commandant Mǎn Chǒng died. Autumn, Seventh Moon [242 Aug 14 – Sep 11], Jiǎshēn [242 Aug 31], Nán’ān prefecture earthquake. Yǐyǒu [242 Sep 1], appointed Manager of the Army General Jiǎng Jì as Grand Commandant. Winter, Twelfth Moon [243 Jan 8 – Feb 6], Wèi prefecture earthquake.

三年春正月，東平王徽薨。三月，太尉滿寵薨。秋七月甲申，南安郡地震。乙酉，以領軍將軍蔣濟為太尉。冬十二月，魏郡地震。

Fourth Year, Spring, Standard Moon [243 Feb 7 – Mar 7], the Emperor was applied the foremost dress, bestowed on the various ministers each on distinct basis. Summer, Fourth Moon [243 May 6 – Jun 4], Yǐmǎo [243 May 29], enthroned as Imperial Empress Zhēn-shì, Great Amnesty. Fifth Moon [243 Jun 5 – Jul 3], first day of the moon, the sun was eclipsed, completely. [Xú] Shào originally was false Nánlíng Supervisor, talent and character open and strong. [Sūn] Yù is Sūn Quán’s branch relation, loyal and good and discerning in affairs. Thus send [Xú] Shào south to return, with [Sūn] Yù as his aide, to spread and declare the state’s commands, inform and explain to the Wú men, all that is to be revealed and spoken of is all to be by the facts. If they are informed and aware, it will not damage the planning for campaigns; overall the temple victory’s far-reaching calculation is the way since antiquity. Thus appoint [Xú] Shào as concurrent Cavalier Regular Attendant, added Serving Chariots Commandant, enfeoffed a city precinct Marquess, [Sūn] Yù as concurrent Dispensing Affairs Yellow Gate Attendant Cadet, bestowed noble title as Marquess Within the Passes. The concubines bestowed on [Xú] Shào and the rest, with the male and female family servants that are here, are to all be permitted to accompany them, to make clear the state’s grace, and they are not required to return, to widely spread great faith.”

冬十月丁亥，詔曰：「昔聖帝明王，靜亂濟世，保大定功，文武殊塗，勳烈同歸。是故或舞干戚以訓不庭，或陳師旅以威暴慢。至于愛民全國，康惠庶類，必先脩文教，示之軌儀，不得已然後用兵，此盛德之所同也。往者季漢分崩，九土顛覆，劉備、孫權乘間作禍。三祖綏寧中夏，日不暇給，遂使遺寇僭逆歷世。幸賴宗廟威靈，宰輔忠武，爰發四方，拓定庸、蜀，役不浹時，一征而克。自頃江表衰弊，政刑荒闇，巴、漢平定，孤危無援，交、荊、揚、越，靡然向風。今交阯偽將呂興已帥三郡，萬里歸命；武陵邑侯相嚴等糾合五縣，請為臣妾；豫章廬陵山民舉眾叛吳，以助北將軍為號。又孫休病死，主帥改易，國內乖違，人各有心。偽將施績，賊之名臣，懷疑自猜，深見忌惡。眾叛親離，莫有固志，自古及今，未有亡徵若此之甚。若六軍震曜，南臨江、漢，吳會之域必扶老攜幼以迎王師，必然之理也。然興動大眾，猶有勞費，宜告喻威德，開示仁信，使知順附和同之利。相國參軍事徐紹、水曹掾孫彧，昔在壽春，並見虜獲。紹本偽南陵督，才質開壯；彧，孫權支屬，忠良見事。其遣紹南還，以彧為副，宣揚國命，告喻吳人，諸所示語，皆以事實，若其覺悟，不損征伐之計，蓋廟勝長算，自古之道也。其以紹兼散騎常侍，加奉車都尉，封都亭侯；彧兼給事黃門侍郎，賜爵關內侯。紹等所賜妾及男女家人在此者，悉聽自隨，以明國恩，不必使還，以開廣大信。」

Appraisal states: In antiquity as Heaven’s Under is public, only by virtue was it shared. Later eras inherited ranks, enthroning sons as heirs. If the hereditary succession was not continued, then it is appropriate to select a close relative of enlightenment and virtue, like Hàn’s Wén and Xuān. This is an unchanging constant standard. Emperor Míng [Cáo Ruì] did not do this, his feelings clung to personal affection, adopting and raising an infant, passing on the great vessels, the entrustment was not focused, insisted on including the branch clans, and in the end Cáo Shuǎng was executed and exterminated, the King of Qí [Cáo Fāng] replaced on the throne. The Duke of Gāoguì village [Cáo Máo] was talented and intelligent and early to mature, enjoyed inquiry and valued literature, overall also having Emperor Wén’s [Cáo Pī’s] manner and legacy, however he was reckless and impatient, angered and unrestrained, and brought on himself great disaster. The King of Chénliú [Cáo Huàn] was himself deferential in facing south, the governing assistant [Sīmǎ Zhāo] controlled the government, looking up to comply with previous forms, he saluted and yielded and so abdicated, therefore enjoying fief of a large state, becoming the Guest to Jìn.
