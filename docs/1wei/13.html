13  Biographies of Zhōng Yáo, Huà Xīn, Wáng Lǎng

Zhōng Yáo

Zhōng Yáo appellation Yuáncháng was a Yǐngchuān Chángshè man. [1] Once with his distant cousin’s father [Zhōng] Yú both arrived at Luòyáng, on the road encountered a physiognomist who said: “This child has physiognomy of nobility, however he will have distress in water, work hard to be careful of it!” Traveled not yet ten lǐ, crossed a bridge, the horse was alarmed, he fell in the river and nearly died. [Zhōng] Yú, because the physiognomist’s words were correct, increasingly valued [Zhōng] Yáo, and so provided for his expenses, allowing him to focus on study. Nominated Filial-Incorruptible,1 [2] appointed Secretariat Writer Cadet, Yánglíng Magistrate,2 due to illness left. Recruited to the Three Offices, became Minister of Justice Rectifier, Yellow Gate Attendant Cadet. At the time, the Hàn Emperor was at the western capital, Lǐ Jué and Guō Sì and the rest made chaos inside Cháng’ān, with the peast of the passes cut off. Tàizǔ was designate Yǎn Province Governor, had just sent envoy to send up letter. [3] [Lǐ] Jué and [Guō] Sì and the rest believed: “The east of the passes wish to establish their own Heaven’s Son, now although Cáo Cāo has sent envoy mission, it is not his genuine intent,” and discussed detaining Tàizǔ’s envoy and refusing his intentions. [Zhōng] Yáo persuaded [Lǐ] Jué and [Guō] Sì and the rest stating: “Right now outstanding heroes are all rising, each feigning commands to claim authority, only Cáo Yǎnzhōu3 devotes to the Royal House, but to yet oppose his loyal devotion, is not how to support the expectations of the future.” [Lǐ] Jué and [Guō] Sì and the rest used [Zhōng] Yáo’s advice, generously applied answer and reply, because of this Tàizǔ’s envoy mission therefore was able to be connected. Tàizǔ had already repeatedly heard Xún Yù’s praise of [Zhōng] Yáo, also heard of his persuading [Lǐ] Jué and [Guō] Sì, and was increasingly open in heart. Later [Lǐ] Jué seized Heaven’s Son, [Zhōng] Yáo with Secretariat Writers Cadet Hán Bīn together strategized. In Heaven’s Son being able to exit Cháng’ān, [Zhōng] Yáo had constributions. Appointed Censorate Central Deputy, promoted to Attendant Central Secretariat Writer Deputy-Director, further in recognition of previous achievements enfeoffed as Marquess of Dōngwǔ Precinct.

鍾繇字元常，潁川長社人也。〔一〕嘗與族父瑜俱至洛陽，道遇相者，曰：「此童有貴相，然當厄於水，努力慎之！」行未十里，度橋，馬驚，墮水幾死。瑜以相者言中，益貴繇，而供給資費，使得專學。舉孝廉，〔二〕除尚書郎、陽陵令，以疾去。辟三府，為廷尉正、黃門侍郎。是時，漢帝在西京，李傕、郭汜等亂長安中，與關東斷絕。太祖領兗州牧，始遣使上書。〔三〕傕、汜等以為「關東欲自立天子，今曹操雖有使命，非其至實」，議留太祖使，拒絕其意。繇說傕、汜等曰：「方今英雄並起，各矯命專制，唯曹兗州乃心王室，而逆其忠款，非所以副將來之望也。」傕、汜等用繇言，厚加答報，由是太祖使命遂得通。太祖既數聽荀彧之稱繇，又聞其說傕、汜，益虛心。後傕脅天子，繇與尚書郎韓斌同策謀。天子得出長安，繇有力焉。拜御史中丞，遷侍中尚書僕射，并錄前功封東武亭侯。

[1] Xiānxián Xíngzhuàng states: Zhōng Hào appellation Jìmíng, warm and gentle, sincere and cautious, widely learned in poetry and legal codes, instructed students of over a thousand people, became Prefectural Merit Officer. At the time Tàiqiū Chief Chén Shí was West Gate Precinct Chief,4 [Zhōng] Hào deeply respected and thought him extraordinary. [Chén] Shí was younger than [Zhōng] Hào by seventeen years, always courteously treated him and shared friendly relationship. It happened he was recruited by the Offices of the Excellencies, when about to take leave, the Administrator asked: “Who can succeed you sir?” [Zhōng] Hào said: “If Your Enlightened Office5 wishes to certainly obtain the person, the West Gate Precinct Chief can be employed.” [Chén] Shí said: “Lord Zhōng seems to not take examining men as a focus, I do not know why he alone recognizes me.” [Zhōng] Hào became an Excellency over the Masses Assistant, the Excellency went out, the road was muddy, the guides and followers hated being sprayed, and kept far away from the Excellency Carriage. The Excellency hammered the handrail saying: “Today the Excellency over the Masses is merely traveling alone!” Returning to the Office and facing the side doors, the bellman did not support him, when greeting the assistants and associates,6 the Excellency dismissively waved his hand and did not look back. At the time the entire Office’s assistants and associates all resigned7 and exited. [Zhōng] Hào was West Department Assistant, at once opened the Office Gates to scatter explanatory speech to those that had already exited, stating: “Subordinate ministers unable to prove themselves correct to their lord, if the Director of Retainers8 cites regulations9 that the Excellency lost the etiquitte of a high minister and also not competent in office, how will you sirs to the end of your lives ever be appointed?” The assistants and associates because of this all stopped. The Capital Offices indeed went to the West Department Assistant to investigate the empty Office and meaning of the departures, [Zhōng] Hào summoned the Capital Office clerks, to see the assistants and associates and show the names, and so it stopped. Beginning to end he was nine times recruited by the Three Offices, promoted to Nánxiāng and Línlǜ Chief,10 did not go to office. At the time inside the prefecture the elder generations that Within the Seas submitted toward were Cāngwú Administrator Dìnglíng’s Chén Zhìshū, former Líyáng Magistrate Yǐngyīn’s Xún Shū, and [Zhōng] Hào. Minister Treasurer Lǐ Yīng took as exemplars these three men stating: “Lord Xún’s pure discernment is difficult to surpass, Chén’s and Zhōng’s utmost vertiue can be a teacher.” [Lǐ] Yīng’s father’s sister was [Zhōng] Hào’s elder brother’s wife, begat a son [Zhōng] Jìn, with [Lǐ] Yīng equal in age, both having good reputation. [Zhōng] Jìn also enjoyed study and admired antiquity, had deferential conduct. When a child, [Lǐ] Yīng’s grandfather Grand Commandant [Lǐ] Xiū said: “[Zhōng] Jìn seems to have our family’s nature, if the state has the way he will not deposed, if the state is without the way he will avoid execution.” He further had [Lǐ] Yīng’s younger sister married as a wife to him. [Zhōng] Jìn was recruited by the provincial governor, never once submitted to accept. [Lǐ] Yīng spoke to [Zhōng] Jìn stating: “Mèng Kē believed a man without heart of likes and dislikes and right and wrong was not a man. Younger brother as a man why are you too lacking in black-white (discrimination)?” [Zhōng] Jìn once told [Lǐ] Yīng’s words to [Zhōng] Hào, [Zhōng] Hào said: “For Yuánlǐ, his grandfather is an Excellency in position, his various uncles all are flourishing, and he is Excellency Hán’s siter’s son, only because of that he is able to be that way. Guó Wǔzǐ enjoyed finding other people’s faults, becoming a root of resentment. Now how is this the time for that? Protecting self and preserving family, your way is correct.” [Zhōng] Jìn early died, although [Lǐ] Yīng carried achievements and fame, rank reaching the minister assistants, yet in the end he died in the era’s disasters. [Zhōng] Hào aged sixty-nine died at home. [Zhōng] Hào’s two sons [Zhōng] Dí and [Zhōng] Fū both due to the Partisan Prohibitions did not servce in office. [Zhōng] Yáo then was [Zhōng] Dí’s grandson.11

〔一〕　先賢行狀曰：鍾皓字季明，溫良篤慎，博學詩律，教授門生千有餘人，為郡功曹。時太丘長陳寔為西門亭長，皓深獨敬異。寔少皓十七歲，常禮待與同分義。會辟公府，臨辭，太守問：「誰可代君？」皓曰：「明府欲必得其人，西門亭長可用。」寔曰：「鍾君似不察人為意，不知何獨識我？」皓為司徒掾，公出，道路泥濘，導從惡其相灑，去公車絕遠。公椎軾言：「司徒今日為獨行耳！」還府向閤，鈴下不扶，令揖掾屬，公奮手不顧。時舉府掾屬皆投劾出，皓為西曹掾，即開府門分布曉語已出者，曰：「臣下不能得自直於君，若司隸舉繩墨，以公失宰相之禮，又不勝任，諸君終身何所任邪？」掾屬以故皆止。都官果移西曹掾，問空府去意，皓召都官吏，以見掾屬名示之，乃止。前後九辟三府，遷南鄉、林慮長，不之官。時郡中先輩為海內所歸者，蒼梧太守定陵陳稚叔、故黎陽令潁陰荀淑及皓。少府李膺常宗此三人，曰：「荀君清識難尚，陳、鍾至德可師。」膺之姑為皓兄之妻，生子覲，與膺年齊，並有令名。覲又好學慕古，有退讓之行。為童幼時，膺祖太尉脩言：「覲似我家性，國有道不廢，國無道免于刑戮者也。」復以膺妹妻之。覲辟州宰，未嘗屈就。膺謂覲曰：「孟軻以為人無好惡是非之心，非人也。弟於人何太無皂白邪！」覲嘗以膺之言白皓，皓曰：「元禮，祖公在位，諸父並盛，韓公之甥，故得然耳。國武子好招人過，以為怨本，今豈其時！保身全家，汝道是也。」覲早亡，膺雖荷功名，位至卿佐，而卒隕身世禍。皓年六十九，終於家。皓二子迪、敷，並以黨錮不仕。繇則迪之孫。

〔三〕　世語曰：太祖遣使從事王必致命天子。

At the time Guānzhōng’s various officers Mǎ Téng, Hán Suì and others each gathered strong troops and fought one another. Tàizǔ was about to have affairs east of the mountains, believed the west of the passes were a concern, and so memorialized [Zhōng] Yáo as Attendant Central observing Director of Retainers Colonel, Holding Stave to supervise Guānzhōng’s various armies, entrusting him with rear affairs, specially empowered to not be confined by legal code regulations. [Zhōng] Yáo reached Cháng’ān, sent letters to [Mǎ] Téng and [Hán] Suì and the rest, explaining the disasters and fortunes to them, [Mǎ] Téng and [Hán] Suì each sent sons to enter as attendants. When Tàizǔ was at Guāndù, with Yuán Shào locked together, [Zhōng] Yáo sent horses of over two thousand to provide for the army. Tàizǔ’s letter to [Zhōng] Yáo stated: “Obtaining the sent horses, it extremely answered to the emergencies. The west of the passes pacified and settled, the Court without worry of looking back west, is your accomplishment sir. In the past Xiāo Hé defended Guānzhōng, sufficient provisions to form the army, you also are comparable to this.” Afterward the Xiōngnú Chányú made chaos in Píngyáng, [Zhōng] Yáo commanded the various armies to besiege him, had not yet captured it when [Yuán] Shàng’s installed Hédōng Administrator Guō Yuán arrived at Hédōng, multitudes extremely flourisihing. The various officers discussed wishing to lift the siege and leave, [Zhōng] Yáo said: “The Yuán clan is currently powerful. At the coming of [Guō] Yuán, Guānzhōng secretly communicates with him. That they have not yet completely rebelled, is only in consideration for my prestige and reputation. If we abandon this and leave, it shows them we are weak, and of the people of these places, who would not be bandit and enemy? Even if we wished to return home, could we reach it? This is to when not yet battled in advance destory oneself. Moreover [Guō] Yuán is stubborn and obstinate and eager to win, certainly will think lightly of our army. If he crosses the Fén to make camp, when he has not yet crossed striking him, can be a great victory.” Zhāng Jì advised Mǎ Téng to join in striking [Guō] Yuán and [Mǎ] Téng sent his son [Mǎ] Chāo to lead elite troops to oppose him. [Guō] Yuán arrived, indeed recklessly crossed the Fén, the masses stopped him, he did not listen. Crossing the river and not yet halfway, struck, greatly defeated him, [4] beheaded [Guō] Yuán, forced the surrender of the Chányú. The story is in [Zhāng] Jì’s biography. Afterward Hédōng’s Wèi Gù made chaos, with Zhāng Chéng, Zhāng Yǎn, and Gāo Gàn and others together acting as bandits, [Zhōng] Yáo again led the various officers to suppress and defeated them. [5] From since Heaven’s Son was moved west, Luòyáng’s population was completely exhausted, [Zhōng] Yáo relocated Guānzhōng people, and also recruited and accepted fugitives and deserters to fill it, within several years the registered people gradually filled. When Tàizǔ campaigned against Guānzhōng, he obtained them as resources, memorialized [Zhōng] Yáo as Front Army Preceptor.

時關中諸將馬騰、韓遂等，各擁彊兵相與爭。太祖方有事山東，以關右為憂。乃表繇以侍中守司隸校尉，持節督關中諸軍，委之以後事，特使不拘科制。繇至長安，移書騰、遂等，為陳禍福，騰、遂各遣子入侍。太祖在官渡，與袁紹相持，繇送馬二千餘匹給軍。太祖與繇書曰：「得所送馬，甚應其急。關右平定，朝廷無西顧之憂，足下之勳也。昔蕭何鎮守關中，足食成軍，亦適當爾。」其後匈奴單于作亂平陽，繇帥諸軍圍之，未拔；而袁尚所置河東太守郭援到河東，眾甚盛。諸將議欲釋之去，繇曰：「袁氏方彊，援之來，關中陰與之通，所以未悉叛者，顧吾威名故耳。若棄而去，示之以弱，所在之民，誰非寇讎？縱吾欲歸，其得至乎！此為未戰先自敗也。且援剛愎好勝，必易吾軍，若渡汾為營，及其未濟擊之，可大克也。」張既說馬騰會擊援，騰遣子超將精兵逆之。援至，果輕渡汾，眾止之，不從。濟水未半，擊，大破之，〔一〕斬援，降單于。語在既傳。其後河東衛固作亂，與張晟、張琰及高幹等並為寇，繇又率諸將討破之。〔二〕自天子西遷，洛陽人民單盡，繇徙關中民，又招納亡叛以充之，數年間民戶稍實。太祖征關中，得以為資，表繇為前軍師。

[4] Sīmǎ Biāo’s Zhànlüè states: Yuán Shàng sent Gāo Gàn and Guō Yuán commanding troops of several tens of thousands of men, with the Xiōngnú Chányú plundering Hédōng, sent envoy to with Mǎ Téng and Hán Suì and others join in alliance, [Mǎ] Téng and the rest secretly agreed to it. Fù Gàn advised [Mǎ] Téng stating: “The ancients had a saying: ‘Those that follow the way prosper, those that oppose virtue perish.’ Cáo-Gōng serves Heaven’s Son and executs the violent and chaotic, laws clear and state governed, above and below obey command, those with righteousness certainly rewarded, those without righteousness certainly punished, it can be called to follow the way. The Yuán clan turns back on Royal command, drives on the Hú caitiffs to oppress the central states, lenient yet with many jealousies, benevolent yet without decisiveness, although their troops are strong, in fact they have lost the hearts Under Heaven, it can be called to oppose virtue. Now you General already serve those with the way, do not exhaustively devote your strength, secretly embrace both sides, wishing to sit and watch for success or failure. I fear that when success or failure is decided, in presenting statements to reproach and punish, you General will be first to be executed.” Thereupon [Mǎ] Téng was afraid. [Fù] Gàn said: “The wise turn disaster into fortune. Now Cáo-gōng and the Yuán clan are locked together, and Gāo Gàn and Guō Yuán alone control Hédōng. Even if Cáo-gōng has an absolutely sure plan, he cannot force Hédōng to not be endangered. General you truly are capable of leading troops to suppress [Guō] Yuán, inside and outside striking them, the situation is to certainly succeed. This is for you General in one move to cut off an arm of the Yuán clan, resolve the emergencies of one region, Cáo-gōng certainly will heavily value your virtue General. Xún Qīng also said, those that kill someone die, those that wound someone are punished, in this the Hundred Kings were alike, there are none that know how it came about. It is for those about to kill someone to not necessarily die, those that wish to wound others to not necessarily be punished. Releasing them without moral transformation then is to entrap them in punishment and execution. Therefore what punishments regulate is on grounds that cannot be moved. 〔一〕　袁宏曰：夫民心樂全而不能常全，蓋利用之物懸於外，而嗜慾之情動於內也。於是有進取貪競之行，希求放肆之事。進取不已，不能充其嗜慾，則苟且僥倖之所生也；希求無厭，無以愜其慾，則姦偽忿怒之所興也。先王知其如此，而欲救其弊，或先德化以陶其心；其心不化，然後加以刑辟。書曰：「百姓不親，五品不遜。汝作司徒而敬敷五教。蠻夷猾夏，寇賊姦宄。汝作士，五刑有服。」然則德、刑之設，參而用之者也。三代相因，其義詳焉。周禮：「使墨者守門，劓者守關，宮者守內，刖者守囿。」此肉刑之制可得而論者也。荀卿亦云，殺人者死，傷人者刑，百王之所同，未有知其所由來者也。夫殺人者死，而相殺者不已，是大辟可以懲未殺，不能使天下無殺也。傷人者刑，而害物者不息，是黥、劓可以懼未刑，不能使天下無刑也。故將欲止之，莫若先以德化。夫罪過彰著，然後入于刑辟，是將殺人者不必死，欲傷人者不必刑。縱而弗化，則陷於刑辟。故刑之所制，在於不可移之地。禮教則不然，明其善惡，所以潛勸其情，消之於未殺也；示之恥辱，所以內愧其心，治之於未傷也。故過微而不至於著，罪薄而不及於刑。終入罪辟者，非教化之所得也，故雖殘一物之生，刑一人之體，是除天下之害，夫何傷哉！率斯道也，風化可以漸淳，刑罰可以漸少，其理然也。苟不能化其心，而專任刑罰，民失義方，動罹刑網，求世休和，焉可得哉？周之成、康，豈按三千之文而致刑錯之美乎？蓋德化漸漬，致斯有由也。漢初懲酷刑之弊，務寬厚之論，公卿大夫，相與恥言人過。文帝登朝，加以玄默。張武受賂，賜金以愧其心；吳王不朝，崇禮以訓其失。是以吏民樂業，風流篤厚，斷獄四百，幾致刑措，豈非德刑兼用已然之效哉？世之欲言刑罰之用，不先德教之益，失之遠矣。今大辟之罪，與古同制。免死已下，不過五歲，既釋鉗鎖，復得齒于人倫。是以民無恥惡，數為姦盜，故刑徒多而亂不治也。苟教之所去，罰當其罪，一離刀鋸，沒身不齒，鄰里且猶恥之，而況于鄉黨乎？而況朝廷乎？如此，則夙沙、趙高之儔，無施其惡矣。古者察其言，觀其行，而善惡彰焉。然則君子之去刑辟，固已遠矣。過誤不幸，則八議之所宥也。若夫卞和、史遷之冤，淫刑之所及也。苟失其道，或不免於大辟，而況肉刑哉！漢書：「斬右趾及殺人先自言告，吏坐受賕，守官物而即盜之，皆棄巿。」此班固所謂當生而令死者也。今不忍刻截之慘，而安剿絕之悲，此最治體之所先，有國所宜改者也。

[38] Wèi lüè states: [Dǒng] Yù appellation Jìzhí, by nature was inarticulate but enjoyed study. During Xīngpíng [194 - 195], Guānzhōng was disturbed in chaos, with his elder brother Jìzhōng he depended on General Duàn Wēi. He foraged for grain and carried goods to sell, but always carried classic books, taking leisure moments to study and read. His elder brother laughed at him but [Dǒng] Yù did not change. Reaching Jiàn’ān’s beginning, the Royal Network was slightly established, the prefecture nominated him Filial-Incorruptible, gradually promoted to Yellow Gate Attendant Cadet. At the time, the Hàn Emperor entrusted the government to Tàizǔ, [Dǒng] Yù dawn to dusk attended and lectured, and was favored and trusted by Heaven’s Son. Reaching the Twenty-Second Year [217], inside Xǔ the Hundred Officials feigned Regulations, although [Dǒng] Yù did not participate in the plot, yet he was noted and sent to Yè, transferred to the Surplus. Once he followed Tàizǔ on western campaign, the road went through Mèngjīn, passing by the burial mound of the King of Hóngnóng.84 Tàizǔ was doubtful and wished to visit it, and looked back to ask his Left and Right, of the Left and Right none replied, [Dǒng] Yù then crossed the ranks to advance stating: “In the principles of the Chūnqiū, a state’s lord that succeeded the throne that did not exceed a year and yet died, has not yet become a lord. The King of Hóngnóng’s succession ascension was already shallow, he also was by a cruel minister controlled, reduced to a vassal state, it is not appropriate to visit.” Tàizǔ thus passed by. During Huángchū, he was sent out as a prefecture Administrator. In Emperor Míng’s time, he entered as an Attendant Central, Minister of Agriculture. In several years he died of illness. Previously, [Dǒng] Yù excelled in researching the Lǎo-zǐ, and for the Lǎo-zǐ composed teaching annotations. He also excelled in the Zuǒzhì Zhuàn, and further composed vermilion and black ink distinguishing of differences.85 Someone came to follow him in study, [Dǒng] Yù would not agree to teach, but said: “You must first read a hundred times.” He said: “Read a book a hundred times and the meaning will itself appear.” The follower in study said: “I suffer a lack of time.” [Dǒng] Yù said: “You must use the three surpluses.” Someone asked the meaning of three surpluses, [Dǒng] Yù said: “Winter is the surplus of the year, night is the surplus of the day, cloud and rain is the surplus of hours.” Because of this of various students few followed [Dǒng] Yù, and there was no transmission of his vermilion and black.

Shìyǔ states: [Dǒng] Yù’s son [Dǒng] Suí, position reached Confidential Writers Overseer, also had talent and learning. The King of Qí [Sīmǎ] Jiǒng’s meritorious minister Dǒng Ài was [Dǒng] Suí’s son.

Wèi lüè has [Dǒng] Yù with Jiǎ Hóng, Hándān Chún, Xuē Xià, Wěi Xǐ, Sū Lín, Yuè Xiáng altogether seven people as Classicist Exemplars, its preface states: “From the inauguration of Chūpíng [190], reaching the end of Jiàn’ān [220], Heaven’s Under was divided and collapsed, people full of carelessness, the social network already declined, with the classicist ways very extreme. Reaching after Huángchū Inaugural Year [220], the new Ruler then restored, first sweept and cleaned the dust and ash of the Grand Academy, mended the gaps and breakages of the stone steles, filled the personnel registers of Academic Scholars, relied on Hàn’s Jiǎ-Yǐ for examinations.86 Extended announcements to the provinces and prefectures, that any that wished to study, all be sent to visit the Grand Academy. When the Grand Academy first opened, there were disciples of several hundred people. Reaching to during Tàihé [227 - 233] and Qīnglóng [233 - 237], the central and exterior had many affairs, people wished to avoid work. Even those that by nature did not understand scholarship, many requested to go to the Grand Academy. The Grand Academy various students numbered in the thousands, but the various Academic Scholars tended to all be coarse and negligent, unable to instruct disciples. The disciples originally also were avoiding conscription, indeed without ability of study and scholarship, in winter coming and in spring leaving, year after year it was like this. Also, even though there were elite ones, yet the Terrace Pavilion nomination standards were too high, and additionally they did not focus on mastering the overall meanings, but questioned around word meanings and ink methods and punctuation annotations, out of a hundred people tested together, those that passed did not reach ten. Therefore scholars of ambition in study then again declined, while those that sought the superficial and empty all competed. During Zhèngshǐ [240 - 249], there was Edict to discuss Huánqiū, invisting all scholars. At the time the Cadet offices and Excellency over the Masses’s managed clerks were over twenty thousand people, although further spread out, those seen at the capital region were still ten thousand people, but the answering letters to participate in the discussion were overall hardly any people. Also at the time the Court and Hall Excellencies and Ministers on down were over four hundred people, those able to hold a brush were not even ten people, most all followed one another to eat their fill and then retire. Alas! The decline and fall of scholarly work, then reached to this! Therefore my private heart always insignificantly valued those few elders, that each were in the middle of desolation and chaos, yet able to maintain their will all the more sincerely.”

Jiǎ Hóng appellation Shūyè was a Jīngzhào Xīnfēng man. Enjoyed study and had talent, and specially proficient in the Chūnqiū Zuǒ Zhuàn. At Jiàn’ān’s beginning, served in the prefecture, nominated an Accounting Assistant,87 answered the province’s recruitment. At the time in the province from the Advisors to Army Affairs on down were over a hundred people, only [Jiǎ] Hóng with Féngyì’s Yán Bāo in literary comprehension and talent and scholarship were the most high. [Jiǎ] Hóng successively served as Magistrate in three counties, wherever he was then he opened and cleared stables and houses, personally teaching various students. Later Mǎ Chāo rebelled, [Mǎ] Chāo seized [Jiǎ] Hóng, brought him to visit Huáyīn, having him compose a spread announcement. [Jiǎ] Hóng had no choice, and composed it. Excellency over the Masses Zhōng Yáo was in the east, recognized his writing, and said: “This is Jiǎ Hóng’s composition.” Reaching to when [Mǎ] Chāo was defeated and fled, Tàizǔ summoned [Jiǎ] Hóng and installed him as an Army Planning Assistant, yet as he had previously for [Mǎ] Chāo composed the spread announcement document, therefore he was not immediately assessed. Later he was then sent out as Yīnquán Chief. During Yánkāng [220], transferred to the King of Báimǎ’s Chancellor. He excelled in ability to converse and joke. The King [Cáo] Biāo also was refined and enjoyed literary scholarship, always taking him as a teacher and exemplar, surpassing his Three Ministers. In several years he died of illness, when he died he was aged over fifty. At the time people on his behalf regretted that his career did not reach to the Two Thousand Shí level. But Yán Bāo also successively served in two counties, during Huángchū, due to high talent entered as Confidential Writers Deputy, repeatedly presenting writings and rhapsodies, Emperor Wén was amazed with him, sent out as Xīpíng Administrator, died in office.

Xuē Xià appellation Xuānshēng was a Tiānshuǐ man. Widely learned and had talent. Tiānshuǐ of old had the Jiāng, Yán, Rén, Zhào Four Surnames, always foremost inside the prefecture, and [Xuē] Xià was from a humble family but would not submit or bow. The Four Surnames wished to together punish him, [Xuē] Xià thus travelled to escape, east visiting the capital region. Tàizǔ had formerly heard his reputation, and extremely courteously received him. Later the Four Surnames also used a prisoner to remotely implicate [Xuē] Xià, dispatch sent to Yǐngchuān, arrested and seized, bound in prison. At the time Tàizǔ was already at Jì Province, heard that [Xuē] Xià had been taken prisoner by his home prefecture, patted hands and said: “[Xuē] Xià is without crime, those Hànyáng boys simply wish to kill him!”88 Thus announced to Yǐngchuān to investigate and release him, summoned and installed an Army Planning Assistant. Emperor Wén also commended his talent, during Huángchū he became a Confidential Writers Deputy, every time the Emperor with [Xuē] Xià discussed and analyzed books and commentaries, never once was it not to end of day. Every time in calling him it was not by personal name, but said Xuē-jūn. [Xuē] Xià lived in extreme poverty, the Emperor also noticed his clothes were thin and took off his own imperial gown he was wearing to bestow on him. After this the Campaigning East General Cáo Xiū came to Court, at the time the Emperor was with [Xuē] Xià consulting and discussing, and then the exterior informed that [Cáo] Xiū had arrived, the Emperor invited him to enter. When seated, the Emperor looked back to [Xuē] Xià and spoke of him to [Cáo] Xiū stating: “This sir is the Confidential Writers Deputy, Tiānsuǐ’s Xuē Xuānshēng, you chould converse together.” He met with treatment like this. Soon wishing to employ him, it happened that Emperor Wén passed away. Reaching to durying Tàihé, once he had a public matter transferred to Lán Terrace. Lán Terrace itself believed it was a Terrace, while the Confidential Documents was a bureau, saying [Xuē] Xià should not have transferred it, pushing to have him implicated in connection. [Xuē] Xià replied to it stating: “Lán Terrace is an exterior Terrace, the Confidential Writers is an Inner Pavilion, the Terrace and Pavilions are unified, how can there be no transfer between them?” Lán Terrace conceded with nothing to retort.89 From this on afterward, therefore it became the standard. After several years he died of illness, ordered his children to not return to Tiānshuǐ.

Wěi Xǐ appellation Zǐyá was a Jīngzhào man. Of a generationally humble family, in youth he enjoyed study. During Chūpíng [190 - 193], the Three Adjuncts were in chaos,90 [Wěi] Xǐ went south to be a guest in Jīng Province, not disturbed by the desolation, carried on back classic books, every time he had extra days from foraging, then he recited and studied them. When Tàizǔ settled Jīng Province, summoned and installed him as an Army Planning Assistant. During Huángchū, he became a King of Qiáo’s Cadet Central. The King formerly had heard he was a classicist, always was open-hearted and followed in study. [Wěi] Xǐ also respectfully instructed the King, and from this greatly obtained bestowments and gifts. Due to illness he returned, appointed a Cadet Central. Aged over eighty, due to old age he stayed home, those that came to him to study were extremely many. [Wěi] Xǐ understood the classics, and also excelled in astronomy, always looking up to gaze at Heaven’s signs, sighed and said to Yú Huàn stating: “Heaven’s Under’s weapons and spears are still not yet ceased, why is it like this?” [Yú] Huàn also always followed and asked of the Zuǒ-shì Zhuàn, [Wěi] Xǐ answered stating: “To wish to know the subtle nothing compares to the Yì. In the coherence of human relationships nothing compares to the Lǐ. To know the many names of mountains and rivers and grasses and trees nothing compares to the Shī. The Zuǒ-shì is simply a book about chopping down one another, not worth thorough attention.” [Yú] Huàn therefore followed and asked of the Shī, [Wěi] Xǐ explained the Qí, Hán, Lǔ, Máo four schools of interpretations, no longer holding the texts, reciting like chanting. Also he compiled and composed explanations to the classics of several hundred thousand words, had not yet reached to transcribed copying but became deaf, after several years died of illness.

Hándān Chún’s matters are in Wáng Càn’s biography, Sū Lín’s matters are in Liú Shào and Gāotáng Lóng’s biographies, Yuè Xiáng’s matters are in Dù Jī’s biography.

Yú Huàn states: Studying as a resource for people, is it like indigo as a dye for raw silk? Therefore even Zhòngní yet said: “I was not born knowing it.” All the more for ordinary sorts! Moreover the reason wordly people do not value study, certainly is because of seeing people that ‘recite three hundred poems but yet unable to particularly respond to the Four Quarters.’ I believe this then is merely the lowest level, not suitable for considering the middle and above, with character and substance appropriately fitting and adding to it with literary refinement. Now these several worthies, are overall those I know. Examining their matters and abilities, truly they are not many. Yet for maintaining study without stopping, then above they were commended by Emperors and Kings, below they were the country’s famed classicists, was it not through study? By this observing it, how can study be stopped?

〔三〕　魏略曰：遇字季直，性質訥而好學。興平中，關中擾亂，與兄季中依將軍段煨。采稆負販，而常挾持經書，投閒習讀。其兄笑之而遇不改。及建安初，王綱小設，郡舉孝廉，稍遷黃門侍郎。是時，漢帝委政太祖，遇旦夕侍講，為天子所愛信。至二十二年，許中百官矯制，遇雖不與謀，猶被錄詣鄴，轉為宂散。常從太祖西征，道由孟津，過弘農王冢。太祖疑欲謁，顧問左右，左右莫對，遇乃越第進曰：「春秋之義，國君即位未踰年而卒，未成為君。弘農王即阼既淺，又為暴臣所制，降在藩國，不應謁。」太祖乃過。黃初中，出為郡守。明帝時，入為侍中、大司農。數年，病亡。初，遇善治老子，為老子作訓注。又善左氏傳，更為作朱墨別異。人有從學者，遇不肯教，而云「必當先讀百遍」。言「讀書百遍而義自見」。從學者云：「苦渴無日。」遇言「當以三餘」。或問三餘之意，遇言「冬者歲之餘，夜者日之餘，陰雨者時之餘也」。由是諸生少從遇學，無傳其朱墨者。世語曰：遇子綏，位至祕書監，亦有才學。齊王冏功臣董艾，即綏之子也。魏略以遇及賈洪、邯鄲淳、薛夏、隗禧、蘇林、樂詳等七人為儒宗，其序曰：「從初平之元，至建安之末，天下分崩，人懷苟且，綱紀既衰，儒道尤甚。至黃初元年之後，新主乃復，始掃除太學之灰炭，補舊石碑之缺壞，備博士之員錄，依漢甲乙以考課。申告州郡，有欲學者，皆遣詣太學。太學始開，有弟子數百人。至太和、青龍中，中外多事，人懷避就。雖性非解學，多求詣太學。太學諸生有千數，而諸博士率皆麄疏，無以教弟子。弟子本亦避役，竟無能習學，冬來春去，歲歲如是。又雖有精者，而臺閣舉格太高，加不念統其大義，而問字指墨法點注之間，百人同試，度者未十。是以志學之士，遂復陵遲，而末求浮虛者各競逐也。正始中，有詔議圜丘，普延學士。是時郎官及司徒領吏二萬餘人，雖復分布，見在京師者尚且萬人，而應書與議者略無幾人。又是時朝堂公卿以下四百餘人，其能操筆者未有十人，多皆相從飽食而退。嗟夫！學業沈隕，乃至於此。是以私心常區區貴乎數公者，各處荒亂之際，而能守志彌敦者也。」賈洪字叔業，京兆新豐人也。好學有才，而特精於春秋左傳。建安初，仕郡，舉計掾，應州辟。時州中自參軍事以下百餘人，唯洪與馮翊嚴苞（交）〔文〕通才學最高。洪歷守三縣令，所在輒開除廄舍，親授諸生。後馬超反，超劫洪，將詣華陰，使作露布。洪不獲已，為作之。司徒鍾繇在東，識其文，曰：「此賈洪作也。」及超破走，太祖召洪署軍謀掾。猶以其前為超作露布文，故不即敘。晚乃出為陰泉長。延康中，轉為白馬王相。善能談戲。王彪亦雅好文學，常師宗之，過於三卿。數歲病亡，亡時年五十餘，時人為之恨仕不至二千石。而嚴苞亦歷守二縣，黃初中，以高才入為祕書丞，數奏文賦，文帝異之。出為西平太守，卒官。薛夏字宣聲，天水人也。博學有才。天水舊有姜、閻、任、趙四姓，常推於郡中，而夏為單家，不為降屈。四姓欲共治之，夏乃游逸，東詣京師。太祖宿聞其名，甚禮遇之。後四姓又使囚遙引夏，關移潁川，收捕繫獄。時太祖已在冀州，聞夏為本郡所質，撫掌曰：「夏無罪也。漢陽兒輩直欲殺之耳！」乃告潁川使理出之，召署軍謀掾。文帝又嘉其才，黃初中為祕書丞，帝每與夏推論書傳，未嘗不終日也。每呼之不名，而謂之薛君。夏居甚貧，帝又顧其衣薄，解所御服袍賜之。其後征東將軍曹休來朝，時帝方與夏有所咨論，而外啟休到，帝引入。坐定，帝顧夏言之於休曰：「此君，祕書丞天水薛宣聲也，宜共談。」其見遇如此。尋欲用之，會文帝崩。至太和中，嘗以公事移蘭臺。蘭臺自以臺也，而祕書署耳，謂夏為不得移也，推使當有坐者。夏報之曰：「蘭臺為外臺，祕書為內閣，臺、閣，一也，何不相移之有？」蘭臺屈無以折。自是之後，遂以為常。後數歲病亡，敕其子無還天水。隗禧字子牙，京兆人也。世單家。少好學。初平中，三輔亂，禧南客荊州，不以荒擾，擔負經書，每以採稆餘日，則誦習之。太祖定荊州，召署軍謀掾。黃初中，為譙王郎中。王宿聞其儒者，常虛心從學。禧亦敬恭以授王，由是大得賜遺。以病還，拜郎中。年八十餘，以老處家，就之學者甚多。禧既明經，又善星官，常仰瞻天文，歎息謂魚豢曰：「天下兵戈尚猶未息，如之何？」豢又常從問左氏傳，禧答曰：「欲知幽微莫若易，人倫之紀莫若禮，多識山川草木之名莫若詩，左氏直相斫書耳，不足精意也。」豢因從問詩，禧說齊、韓、魯、毛四家義，不復執文，有如諷誦。又撰作諸經解數十萬言，未及繕寫而得聾，後數歲病亡也。其邯鄲淳事在王粲傳，蘇林事在劉邵、高堂隆傳，樂詳事在杜畿傳。魚豢曰：學之資於人也，其猶藍之染於素乎！故雖仲尼，猶曰「吾非生而知之者」，況凡品哉！且世人所以不貴學者，必見夫有「誦詩三百而不能專對於四方」故也。余以為是則下科耳，不當顧中庸以上，材質適等，而加之以文乎！今此數賢者，略余之所識也。檢其事能，誠不多也。但以守學不輟，乃上為帝王所嘉，下為國家名儒，非由學乎？由是觀之，學其胡可以已哉！

Appraisal

Appraisal states: Zhōng Yáo was open and comprehensive, logical and competent, Huà Xīn was pure and clean, virtuous and plain, Wáng Lǎng in literary and wide learning was richly endowed, truly all the eminent and great of an entire era. At the Wèi dynasty’s beginning reign, the first to ascend to the Three Excellencies, how magnificent! Wáng Sù was bright and direct with many learnings, able to chop firewood!91 [39]

評曰：鍾繇開達理幹，華歆清純德素，王朗文博富贍，誠皆一時之俊偉也。魏氏初祚，肇登三司，盛矣夫！王肅亮直多聞，能析薪哉！〔一〕

[39] Liú Shí believed: [Wáng] Sù was upright in serving superiors but enjoyed subordinates flattering himself, this is the first contradiction. By nature he was addicted to glory and nobility but did not seek improper pandering, this is the second contradiction. He was stingy and begrudging with wealth and property but in managing self was not corrupt, this is the third contradiction.

〔一〕　劉寔以為肅方於事上而好下佞己，此一反也。性嗜榮貴而不求苟合，此二反也。吝惜財物而治身不穢，此三反也。
