10  Biographies of Xún Yù, Xún Yōu, Jiǎ Xǔ

Xún Yù

Xún Yù appellation Wénruò was a Yǐngchuān Yǐngyīn man. Grandfather [Xún] Xhū appellation Jìhé was a Lǎnglíng Magistrate,1 during the time of Hàn (Emperors) Shùn and Huán was famed in the era, had sons of eight people, called as the Eight Dragons. [Xún] Yù’s father [Xún] Gǔn was a Jǐnán Chancellor, father’s younger brother [Xún] Shuǎng was an Excellency of Works. [1]

荀彧字文若，潁川潁陰人也。祖父淑，字季和，朗陵令。當漢順、桓之閒，知名當世。有子八人，號曰八龍。彧父緄，濟南相。叔父爽，司空。〔一〕

[1] Xù Hàn shū states: [Xún] Xhū had high talent, Wáng Chàng and Lǐ Yīng all took him as a teacher. Became Lǎnglíng Marquess’s Chancellor, called as Shénjūn.2

Zhāng Fán’s Hàn jì states: [Xún] Shū was widely learned and had high conduct, with Lǐ Gù and Lǐ Yīng were comrades3 and good friends, selected out Lǐ Zhāo from minor clerks, befriended Huáng Shūdù as a young child, as a Worthy-Good and Upright-Correct was summoned,4 in appraising policies sharply criticized the Liáng clan,5 went out to fill vacancy as Lǎnglíng Marquess’s Chancellor, died in office. Eigh sons: Jiǎn, Gǔn, Jìng, Dào, Shēn, Shuǎng, Sù, Fū. [A] [Xún] Shuǎng appellation Címíng in youth enjoyed study, aged twelve could recite the Chūnqiū and Lúnyǔ, immersed thought in the classics and canon, did not answer recruitments, accumulating for ten and several years. When Dǒng Zhuó controlled the government, again summoned [Xún] Shuǎng, [Xún] Shuǎng wished to flee and leave, the clerks held him urgently. Edict was sent down to the prefecture, at once appointed Píngyuán Chancellor. On the way reaching to Yuànlíng, again pursued and appointed Minister of Staff. Oversaw affairs for three days, certificate appointed him Excellency of Works. [Xún] Shuǎng in rising from plain clothes in ninety-five days yet reached the Three Excellencies. [Xún] Shū’s former residence was at Xīháo borough, the county Magistrate Yuàn Kāng said: “In the past Gāoyáng-shì had talented sons of eight men,” and arranged the borough into Gāoyáng borough. [Xún] Jìng appellation Shūcí, also had utmost virtue, fame almost second to [Xún] Shuǎng, lived in seclusion to the end of his life.

Huángfǔ Mì’s Yìshì zhuàn: Someone asked Xǔ Zǐjiàng: “Of [Xún] Jìng and [Xún] Shuǎng who is worthier?” [Xǔ] Zǐjiàng said: “The two men are both jade. Címíng is outwardly shiny, Shūcí is inwardly glossy.”

[A] Pronounced Fū.

〔一〕　續漢書曰：淑有高才，王暢、李膺皆以為師，為朗陵侯相，號稱神君。張璠漢紀曰：淑博學有高行，與李固、李膺同志友善，拔李昭於小吏，友黃叔度于幼童，以賢良方正徵，對策譏切梁氏，出補朗陵侯相，卒官。八子：儉、緄、靖、燾、詵、爽、肅、旉。音敷。爽字慈明，幼好學，年十二，通春秋、論語，耽思經典，不應徵命，積十數年。董卓秉政，復徵爽，爽欲遁去，吏持之急。詔下郡，即拜平原相。行至苑陵，又追拜光祿勳。視事三日，策拜司空。爽起自布衣，九十五日而至三公。淑舊居西豪里，縣令苑康曰昔高陽氏有才子八人，署其里為高陽里。靖字叔慈，亦有至德，名幾亞爽，隱居終身。皇甫謐逸士傳：或問許子將，靖與爽孰賢？子將曰：「二人皆玉也，慈明外朗，叔慈內潤。」

At the time when [Xún] Yù was young, Nányáng’s Hé Yǒng was amazed with him, stating: “A talent to aide Kings.” [2] Yǒnghàn Inaugural Year [189], nomianted Filial-Incorruptible, appointed Office Supplies Director.6 In the chaos of Dǒng Zhuó, he requested to be sent out to fill vacancies as a clerk. Appointed Kāngfù Magistrate,7 then abandoned office and returned home, spoke with the elders stating: “Yǐngchuān is a land of battle on all four sides, when Heaven’s Under has changes, it always is thoroughfare for soldiers, it is appropriate to urgently leave it, and not stay for a long time.” Of the villagers many cherished their homeland and yet hesitated, it happened that Jì Province Governor the same prefecture’s Hán Fù sent cavalry to welcome to move, none would follow, [Xún] Yù alone led his clansmen to reach Jì Province, but Yuán Shào had already seized [Hán] Fù’s position, treated [Xún] Yù with the courtesies toward a highest guest. [Xún] Yù’s younger brother [Xún] Chén and the same prefecture’s Xīn Píng and Guō Tú all were appointed by [Yuán] Shào. [Xún] Yù judged that [Yuán] Shào in the end could not achieve great affairs, at the time Tàizǔ was Exerting Warfare General, at Dōng prefecture, Chūpíng Second Year [191], [Xún] Yù left [Yuán] Shào to follow Tàizǔ. Tàizǔ was greatly pleased and said: “This is my Zǐfáng.”8 Appointed him Major,9 at the time his years twenty-nine. At the time Dǒng Zhuó’s power terrified Heaven’s Under, Tàizǔ asked [Xún] Yù about this, [Xún] Yù said: “[Dǒng] Zhuó’s violent cruelty is already extreme, he will certainly end in chaos, and cano do nothing.” [Dǒng] Zhuó sent Lǐ Jué and others to set out east of the passes, whever they passed they captured and plundered, reached Yǐngchuān and Chénliú and returned. Of the villagers that had remained many met with killing and plundering. The next year, Tàizǔ was designate Yǎn Province Governor, later became Defending East General, [Xún] Yù always as Major followed. Xīngpíng Inaugural Year [194], Tàizǔ campaigned against Táo Qiān, appointed [Xún] Yù with remaining affairs. It happened that Zhāng Miǎo and Chén Gōng with Yǎn Province rebelled, secretly invited Lǚ Bù. [Lǚ] Bù had arrived, [Zhāng] Miǎo then sent Liú Yì to inform [Xún] Yù stating: “General Lǚ comes to assist Inspector Lord Cáo10 in striking Táo Qiān, it is appropriate to quickly supply them with army provisions.” The multitudes were doubtful and confused. [Xún] Yù knew [Zhāng] Miǎo was making chaos, at once reined in troops to set preparations, galloping to summon Dōng prefecture Administrator Xiàhóu Dūn, but Yǎn Province’s various cities all answered to [Lǚ] Bù. At the time all of Tàizǔ’s army was attacking [Táo] Qiān, the remaining defending troops were few, and of the supervising officers and chief clerks many were communicating and planning with [Zhāng] Miǎo and [Chén] Gōng. [Xiàhóu] Dūn arrived, that night executed those plotting rebellion of several tens of people, the multitudes were thus settled. Yù Province Inspector Guō Gòng commanded multitudes of several tens of thousands to come reaching below the city walls, some said that they were planning together with Lǚ Bù, the multitudes were extremely afraid. [Guō] Gòng requested to meet [Xún] Yù, [Xún] Yù was about to go. [Xiàhóu] Dūn and others said: “You are the defense for the entire province, to go is certainly to be endangered, it cannot be.” [Xún] Yù said: “[Guō] Gòng with [Zhāng] Miǎo and the rest do not have a previous relationship. Now he has come quickly, his plans certainly are not yet settled. Reaching when they are not yet settled to persuade him, even if he cannot be used, he can be made to stand neutral. If we in advance suspect him, he will be angered and then complete his plans.” [Guō] Gòng observed [Xún] Yù was without fearful thoughts, said Juànchéng would not be easy to attack, and therefore led his troops to leave. Also with Chéng Yù planned, sending him to persuade Fàn and Dōng’ē, in the end preserving three cities, to wait for Tàizǔ. Tàizǔ from Xú Province returned and struck [Lǚ] Bù at Púyáng, [Lǚ] Bù fled east. Second Year [195] Summer, Tàizǔ’s army stationed Chéngshì,11 great famine, people ate each other.

彧年少時，南陽何顒異之，曰：「王佐才也。」〔一〕永漢元年，舉孝廉，拜守宮令。董卓之亂，求出補吏。除亢父令，遂棄官歸，謂父老曰：「潁川，四戰之地也，天下有變，常為兵衝，宜亟去之，無久留。」鄉人多懷土猶豫，會冀州牧同郡韓馥遣騎迎立，莫有隨者，彧獨將宗族至冀州。而袁紹已奪馥位，待彧以上賓之禮。彧弟諶及同郡辛評、郭圖，皆為紹所任。彧度紹終不能成大事，時太祖為奮武將軍，在東郡，初平二年，彧去紹從太祖。太祖大悅曰：「吾之子房也。」以為司馬，時年二十九。是時，董卓威陵天下，太祖以問彧，彧曰：「卓暴虐已甚，必以亂終，無能為也。」卓遣李傕等出關東，所過虜略，至潁川、陳留而還。鄉人留者多見殺略。明年，太祖領兗州牧，後為鎮東將軍，彧常以司馬從。興平元年，太祖征陶謙，任彧留事。會張邈、陳宮以兗州反，潛迎呂布。布既至，邈乃使劉翊告彧曰：「呂將軍來助曹使君擊陶謙，宜亟供其軍食。」眾疑惑。彧知邈為亂，即勒兵設備，馳召東郡太守夏侯惇，而兗州諸城皆應布矣。時太祖悉軍攻謙，留守兵少，而督將大吏多與邈、宮通謀。惇至，其夜誅謀叛者數十人，眾乃定。豫州刺史郭貢帥眾數萬來至城下，或言與呂布同謀，眾甚懼。貢求見彧，彧將往。惇等曰：「君，一州鎮也，往必危，不可。」彧曰：「貢與邈等，分非素結也，今來速，計必未定；及其未定說之，縱不為用，可使中立，若先疑之，彼將怒而成計。」貢見彧無懼意，謂鄄城未易攻，遂引兵去。又與程昱計，使說范、東阿，卒全三城，以待太祖。太祖自徐州還擊布濮陽，布東走。二年夏，太祖軍乘氏，大饑，人相食。

[2] Diǎnlüè states: Central Regular Attendant Táng Héng wished to have his daughter be wife to Rǔnán’s Fù Gōngmíng, Gōngmíng would not marry her, transferred to give to [Xún] Yù. His father [Xún] Gǔn admired [Táng] Héng’s influence, and had [Xún] Yù marry her. [Xún] Yù was ridiculed by commentators.

Your Servant Sōngzhī notes: Hàn annals say Táng Héng in Emperor Huán’s Yánxī Seventh Year [164] died, calculating [Xún] Yù at the time was aged only two years, then on the day of [Xún] Yù’s marriage, [Táng] Héng had been dead for a long time. The claim of admiring influence is not correct. Your Servant Sōngzhī also believes: [Xún] Gǔn being one of the Eight Dragons certainly was not wrongfully obtained. He could be pressured into doing something, how could he be said to be admiring influence? In the past Zhèng Hū for opposing Qí attracted ridicule, Jùn Shēng for refusing Huò met with praise, attracting ridicule was for losing assistance, meeting with praise was commending his far-reaching consideration, both were without suddenly arriving harm, and therefore each preserved their wills. As for when the castrate eunuchs controlled affairs, the Four Seas held breath (in fear), Zuǒ Guàn and Táng Héng decided death or life in their mouths. Therefore at the time a proverb said: “Zuǒ turns the heavens, Táng sits alone,” saying their power had no equal. Complying with them then the six relations were secure, disobey and opposing then great disaster suddenly arrived. This truly was a time to suffer disgrace for a time for preservation in order to survive instead of perish. In the past Jiǎng Xǔ had a marriage connection to the Wáng clan with no damage to his pure and high conduct. [Xún] Gǔn in this marriage was to have what harm?

〔一〕　典略曰：中常侍唐衡欲以女妻汝南傅公明，公明不娶，轉以與彧。父緄慕衡勢，為彧娶之。彧為論者所譏。臣松之案：漢紀云唐衡以桓帝延熹七年死，計彧于時年始二歲，則彧婚之日，衡之沒久矣。慕勢之言為不然也。臣松之又以為緄八龍之一，必非苟得者也，將有逼而然，何云慕勢哉？昔鄭忽以違齊致譏，雋生以拒霍見美，致譏在於失援，見美嘉其慮遠，並無交至之害，故得各全其志耳。至於閹豎用事，四海屏氣；左悺、唐衡，殺生在口。故于時諺云「左迴天，唐獨坐」，言威權莫二也。順之則六親以安，忤違則大禍立至；斯誠以存易亡，蒙恥期全之日。昔蔣詡姻于王氏，無損清高之操，緄之此婚，庸何傷乎！

Táo Qiān died, Tàizǔ wished to therefore take Xú Province, return and then settle [Lǚ] Bù. [Xún] Yù said: “In the past Gāozǔ protected Guānzhōng, Guāng-Wǔ occupied Hénèi, both used deep roots and solid foundations to control Heaven’s Under, advancing sufficient to overcome enemies, retreating sufficient to firmly defend, and therefore although there were difficulties and defeats yet in the end they accomplished a great enterprise. General you originally with Yǎn Province headed affairs, pacified the troubles east of the mountains, of the Hundred Surnames none did not devote heart and happily submit. Moreover the Hé and Jǐ is a vital land of Heaven’s Under, now though it is ravaged and damaged, yet it is easy to self defend, it also is your Guānzhōng and Hénèi, General, it cannot be not first settled. Now having defeated Lǐ Fēng and Xuē Lán, if you divide troops east to strike Chén Gōng, [Chén] Gōng certainly will not dare to look back west, use that interval to rein in troops to collect ripe wheat, ration food and store grain, in one move [Lǚ] Bù can be defeated. Defeat [Lǚ] Bù and after to the south join with Yáng Province to together supress Yuán Shù, to overlook the Huái and Sì. If you abandon [Lǚ] Bù and go east, if you leave many troops then you do not have enough for use, if you leave few troops then the people all will defend the cities and unable to gather and scavenge. [Lǚ] Bù will take advantage of the weakness to invade and plunder, the people’s hearts will be increasingly endangered, only Juànchéng, Fàn, Wèi can be secured, the rest will not be your possession, this is to be without Yǎn Province. If Xú Province is not settled, General where can you return home? Moreover although Táo Qiān is dead, Xú Province is not yet easy to destroy. They are chastened by the previous years’ defeat, will be afraid and so join close together, mutually forming exterior and interior. Now the eastern region all is collecting wheat, and will certainly harden ramparts and raze the fields to wait for you General, General in attacking them you will not capture, in plundering them will not obtain, and in not more then ten days, then multitudes of a hundred thousands without even battling will on its own be destitute. [3] Formerly in suppressing Xú Province, your authoritative punishments were truly enacted, [4] the sons and younger brothers remember the humiliation of the fathers and elder brothers, and certainly each man will defend with no heart for surrender, even if you are able to defeat them, yet you cannot possess them. In affairs there must be an abandoning of this to take that. For the large exchanging the small is permissible, for the secure exchanging the endangered is permissible, expediences for a one time situation without worrying of the foundations as not solid is permissible. Now in these three none is advantageous, may you General carefully consider it.” Tàizǔ thus stopped. Greatly collected wheat, again with [Lǚ] Bù battled, divided troops to pacify the various counties. [Lǚ] Bù was defeated and fled, Yǎn Province was therefore pacified.

陶謙死，太祖欲遂取徐州，還乃定布。彧曰：「昔高祖保關中，光武據河內，皆深根固本以制天下，進足以勝敵，退足以堅守，故雖有困敗而終濟大業。將軍本以兗州首事，平山東之難，百姓無不歸心悅服。且河、濟，天下之要地也，今雖殘壞，猶易以自保，是亦將軍之關中、河內也，不可以不先定。今以破李封、薛蘭，若分兵東擊陳宮，宮必不敢西顧，以其閒勒兵收熟麥，約食畜穀，一舉而布可破也。破布，然後南結揚州，共討袁術，以臨淮、泗。若舍布而東，多留兵則不足用，少留兵則民皆保城，不得樵採。布乘虛寇暴，民心益危，唯鄄城、范、衛可全，其餘非己之有，是無兗州也。若徐州不定，將軍當安所歸乎？且陶謙雖死，徐州未易亡也。彼懲往年之敗，將懼而結親，相為表裏。今東方皆以收麥，必堅壁清野以待將軍，將軍攻之不拔，略之無獲，不出十日，則十萬之眾未戰而自困耳。〔一〕前討徐州，威罰實行，〔二〕其子弟念父兄之恥，必人自為守，無降心，就能破之，尚不可有也。夫事固有棄此取彼者，以大易小可也，以安易危可也，權一時之勢，不患本之不固可也。今三者莫利，願將軍熟慮之。」太祖乃止。大收麥，復與布戰，分兵平諸縣。布敗走，兗州遂平。
